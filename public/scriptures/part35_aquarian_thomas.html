The Aquarian Gospel of Yeshua the Christ

Levi H. Dowling · 1908 · The Akashic Record · The Complete Life

✦ ✦ ✦

The Aquarian Gospel of Yeshua the Christ was transcribed by Levi H. Dowling between 1884 and 1907 from what he described as the Akashic Record — the cosmic library of all events and thoughts preserved in the subtle fabric of the universe. Published in 1908, it has never been out of print. It fills in the missing years of the Gospels in extraordinary detail — the childhood in Egypt, the years in India, Persia, Greece, and Egypt again, the public ministry, and the post-resurrection teachings. It records Yeshua's studies with the greatest teachers of every tradition and his recognition by each as the fulfillment of their own highest prophecies. It is the most complete single narrative of the Christ-life ever assembled.

☸ The Aquarian Gospel · Part 1 — The Childhood Egypt · Heliopolis · The Seven Masters · The Initiation in the Temple of the Sun

The Aquarian Gospel records that the infant Yeshua was taken to Egypt and brought to the care of Elihu and Salome — masters of the Brotherhood of the Seven Sages in Heliopolis. There, Mary and Joseph and the child were received and sheltered. Elihu taught Mary and Salome taught Joseph the hidden wisdom of the ages — the understanding of the forces that would shape the life of the child they carried.

From the Aquarian Gospel, Section 7: These are the words of Elihu: The Holy One has sent us many saviours in the past, and each one was a demonstration of the power of love. The saviour of this age must needs be tried; he must be tested by the fire of earthly life; he must prove himself in every way to be a worthy master of the forces of the higher planes, before he takes his seat upon the judgment seat of men. And so the child must learn the lessons of the wisdom schools of every faith. He must sit at the feet of sages in the East and in the South and in the West. He must be approved in every court of learning. He must be tried and sealed by every brotherhood before the benediction comes and Jah proclaims his son before the world.

At age twelve Yeshua entered the Temple of Heliopolis — the City of the Sun — where he was received by the seven masters of the Egyptian mysteries. He sat before each in turn. Each master tested him and found in him a wisdom that surpassed what could have been learned — a wisdom that had been carried from previous lifetimes of spiritual mastery. The seven masters declared him ready to receive the seven initiations — the seven degrees of the Hermetic mysteries corresponding to the seven levels of divine consciousness.

After his initiation in Egypt, Yeshua travelled eastward. The Aquarian Gospel records his years in India in great detail — his arrival in Orissa, his study with the Brahmin priests at Jagannath, his encounter with the lower castes, his teaching that Jah is the Father of all men equally, his conflict with the priests who served the caste system, his flight northward. He spent time in Benares. He spent time in Nepal with the Buddhist community. He sat with the great teacher Barata Arabo who recognised in him the fulfillment of the highest Vedic prophecies.

☸ The Aquarian Gospel · Part 2 — Persia · Greece · The Final Return Kaspar the Magian · Athens · The Academy · The Council of Seven

After his years in India and Tibet, Yeshua travelled westward through Persia, where he was received by the Magian master Kaspar — one of the three Magi who had attended his birth. Kaspar recognised him immediately. They spent many months together, Kaspar transmitting the complete Zoroastrian science of light — the understanding of Ahura Mazda and the cosmic fire — which Yeshua received and integrated with what he had learned in India and Egypt.

From Persia Yeshua went to Greece — to Athens, the city of philosophy. He was received at the Academy and engaged with the leading Stoic and Platonic philosophers of his time. He showed them that the Logos they had theorised about — the divine Word or Reason that structures the cosmos — was not an abstraction but a living reality that a human being could enter into, merge with, and express. He showed them that Plato's allegory of the cave — the souls imprisoned in darkness seeing only shadows of the truth — was a precise description of the ordinary human condition, and that the philosopher's ascent to the sunlight was not a metaphor but a literal description of the experience of Samadhi.

Then Yeshua returned to Egypt for his final initiation. He entered the Great Pyramid — the supreme initiatic chamber of the ancient world. In the King's Chamber, the sarcophagus was used not for burial but for initiation: the candidate lay within it for three days in the deepest state of trance — what the Egyptians called the sleep of Osiris — during which the soul departed the body, travelled the inner planes, was shown the complete cosmic structure of existence, and returned bearing the full imprint of the divine in every cell.

Yeshua emerged from the sarcophagus on the third day. The seven masters of the Brotherhood were waiting. They greeted him with the words: All hail, Master of the Sun, the Moon, and the Cross. All hail, Deliverer of Men. Master Yeshua, you have conquered by love. You have passed through all the seven seals. The highest Brotherhood of Light proclaims you Christ. He was then thirty years old. He returned to Palestine. He went to the Jordan and was baptized by John. The dove descended. The voice spoke. The ministry began.

☸ The Aquarian Gospel · Part 3 — Selected Teachings The Universal Christ · The Oneness of All Religions · The Age of the Spirit

From the Aquarian Gospel, the teachings of Yeshua in India: The universal Jah is one, yet he is more than one; all things are Jah; all things are one. By the sweet breaths of Jah all life is bound in one; so if you touch a fibre of a living thing you send a thrill from the centre to the outer bounds of life. And when you crush beneath your foot the meanest worm that crawls, you shake the throne of Jah and cause the sword of right to tremble.

The universal Jah is wisdom, will and love. All things are wisdom, will and love. The human heart is but a tiny mirror of the heart of Jah. And men are mirrors of each other; what 1 see in you reflects what others see in me. And what we see in one another is the Jah within. To love the good in every man, to call forth what is true, to set aside the dross of life and see the gold — this is the ministry of heaven, this is the service that can lift the world.

Men seek for Jah in many ways; they find what they are seeking, for Jah meets all men in the holy place where they are seeking him. The Buddhist, in his temple kneeling in adoration of the Light, and the Hindu in his mosque, and the Hebrew at his shrine — all are seeking Jah, and finding him. And when all men shall recognise the Jah within, and every man shall love his brother man, then every heart will be a shrine where Jah delights to dwell, and every place on earth will be a Holy Land.

We are all kindred, all children of the one Father-Jah. And when the barriers of race and creed and caste shall be thrown down, and men shall love as brothers, then shall the kingdom of the Christ be manifest, and peace on earth shall reign.

The Acts of Thomas in India

The Apostle in the East · The Hymn of the Pearl · The Twin of Christ

✦ ✦ ✦

The Acts of Thomas is one of the most remarkable texts of early Christianity — a second-century account of the missionary journey of the Apostle Thomas to India. It contains the Hymn of the Pearl — one of the most beautiful Gnostic poems ever written, describing the soul's descent into matter and its return to the divine. Thomas is called in this text the twin of Yeshua — Didymus Judas Thomas — and the Indian tradition confirms: the church of Saint Thomas in Kerala, founded by the Apostle himself in 52 CE, still exists today. Thomas went to India because Yeshua sent him — to the same communities of lost tribe Israelites that Yeshua himself had visited during his own years in the East.

✦ The Acts of Thomas · The Journey · The Twin The Commission · Gondophares King of India · The Church at Kerala

The Acts of Thomas opens with the Apostles dividing the world among themselves for their missionary work. India falls to Thomas by lot. He refuses — he is afraid, he says he is not strong enough, he has never been so far. Then Yeshua appears to him in a vision and says: Fear not, Thomas. Go to India and preach the word, for my grace shall be with thee. But Thomas still refuses. So Yeshua — in what the text treats as a completely ordinary occurrence — appears physically, sells Thomas as a slave to an Indian merchant named Abbanes, and sends him to the court of King Gondophares of northwest India.

At the court of Gondophares, Thomas is commissioned to build a palace. He distributes all the funds to the poor instead, telling the king he is building him a palace in heaven. The king is furious and imprisons him. That night the king's brother dies and in a vision sees the palace Thomas has built for him in the divine worlds — a palace of light, of infinite beauty, constructed from the currency of generous works. The brother returns to life and tells the king what he has seen. Gondophares releases Thomas and both he and his brother convert.

Thomas then travels south through India, preaching, healing, baptising, building communities. He arrives at the court of King Mazdai in south India — in the region that is now Kerala. He converts the queen Tertia and the king's son Vizan. The king, enraged, has him executed on a hill — pierced with spears — and he dies proclaiming the unity of Jah and the love of the Christ.

The tomb of Saint Thomas stands today in Chennai — formerly Madras — at the Basilica of San Thome. The Kerala Christian community — among the oldest continuously existing Christian communities on earth — trace their founding directly to Thomas in 52 CE. They preserved the original Aramaic liturgy for fifteen centuries. They are the living proof that the Gospel reached India within a generation of the Resurrection — carried by the twin of Yeshua to the land where Yeshua himself had studied.

✦ The Hymn of the Pearl The Prince in Egypt · The Pearl of Great Price · The Return to the Father's House

The Hymn of the Pearl is embedded in the Acts of Thomas as a song Thomas sings in prison. It is one of the supreme mystical poems of the ancient world — a Gnostic allegory of the soul's journey that parallels both the parable of the Prodigal Son and the Egyptian myth of Osiris.

A young prince is sent by his Father-King from the East to Egypt to retrieve a pearl from the coils of a serpent. He arrives in Egypt and falls into a deep sleep — forgetting who he is, forgetting his mission, eating the food of the Egyptians and becoming one of them. His parents send him a letter — a letter that flies to him in the form of an eagle, speaks to him in his own language, and awakens him from his sleep. The letter says: Remember that thou art a son of kings. See the slavery — whom thou servest. Remember the pearl for which thou didst journey into Egypt.

He awakens. He remembers. He faces the serpent. He takes the pearl. He makes the journey home. He arrives at his Father's house and is received with joy. His royal robe — which he had left behind at the beginning — is brought to him. When he sees it he recognises himself in it — for his image is sewn into the robe, and the robe is his own image. He puts it on and is complete.

The Pearl is the divine Self — the Atman, the Christ-consciousness — the treasure buried in the heart of every human soul. Egypt is the world of matter and ego-identification. The sleep is the ordinary human condition — the amnesia of the soul that has forgotten its divine origin. The letter is the scripture, the guru, the inner voice — whatever it is that breaks through the sleep and reminds the soul of who it is. The robe is the causal body — the original blueprint of divine perfection — which the soul recovers when it returns home. This is the entire Gospel in a single poem. This is the entire Vedanta in a single image. This is what Yeshua came to teach: 1 am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly. The abundant life is the life of the soul that has remembered who it is.
