3 Meqabyan

The Third Book of Meqabyan · Ethiopian Maccabees · Prophecy of Christ

✦ Chapter 1

1:1Christ shall bring joy to the people of Egypt; for He shall come to them in a later era to take revenge and destroy the Devil, who wronged the kind and innocent, who misled people, and who hates the work of his Creator.

1:2He shall take revenge and destroy him. He shall turn the Devil's lordship into misery and humiliation, for he has been arrogant in his reasoning.

1:3He shall turn his lordship into humiliation; for the Devil said, "1 will enter the midst of the sea, 1 will ascend to Heaven, 1 will look into the depths, and 1 will seize the children of Adam like bird chicks. Who is higher than 1?"

1:4"Because 1 influenced their minds to distance them from the straight Law of the Lord—as 1 will grow stronger over those who live in this world unless they do the Lord’s Will—there is no one who will remove me from my authority," he said.

1:5"For 1 will be the reason they return to a path that is smooth for going to Gehenna (Hell) with me."

1:6"Those who loved Him and kept His Law hate me for this reason; but those who departed from the Law of their Lord and who erred will come to me, love me, and keep my oath. As 1 make their reasoning evil and change their thoughts so they do not return to their Creator, they will do my bidding as 1 commanded them."

1:7"And when 1 show them the money of this world, 1 will mislead their reasoning from the straight Law; and when 1 show them beautiful and attractive daughters, 1 will distance them from the straight Law by these."

1:8"And when 1 show them shining Indian jewels, silver, and gold, 1 will distance them by these also from the straight Law, so they might return to my work."

1:9"And when 1 show them fine clothes, red silk, white silk, and linens, 1 will distance them by these also from the straight Law and return them to my thoughts. When 1 multiply their money and livestock like sand and show it to them, by this also 1 will return them to my work."

1:10"And when 1 show them jealousy born of arrogance over women, and show them anger and quarrels, by all this 1 will return them to my work."

1:11"And when 1 show them signs, 1 will dwell in the minds of their companions; 1 will place a sign for each individual in their own reasoning, showing them signs in words to mislead them."

1:12"And for those in whom 1 dwell, 1 will show them signs—whether it be in the movement of the stars, the passing of clouds, the flickering of fire, or the cries of beasts and birds. As they are my dwellings, 1 will place signs in their reasoning through all these things."

1:13"They will speak and give signs to their companions; and just as those naysayers told them, 1 will go before them and be a sign for them."

1:14"1 will perform these signs for them so that those who examine them might be misled; they will pay wages to magicians and tell their companions, 'There are no experts like so-and-so, for whom things happen exactly as they spoke; they know prophecy, they distinguish good from evil, and all things happen according to their word.'"

1:15"1 will be joyful when they speak this, so that those who perish and err because of me might increase and the children of Adam might perish. For the Lord humbled me from my rank because of their father, Adam, when 1 said, '1 will not bow to Adam, who is lower than me.'"

1:16"And 1 will take to destruction all his children who live firmly under my command. 1 have an oath from the Lord who created me, that all persons whom 1 mislead might descend to Gehenna with me."

1:17"When He increased His anger against me, and when He commanded that 1 be bound and cast into Gehenna—when my Creator commanded this—1 interceded with my Lord. 1 pleaded before Him, saying: 'As You are angry with me, and as You have admonished me by Your punishment and chastised me by Your wrath—Lord, my Lord, grant me a delay that 1 might speak one thing before You.'"

1:18"And my Lord answered me, saying: 'Speak, 1 will hear you.' At that time 1 began my plea to Him, saying: 'Since 1 was humbled from my rank, let the people whom 1 mislead be like me in Gehenna, where 1 will receive tribulation.'"

1:19"'And let those who refused me, who did not err because of me, who did not keep my command, but who did Your Command, fulfilled Your Will, and kept Your Word—let them belong to Your Lordship. Since they did not err as 1 misled them, having refused what 1 taught them—when You loved me, let them now take the crown You once gave to me.'"

1:20"'Give them the crown of the authorities called Satans who were sent with me; seat them upon my throne at Your Right hand, which became a wilderness because of me and my hosts.'"

1:21"'And let them praise You as You desire; let them be like my hosts and like me. Because You hated me and loved those created from ashes and earth—as my authority has perished and theirs has been raised high—let them praise You as You desire.'"

1:22"My Lord answered me, saying: 'As you have misled them while they saw and heard, if you misled them without them loving My Order, let them belong to you according to your will and your word.'"

1:23"'If they leave the Word of the Books and My Command and come to you—and if you mislead them while their destruction also saddens Me—let them receive tribulation in Gehenna just like you,' He told me."

1:24"You shall receive tribulation in Gehenna until eternity; you shall have no exit from Gehenna forever—neither those whom you misled, nor you."

✦ Chapter 2

2:1"But 1 shall bequeath your throne of lordship to those whom you failed to mislead, like My servant Job." The Lord who rules all said: "1 will give the Kingdom of Heaven to those whom you failed to mislead."

2:2"And 1 (the Devil) provoke the children of Adam in all things; if it were possible for me to mislead them, 1 would not let them remain firm in good work. For 1 provoke all the children of Adam, and 1 make the pleasures of this world sweet to them."

2:3"Whether it be through the love of drink, food, and clothes; or through the love of material things; or through withholding and giving..."

2:4"...or through the desire to hear and see; or the desire to touch and go; or through increasing arrogance and possessions; or through the love of dreams and slumber..."

2:5"...or through increasing drunkenness and drinking; or through increasing insults and anger; or through speaking games and useless things..."

2:6"...or through quarrels and backbiting their companions; or through looking at the attractive daughters of this world; or through smelling perfumes and fragrances that mislead them..."

2:7"...1 hate them through all this so that they cannot be saved. 1 distance them from the Law of the Lord so they might enter with me into the destruction by which 1 was humbled from my rank."

2:8And the prophet told him: "You who destroy people, perish! When you departed from the Law of the Lord and committed a crime in your stubbornness and arrogance—by saddening your Creator and not worshipping Him in your pride—will you still be arrogant toward the Lord’s creation?"

2:9"When your Creator was angry with you, He humbled you from your rank because of your evil work. Why do you lead Adam toward sin—he whom his Creator made from the earth, whom He made as a beloved being, and whom He placed for His praise?" he told him.

2:10"While you—who are subtle and were created from wind and fire—were arrogant in saying, '1 am the Creator'..."

2:11"...when you boasted, the Lord saw your evil work. After you and your hosts denied the Lord, He created Adam so that he might praise Him in your place, that he might praise His Name without ceasing."

2:12"Because you made yourself prouder than all the hosts of Angels who are like you, the Lord created Adam and his children so they might praise the Lord’s Name in place of the praise you and your rejected hosts once gave."

2:13"For this reason, the Lord destroyed you, separating you from all the Angelic chiefs like you. Your hosts, who were of one mind with you—you all went forth and erred from the praise of the Lord because of your useless arrogance and stubbornness. You were arrogant toward your Creator, and toward no other."

2:14"For this reason, He created Adam from the earth so He might be praised by humble beings. He gave him a Command and Law, saying: 'Do not eat from the fruit of the fig tree.'"

2:15"And He appointed him over all the creation He made. He notified him, saying: 'Do not eat from the one fig tree that brings death, lest you bring death upon yourself; yet you may eat fruit from all the trees in the midst of the Garden.'"

2:16"When you heard this Word, you placed treachery in his heart by the words you spoke through your tongue to Eve, who was formed from Adam’s rib."

2:17"You misled Adam, who was clean, with firm treachery so that you might make him a law-breaker just like you."

2:18"When you misled Eve—who was created like an innocent dove and did not know your malice—you made her betray through your success and your crooked words. After you misled Eve, who was created first, she also went and misled the Lord’s creation, Adam, who was also created from the earth."

2:19"You caused him to betray in a disturbance that did not come from his own arrogance; you made him deny his Creator's Word, and you destroyed Adam in your pride."

2:20"In your malice, you distanced him from his Creator’s Love; by your reasoning, you sent him away from the Garden where joy is; and by your hindrance, you made him give up the food of the Garden."

2:21"For from antiquity, you have quarreled with the innocent creation, Adam, so that you might lower him toward Sheol where you will receive hardship—sending him away from the Love that brought him forth and created him from non-existence into true life. By your lies, you made him thirst for a drink from the Garden."

2:22"And although he is earthly, the Lord made him like a subtle Angel who praises his Creator in his flesh, his soul, and his reasoning."

2:23"He created many thoughts for him, like the praise of harps in all their different styles."

✦ Chapter 3

3:1"But He created only one thought for you, so that you might give total praise while you were sent where your Creator sent you."

3:2"But to Adam were given five evil thoughts and five good thoughts—ten thoughts in total."

3:3"Furthermore, he has many thoughts like the waves of the sea, or like a whirlwind that scatters dust lifted from the earth, or like the shaking waves of the sea. From the abundance of his unnumbered thoughts in his heart—like unnumbered raindrops—such are the thoughts of Adam."

3:4"But your thought is only one. As you are not fleshly, you have no other thought."

3:5"But you dwelled in the mind of the snake; in evil treachery, you destroyed Adam, who was a single body. Eve heard the words of the snake, and having heard, she did as she was commanded."

3:6"After she ate the fig fruit, she came and misled the Lord’s first creation, Adam. She brought death upon him and upon her children because she broke her Creator’s Command."

3:7"They were sent out from the Garden by the Lord’s true Judgment. He comforted them in the land where they were sent through the children born of their nature and by the crops found in the earth; yet He did not end the quarrel with the Garden."

3:8"When you expelled them directly from the Garden, so that they might plant plants and raise children to be comforted—renewing their reasoning in the fruit of the earth that the earth prepared—and that they might be comforted by the fruit of the earth and the fruit of the Garden which the Lord gave them..."

3:9"...the Lord gave them trees more green than the trees of the Garden. Eve and Adam—whom you sent away from the Garden when they ate from it—were fully comforted from their sadness."

3:10"As the Lord knows how to comfort His creation, their minds were calmed because of their children and the crops found from the earth."

3:11"Although they were sent to this world that grows nettles and thorns, they strengthened their minds through water and grain."

✦ Chapter 4

4:1The Lord has it in His power to ransom Adam, and He shall put you (the Devil) to shame. He will save a sheep from a wolf's mouth—that is, Adam from the Devil.

4:2However, you shall go toward Gehenna, taking with you the people whom you ruled.

4:3Those who kept the Law of their Creator, the Lord, shall be joyful with Him. He hid them from evil works so that He might make them His own treasure, and so they might praise Him with the honored Angels who did not break the Law of their Creator as you did.

4:4But the Lord—who chose you and gave you more than all the Angels like you so that you might praise Him with His servant Angels—withheld a lofty throne from you because of your arrogance.

4:5Instead, you became infamous and were called one who loves false godhood, and your hosts were called demons.

4:6But those who loved the Lord shall be His kindred, like the honored Angels; and the Seraphim and Cherubim who praise Him stretch forth their wings and praise Him without ceasing.

4:7But in your arrogance and laziness, you destroyed your own praise, though you were meant to praise Him always with your host and your kindred who were created in your likeness.

4:8Lest the praise of the Lord—who created you as a tenth tribe—be diminished when you forgot to praise Him; it seemed to you that it was impossible for Him to create another creation like you. And lest His praise be diminished when you were separated from the unity of your brothers, He created Adam in your place.

4:9But in the arrogance of your reasoning, you neglected the praise of the Lord who created you; therefore He was angry with you. He ridiculed you, bound you, and banished you to Gehenna along with your hosts.

4:10He brought soil from the Earth with His glorified Hands; and adding fire, water, and wind, He created Adam in His own Example and Features.

4:11He appointed him over all the creation He made by His authority, so that His praise might be fulfilled by the praise you should have given Him. Adam’s praise became one with the Angels’ praise, and their praise was equal.

4:12But in the stubbornness of your reasoning and your arrogance, you were humbled from your rank; and having departed from the Lordship of the One who created you, you destroyed yourself.

4:13Know that His praise was not diminished; for the Lord created Adam, who praised Him in the counsel of his reasoning, so that the praise of His Divinity would not be lessened.

4:14For He knows all things before they are done. He knew you before He created you—that you would demolish His Command. As there was a hidden counsel with Him before He created the world, when you denied Him, He created His servant Adam in His own Features and Example.

4:15Just as Solomon spoke, saying: "Before the hills were created, and before the world was successfully made, and before the winds that are the foundations of the Earth were created..."

4:16"...and before He established the foundations of the hills and mountains, and before the work of this world was set, and before the light of the moon and Sun shone, or the cycles of the stars were known..."

4:17"...and before daylight and night alternated, and before the sea was bounded by sand—before any of the created beings were made..."

4:18"...and before all that is seen today was seen, or any names called today were named—1, Solomon, was created." Angels like you, and His servant Adam, were already in the Lord's Mind.

4:19He created Adam so that His glorified Name might be praised when you mutinied, and so He might be praised by His humble servant Adam—who was created from Earth—when you became arrogant.

4:20For being in Heaven, the Lord hears the plea of the poor, and He loves the praise of the humble.

4:21He loves to save those in whom He dwells who fear Him; yet as He does not love the power of horses, and as He does not favor the lap of a concubine, the Lord shall ignore the deeds of the arrogant.

4:22And they shall weep while they cry out because of the sins they committed.

4:23You (the Devil) failed to plead in repentance.

4:24But Adam, who was created from Earth, returned in repentance, weeping bitterly before the Lord because of his sin.

4:25But in your stubbornness and your heart’s arrogance, you did not know the work of Love, nor did you know repentance. You failed to plead before your Creator, the Lord, in repentance, mourning, and sadness.

4:26But Adam, who is ashes and earth, returned to repentance in mourning and sadness, and he returned to humility and the work of Love.

4:27But you did not humble your reasoning or yourself before the Lord who created you.

4:28As for Adam, he humbled himself and pleaded regarding the iniquity he committed; he was not proud.

4:29As you have totally produced crime, it originated from you; yet it was not Adam who produced that error. In your arrogance, you took him with you toward your destruction.

4:30Before He created you both, He knew you were sinners; and knowing your works, He knew that what was done came from the arrogance of your heart.

4:31But He restored Adam—who was without arrogance or malice—through repentance, mourning, and sadness.

4:32For a person who does wrong and does not plead in repentance has multiplied his iniquity more than his earlier sins. In the arrogance of your heart, you failed to plead in repentance; but a person who pleads and weeps, entering repentance before his Creator, the Lord...

4:33...he has truly repented. He has found the work by which he will be saved, so that he might fear the Heart of his Lord. He pleaded before his Creator with bowing and much repentance; and because of that earlier tribulation, the Lord shall lighten his sin so He is not angry with His servant, and He will forgive his former sins.

4:34If he does not return to his former sin and continues in this way, this is perfect repentance. Adam did not forget to think of his Creator nor to implore Him in repentance.

4:35And you (the Devil)—plead in repentance toward your Creator, and do not wrong them because they are flesh and blood; for the Lord who created them knows their weakness. Do not wrong the people He created by His authority.

4:36And after their souls are separated from their flesh, their flesh shall be dust until the day the Lord chooses.

✦ Chapter 5

5:1Know the Lord who created you; as He created you in His Features and Example while you are on Earth, do not forget the Lord who established you, saved you, and whom Israel glorified. He placed you in the Garden so you might be joyful and tend the Earth.

5:2When you broke His Command, He sent you away from the Garden toward this world—which He cursed because of you—that grows nettles and thorns.

5:3For you are Earth, and she (the ground) is Earth; you are dust, and she is dust; you are soil, and she is soil. You are fed by the grain found from her, and you will return to her. You will be soil until He chooses to raise you, and He shall examine the sins you committed and all your iniquity.

5:4Know what you will answer Him at that time. Think of the good and evil you did in this world; examine whether the evil or the good would abound—test yourself.

5:5If you do good, it is a good thing for you, so that you might be joyful on the day when the dead arise.

5:6But if you do evil, woe to you! For you will receive hardship according to the work of your hands and the evil of your reasoning. If you do an evil thing to your companion and do not fear the Lord, you will receive your hardship.

5:7And if you betray your companion, and if you call on the Lord’s Name and swear a lie—as you will receive hardship according to your work—woe to you.

5:8You tell lies to your companion, pretending they are the Truth, even though you know you spoke a lie.

5:9You persuade those around you with your falsehoods as if they were the Truth, multiplying things that are not true; you will receive hardship according to your sin. You deny your companion while telling him, "1 will give to you," what you have no intention of giving.

5:10When you say "1 will give" in your pure reasoning, demons make use of you like dogs; they make you forget everything. Whether you withhold or desire to give, they do not know the person for whom they gather wealth. As it has been said: "They shall grow fat"—the money of this world appetizes you so that you might "fatten" the money that will not benefit you and that you will not eat.

5:11And again, as it has been said: "Adam’s lying children make a false balance." As for them, they go from robbery to robbery; the money of this world appetizes you.

5:12O people, do not place your hope in distorted scales and balances, in stealing another’s money, in taking money through transgression, in infringing on your companion’s property, or in stealing his field. Avoid all the lies you do for your own profit at your companion’s expense.

5:13If you do this, you will receive your hardship according to your work.

5:14O people, be fed by the honest work of your hands; do not desire robbery. Do not love to rob and eat another’s money without justice or by what is not due.

5:15Even if you eat it, it will not satisfy you. When you die, you will leave it for another; even if you grow fat on it, it will not benefit you.

5:16And if your money increases, do not let it distort your reasoning. As the money of sinners is like smoke from a griddle that the wind takes away, the small amount of money accumulated in Truth is better than the wealth of sinners.

✦ Chapter 6

6:1Think of the day when you will die—when your soul is separated from your flesh, when you leave your money for another, and when you go on the path you do not know. Think of the tribulation that shall come upon you.

6:2The demons that will receive you are evil; their features are ugly and they are frightening in their appearance. They won't listen to your words, and you won't listen to theirs.

6:3Because you did not do the Will of your Creator, they will not hear you in your plea when you beg them; for this reason, they will completely terrify you.

6:4But those who fulfilled the Lord’s Will have no fear, for the demons fear them. However, demons shall ridicule the souls of sinners.

6:5But the souls of kind people shall be joyful with the Angels in happiness; they shall be made joyful because they scorned this world. But evil angels shall receive the souls of sinners.

6:6Angels of mercy shall receive the souls of kind and righteous people; they are sent from the Lord to comfort the souls of the righteous. Just as evil angels are sent from the Devil to ridicule the souls of sinners, demons shall receive the souls of sinners.

6:7O sinners, woe to you! Weep for yourselves before the day of your death arrives—before the time you reach the Lord.

6:8Enter into repentance in your current era before your time passes, so that you might live in joy and happiness without tribulation or disease. Since your time will not return once you have died, weep now.

6:9Lest you follow a vain path that distances you from the Lord, let love be lavished in your self-criticism, so that gluttony and excessive pleasure are not found in you. As a body that is sated without measure will not think of the Lord’s Name, the Devil's wealth shall dwell in it. Since the Holy Spirit won't dwell in such a person, let not those excessive pleasures be found in you.

6:10As Moses spoke: "Jacob ate and was satisfied; he grew fat, tall, and wide, and the Lord who created him was separated from him."

6:11"And his lifestyle distanced him from the Lord." As a body sated without measure or moderation won't think of the Lord's Name, let not the love of excessive pleasures be found among you. Since a belly full without measure is like a boar or a wandering horse, let not immoderate eating, drinking, or adultery be found in you.

6:12But a person who eats in moderation shall live strengthened by the Lord’s support; he shall be firm like the horizon or like a tower with a stone fence. A person who forgets the Lord’s Law shall flee even when no one is chasing him.

6:13A kind person shall live in respect, like a lion.

6:14But those who do not love the Lord won't keep His Law, and their reasoning is not straight.

6:15The Lord shall bring sadness and alarm upon them while they are in this world—they will be seized with trembling and fright, and with countless tribulations. Their money will be snatched away, and they will be bound by their hands in chains by their masters...

6:16...lest they be among those who rest from tribulation, and lest their lifestyle be one of joy. Lest they find rest during the alarming tribulations that come upon each of them, He shall bring sadness and alarm upon them.

✦ Chapter 7

7:1But as David spoke, saying: "1 have believed in the Lord; 1 will not fear, having said, 'What can a man do to me?'"—there is no fright or alarm for those who believe in the Lord.

7:2And again, as he spoke, saying: "If warriors surround me, 1 have believed in Him; 1 have asked one thing of the Lord, and that 1 seek"—those who believe in Him have no fear upon them. A person who believes in Him shall live in Life forever, and he will not fear the arrival of an evil thing.

7:3Who is the person who was put to shame for believing in the Lord? Or who was ignored by Him for having a desire?

7:4As He has said: "1 love him who loves Me, and 1 shall honor him who glorifies Me. 1 shall keep him who returns to Me in repentance." Who is the person who was shamed for believing in Him?

7:5Judge with Truth and save the widow; save them so that the Lord might save you from all who oppose you in evil. Protect them; for the children of kind people are honored and given success, and He shall save your children after you, for they will not be troubled for lack of grain.

✦ Chapter 8

8:1Job believed in the Lord; because he did not neglect to praise his Creator, the Lord saved him from all the tribulation that the enemy of Adam's children, the Devil, brought upon him. He said, "The Lord gave, the Lord withheld; it happened as the Lord desired for me; let the Name of the Lord be praised by all on Earth and in Heaven." Because he did not let his mind be saddened, the Lord saved him.

8:2When the Lord saw that Job’s heart was cleansed from sin, He received him with great honor.

8:3And He gave him wealth that abounded more than his previous wealth, because he had completely endured his tribulation. He cured him of his wounds because of his endurance through all the trials that came upon him.

8:4And if you, like him, endure the tribulation coming from the demons sent against you, you will be admired.

8:5Endure the tribulation so that the Lord might be a fortress of refuge for you against those who hate you, and that He might be a fortress of refuge for your children's children and those after you. Do not let your minds be saddened by the trials that come upon you; believe in Him, and He shall be your fortress.

8:6Beg Him, and He will hear you. Have hope, and He will forgive you. Beg Him, and He will be a Father to you.

8:7Think of Mordecai and Esther, Judith and Gideon, Deborah and Barak, Jephthah and Samson...

8:8...and other persons like them who were disciplined to believe in the Lord and whose enemies did not defeat them.

8:9For the Lord is True, and He does not show favoritism. But those who love to commit sin against themselves receive hardship. All who fear Him and keep His Law shall preserve their bodies, and He shall give them love and honor.

8:10He shall make them joyful in their going out and their coming in, in their life and their death, and in their rising and sitting. For He saves, and He sets apart.

8:11For He brings sadness, and He brings pardon.

8:12He makes poor, and He honors; He makes wretched, and when He honors, He makes them joyful.

✦ Chapter 9

9:1Whether it be what is in Heaven or what is on Earth, whether it be subtle or stout—everything and all His wealth exists established in His Order.

9:2There is nothing that has departed from the Law and Order of the Lord, who created all the world. Even the track of a vulture that flies in Heaven—He commands it toward the destination He desires.

9:3He commands the path of the earth-snake that lives in a cave toward where He desires, and the path of a boat on the sea. Apart from the Lord alone, there is none who knows its path.

9:4And apart from the Lord alone, there is none who knows the path where a soul goes when it is separated from its flesh, whether it be a righteous or a sinful soul.

9:5Who knows where it will turn—whether it turns in the wilderness or upon a mountain? Or whether it flies like a bird, or is like the dew of Heaven that alights upon a mountain...

9:6...or whether it is like the deep wind, or like lightning that straightens its own path...

9:7...or whether it is like stars that shine in the deep, or like sand on a seashore piled in the deep...

9:8...or whether it is like a foundation stone firmed up on the edge of the deep sea, or like a tree that gives beautiful fruit, growing by a water spout...

9:9...or whether it is like the reed that the heat of the Sun burns, which the wind lifts and takes to another place where it did not grow, and whose trace is not found?

9:10Or whether it is like misty vapor whose trace is not found? Who knows the Work of the Lord? Who are His counselors? With whom did He consult?

9:11Since the Thoughts of the Lord are hidden from people, who can examine and know His Work?

9:12As He created Earth upon Water and firmed her up without stakes, there are none who can know the Lord’s Counsel or His Wisdom. He created Heaven in His perfect Wisdom, firmed it up in the winds, and stretched forth a lofty cosmos like a tent.

9:13He commanded the clouds to rain upon the Earth, grew the grass, and grew fruits without number to be food for people, so that we might believe in the Lord and be joyful in unity.

9:14The Lord is the One who gives the children of Adam joy, fatness, and satisfaction. The Lord gives so that they might be satisfied and praise Him who gave them fruit from the Earth...

9:15...and who dressed them in beautiful robes, giving them all love and plenty—the joy and happiness given to those who fulfill the Lord's Will.

9:16He gives love and honor in the house He prepared and in the Kingdom of Heaven to those fathers who keep the Lord’s Law.

9:17He gives love and honor in the place He prepared and in the Kingdom of Heaven to the fathers who lived firmly in His Worship and His Law, and who did not depart from it. He made them famous and raised them up to keep His Order and Law. 1 have seen what the Lord does for His friends in this world by weakening their enemies and keeping their bodies safe.

9:181 have seen that He gives them all they ask of Him and fulfills their desires. Do not depart from the Lord, but fulfill His Will.

9:19Do not depart from His Command and His Law, lest He be angry with you and destroy you at once—lest He be angry and whip you in the tribulation where you lived formerly. Do not depart from the Order of your fathers, lest your dwelling be in Gehenna, where there is no exit for eternity.

9:20Keep the Law of your Creator when your soul is separated from your flesh, so that He might do a good work for you when you stand before Him.

9:21For the Kingdoms of Earth and Heaven are His; Kingdom and capability are His; and kindness and pardon are only His.

9:22As He makes rich and poor, as He makes wretched and honors—keep the Lord's Law.

9:23And David spoke because of Him, saying: "Man seems vain, and his time passes like a shadow."

9:24He spoke of Him, saying: "But Lord, You live forever, and the invocation of Your Name is for all generations."

9:25And again he said: "Your Kingdom is the Kingdom of all the world, and Your Rulership is for all generations." You returned a kingdom to David, taking it from Saul.

9:26But there is none who can appoint You; there is none who can dismiss You. You see all, yet there is none who can see You.

9:27Your Kingdom won't perish forever, for all generations. There is none who will rule Him, but He rules all. He sees all, but none see Him.

9:28As He created man in His Features and His example so that they might praise Him and know His Worship in straight reasoning without doubt, He examines and knows what the kidneys produce and what a mind thinks.

9:29Yet they bow to stone, to wood, and to silver and gold worked by a person's hand.

9:30They sacrifice sacrifices to them until the smoke of their sacrifice reaches Heaven, so that their sins remain firm before the Lord; yet they refused to worship the Lord who created them. He shall accuse them because of all the sins they committed in worshipping those idols.

9:31They learned to bow to idols and all improper, stained work—fortune-telling by stars, sorcery, idol worship, evil desires, and all the work the Lord does not love. They did not keep the Lord's Command which they had learned.

9:32Since they did not love to worship the Lord—though they could have saved their bodies from sin and iniquity through His servants the Angels and the wealth they praise before the Lord—they do all this while lacking good works.

9:33When they all arise together from the graves where they were buried and where their bodies perished, their souls shall stand empty before the Lord. But the souls of kind persons lived in the Kingdom of Heaven prepared for them.

9:34But the souls of sinners shall live in Gehenna. When the graves are opened, those who are dead shall arise, and souls shall return to the flesh from which they were formerly separated.

9:35Just as they were born in their nakedness from their mother’s belly, they shall stand in their nakedness before the Lord. The sins they committed from their infancy until that time shall be revealed.

9:36They shall receive the punishment for their sins upon their bodies; whether their sins were few or many, they shall receive hardship according to their sins.

✦ Chapter 10

10:1For the blood of the soul given by the Lord shall dwell in them as it did formerly. If you do not believe the dead will arise, observe how creatures arise in the rainy season without being born of a mother or father.

10:2He commanded them formerly by His Word to die.

10:3Their flesh being destroyed and rotten, yet renewed again, they shall arise as He desires.

10:4Again, when the rain falls and satisfies the Earth, they shall arise as they were created formerly.

10:5Just as those who are ever-living with a soul of blood, and those who live in this world, and those whom Water produces have been created—He having said, "Let them be created"—as the Lord’s authority dwells upon the Water, she gives them a soul of blood by His authority and His Word.

10:6Since they are created by His authority and Word without a father or mother—you who are blind in reasoning, who say, "The dead won't arise"—if you have knowledge or Wisdom, how can you say the dead won't arise by the Word of their Creator, the Lord?

10:7As the dead—who were ashes and dust in the grave—shall arise by the Lord’s Word, as for you: enter into repentance and return to your religion.

10:8Just as His Word spoke formerly, they shall arise by the Dew of Pardon found from the Lord. That Word shall turn the whole world and arouse the dead as He desires.

10:9Know that you will arise and stand before Him; do not let it seem to you in your dullness of mind that you will remain in the grave.

10:10It is not so. You will arise and receive hardship according to the measure of the work you did, whether it was good or evil. Do not think you will remain there, for this is the Day when they will receive hardship.

10:11In the time of Resurrection, you will receive hardship for all the sins you committed. You will pay for the sins written from your infancy until that time; you will have no excuse or pretext for your sins, unlike the way you deny your sins in the work of this world.

10:12Just as you make your false word seem like truth before you, and make the lies you spoke seem like truth—you will have no such pretext there.

10:13Because your soul knows all the evil work you did, and because she will reveal it before her Creator, the Lord—as the Lord’s Word shall dwell upon you and speak against you—you have no reason to offer an excuse.

10:14You will be ashamed there because of the sins you committed. You might wish to be among those thanked for their beautiful work; so, lest you be shamed before man and Angels on the day of Judgment, quickly enter into repentance in this world before you arrive there.

10:15Those who praise the Lord with the Angels shall receive their reward from their Creator without shame, and they shall be joyful in the Kingdom of Heaven. However, unless you did good works while you were in the flesh, you have no portion with the righteous.

10:16Since you were not prepared while you had knowledge and while you had this world to enter repentance, there shall be useless regret upon you—especially because you did not give a morsel to the hungry when you had money.

10:17And because you did not clothe the naked when you had clothes, and you did not save the wronged when you had authority.

10:18Because you did not teach the sinner when you had knowledge, so that he might return and repent, and that the Lord might forgive him the sins he formerly did in ignorance. And because you did not fight the demons who quarrel with you when you had the Power to prevail.

10:19And because you did not fast or pray when you had the strength to weaken the power of youth over the flesh, and to subject yourself to Righteousness, which does not show favoritism to the flesh...

10:20...which does not favor the pleasures of this world in fine drink and sweet food, nor adornment in fine clothes, silver, and gold...

10:21...and as you did not fast or pray when you had the strength to subject yourself to Righteousness—which does not adorn itself in honored Indian jewels like emeralds and topazes—there shall be useless regret upon you. This is not the proper ornament for a person.

10:22The true ornament of a person is purity, wisdom, and knowledge—loving one another as is right, without envy, jealousy, doubt, or quarrels. It is loving your companion as yourself...

10:23...and not doing evil to someone who did evil to you. This is true love, so that you might enter the Kingdom of Heaven given to those who endured tribulation. He might then give you the honored Kingdom and your reward for having hope in the Kingdom of Heaven at the time of Resurrection, along with those honored in knowledge and wisdom.

10:24Do not say, "After we die we won't arise," for the Devil cuts off the hope of those who speak and think this, so they won't be saved at the Resurrection. They will realize they have hardship upon them when the Advent arrives. In the Resurrection, those who committed sin in ignorance will be deeply sad when their sins are brought back to them, for they did not believe they would arise on that Day.

10:25For this reason, they shall be reproached according to the measure of the evil work they did in this world, and they shall see the Resurrection they denied, by which they will arise together in the flesh.

10:26They shall weep at that time because they did not do good works. It would have been better for them to weep in this world, if possible, rather than be among those who weep in Gehenna.

10:27If we do not weep in this world by our own choice, demons will make us weep against our will in Gehenna. If we do not enter into repentance in this world, we prepare worthless and useless cries and mourning in Gehenna.

10:28Prepare good works, so that you might cross from death to Life, and go from this passing world toward the Kingdom of Heaven, to see the Light of the Kingdom that surpasses all light in this world.

10:29Refuse the pleasures of this world, so that you might be joyful beyond measure in the Kingdom of Heaven—in joys that are not fulfilled from today until eternity, along with those who believe the dead arise.
