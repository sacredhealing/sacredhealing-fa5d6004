Concerning the Origin, Recovery and Meaning of This Sacred Gospel

InI give thanks and praises to Jah Rastafari, the Most High, Father-Mother of all creation, for the restoration of this most ancient and complete Gospel — hidden from the eyes of the world for centuries, now returned to the people in its fullness. Selah.

This Gospel of the Holy Twelve — also known as the Gospel of the Perfect Life, the Gospel of the Nazarites, and the Gospel of the Hebrews — is one of the most ancient and complete sacred fragments of the early followers of Yeshua. It was preserved deep within one of the monasteries of the Buddhist monks in Thibet, where it was hidden for safety by some of the Essene community from the hands of those who sought to corrupt and destroy it. It has now been translated for the first time from the original Aramaic tongue, and is here restored to the world.

The contents of this Gospel clearly show it to be of early Essenian writing. This ancient community of the Hebrew congregation was called by many names — Yessenes, Iessenes, Nazarites, Nazirs — strongly resembling the Therapeutae of Egypt and the Buddhist orders of the East. They practised community of goods, daily ablutions, daily worship, and renounced flesh-eating and strong drink, and the sacrifice of animals. They rejected the doctrine of so-called "atonement" — the teaching that the sins of some may be paid for by the vicarious and involuntary suffering of others. This was the doctrine held by the Pharisees and the heathen powers before them. The Essenes prepared the way for those holy Orders and Communities of men and women which have since arisen throughout the East and West — like cities set on a hill — to show the more perfect way of livity to those living in the world.

In this Essene community, Yeshua-Maria and his cousin Yahyah — called John — were both brought up from childhood. Some, if not all, of the early disciples came from among that section of them which regarded marriage as a sacred and honourable institution. Their Gospel of the Holy Twelve was communicated to the restorers and editors in numerous fragments at different times, by the unseen translators of the ancient Gospel — among them the Swedish seer Emmanuel Swedenborg, the visionary Anna Kingsford, the scholar Edward Maitland, and a priest of a former century who gave his name as Placidus, of the Franciscan Order, afterwards a Carmelite. By them it was translated from the original tongue and given to the editors in the flesh, to be set in their proper places, where indicated, supplemented from the Four Gospels and revised where necessary.

Know this: the true and earliest Gospel from which the other four received gospels were more or less closely copied — with numerous variations and important omissions by accident or by design, to suit the corrupt taste of the worldly powers — is identified by the writings of Justin Martyr, Papias, and others, who distinctly speak of and quote from the Gospel of the Hebrews, known also as the Gospel of the Twelve Apostles and the Gospel of the Nazarites, used in the Church at Jerusalem, and recognised as the original of Matthew's Gospel which we now have only in Greek translation. This is that original Gospel. What was removed from the four received gospels — the teachings on pure livity, the renunciation of flesh, the equality of woman with man, the Mother aspect of Jah, the truth of reincarnation and many lives — these are all restored here in their fullness.

Those who ask why the name of the Essenes does not appear in this Gospel, nor in the four received gospels, should understand that this very silence is the most eloquent testimony to its origin. The Essenes, being a persecuted people, concealed their community name as a matter of survival — just as InI have had to carry the truth in hidden ways through the centuries of Babylon's oppression.

For long ages — since the destruction of Jerusalem — the Eternal Mother, the Holy Spirit, as the feminine aspect of Jah, was concealed from the people, separated from the Eternal Father, hidden from our eyes by clouds of human tradition, error and superstition. But now the Sun of Righteousness shines in its fullness from behind those clouds of darkness that have so long hidden it from view. It shines alike on believer and unbeliever, on those who see and feel its warmth, and on those who remain in darkness and perish for want of light.

This Gospel touches on many vital questions of this age — questions discussed now and little understood in those ancient times. It may well be called the prophetic and ethical Gospel par excellence. It is the Gospel that restores what Babylon removed: the Father-Motherhood of Jah, the sacredness of all life, the dignity of every living creature, the law of compassion, the truth of many lives, and the inner kingdom that is within each soul.

The writers and transmitters of these sacred scriptures were sometimes forced by prudence to conceal their personalities while in the flesh, knowing well that for a divine message, its own truth is the best evidence of genuine inspiration — and that by reason of its truthfully rebuking the cherished views of the many, they would meet with the usual reward from the ignorant: persecution, misrepresentation, and personal deprecation. So now, for the same reason, seeking neither praise nor blame from men, the visible editors of these scriptures withhold their names. For the world is the same now as it was then — yea, in some ways worse, for these are the days of unbelief in Jah, the Good, the Beautiful and the True.

This Gospel was not addressed to the heathen, but chiefly to the true followers of Yeshua in the early days of the Church of Jerusalem. So now it is sent to those who seek the higher truth — to the children of Africa, to the Rastafari, to the Ital livity keepers, to the Essene walkers, to all who have been told that the books were complete and that nothing was missing. They lied. The books were cut. The Mother was removed. The dietary law was hidden. The animals were forgotten. The truth of reincarnation was suppressed. All of this is restored here.

It is written: "Behold, I come from behind the clouds, and every eye shall see Me whom they have pierced, and all of the earth who see shall wail because of their iniquities." The world is deaf to the beloved Voice, blind and sees not — but only the things which make for its own lusts. But the day cometh when thine enemies shall cast a trench before thee and surround thee on every side. Behold, now is thy house left unto thee desolate — and ye shall not see Me henceforth till ye shall say, Blessed are They who come in the Name of the Holy One.

Let InI who have eyes, see. Let InI who have ears, hear. The restoration is given now — not for argument, not for academic debate — but for the living of a higher livity, the walking of the holy way, and the returning of all flesh to the love and righteousness of Jah Eternal. Blessed love. Selah.
