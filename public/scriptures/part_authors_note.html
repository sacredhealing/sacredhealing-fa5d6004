A NOTE FROM THE HEART

Kritagya Das · Compiler · 2026

✦ To Every Seeker Who Holds This Book

I am looking for the real truth. That is what this book is. Not a doctrine. Not a denomination. Not an agenda. A search — sincere, ongoing, and from the heart.

I want to connect with the real tribes and the real people who can connect with the real truth. I want to find what Babylon removed. What the agenda erased. What was taken out of the Book over centuries to make it look a certain way — to control, to limit, to keep people small and separate and afraid of one another.

It is very important to me that all Rastas, Ethiopians, Christians, and seekers around the world can connect with what is in these pages. You do not have to be a Christian. You do not have to be a Rastafarian. You do not have to belong to any tradition or carry any label. If you are a sincere seeker of truth — this is for you.

That is why in the deeper sections of this book I share deeper secrets and deeper truths — material that goes beyond any single religion, that points to something universal, that blends the wisdom of different traditions so that every honest seeker can find a piece of themselves in these pages. Whether you come from an Ethiopian Orthodox background, a Rastafari reasoning, a Christian church, a Vedic tradition, or from no tradition at all — the truth belongs to you. It was never anyone's property to keep from you.

My sincere apology if I made mistakes during this compilation. I am not a scholar. I am not a bishop. I am not a priest. I am a seeker who could not find the book I was looking for — and so I built it. Every page was written from the heart, with care, and with the honest intention of helping every person who holds it to come a little closer to the light.

I never really connected with the Bible properly before this. Something always felt incomplete. Like pieces had been removed. Like the story had been told by people with an interest in telling it a certain way. This compilation is my attempt to put some of those pieces back — to restore what was taken, to include what was excluded, to give back to every reader what was always theirs.

If I can bring even a single glimpse of truth with this — if one person holds this book and feels something real move inside them — then it was worth every hour that went into building it.

I hope you enjoy this reading. I hope it opens something. I hope it brings you closer to the truth you have been searching for — because that is exactly what it did for me.

With love and with light — Kritagya Das
