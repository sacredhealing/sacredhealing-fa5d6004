A NOTE FROM THE HEART

Kritagya Das · 2026

✦ To Every Seeker Who Holds This Book

I was searching for a real Bible that got everything. Because I know that the agenda — if we say it like this — took away many important scriptures from the Bible. As they took away many years of Jesus, they also took away many important scriptures. So I wanted to bring back the real truth about this holy scripture.

That is why when I did not find a complete Ethiopian Bible — when I did not find the Bible of Haile Selassie I — I found the idea to build it myself. And as I got deeper into the creation, I felt a very strong connection to the Bible which I never had done before. I have read the Bible. In Swedish I read it before. But now it hit me differently.

Actually, I just wanted to have a version myself — with all the truth, with all the books, with all the sacred knowledge — so I can connect deeper to Jah myself. Then the idea came to publish it, to share it with others. Because I know if I can benefit from it, I know others can. Because I saw there were many types of Bibles out there, but there is nothing in the world that got what I had created here.

So I wanted to bring everything into one holy sacred scripture — that seekers, no matter what tradition you belong to, no matter what you believe in, can find deeper truths and deeper meaning within this holy scripture.

As I am not a Christian, I am not a Rastafarian myself. I still wanted to honour all the Christians and all the Rastafarians on all universal levels while I was creating this. I also wanted to blend and make everyone understand that everything is universal. So this can apply to everyone.

So my dear sincere apologies if I did some mistakes during this. I did it from my heart to help every seeker in every tradition, in every religion to find the real truth. This is what it is about for me. If I can bring some glimpses of truth with this, then I am very happy.

I hope you will enjoy this reading. Because for me, this was something I was looking for. I never really got in touch with the Bible properly. I was looking for the real truth — about what the agenda, through the years, removed to make it look a certain way. And I am only interested in finding the real truth about everything I find about the Bibles.

I want to connect real with the real tribes and the real people who can connect with the real truth. And in the end of the book I am also sharing deeper secrets and deeper truths — to make everyone understand that everything is universal. So no matter what religion you belong to, no matter what tradition, you can find yourself in these pages.

So I feel very thankful to have this inspiration to bring this sacred scripture together. With my sincere heart.

— Kritagya Das · 2026
