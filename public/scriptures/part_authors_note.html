A NOTE FROM THE HEART

Kritagya Das · Compiler · 2026

✦ To Every Seeker Who Holds This Book

I was searching for a real Bible that had everything. I know that over the centuries, many important scriptures were taken away from the Bible. Many years of the life of Yeshua were removed. Important books that were always part of the original canon were excluded. I wanted to bring back the real truth about this holy scripture.

When I could not find a complete Ethiopian Bible in English — when I searched and found that no such book existed — the idea came to me to build it myself. And as I went deeper into the creation of it, something unexpected happened. I felt a very strong connection to the Bible that I had never felt before. I had read the Bible before, in Swedish. But now it hit me differently. The words landed in a new place.

I just wanted a version with all the truth — with all the books, with all the sacred knowledge — so I could connect deeper to Jah myself. That was the beginning. And then the idea came to publish it, to share it with others. Because I know that if I can benefit from it, others can too. I looked at everything that existed and I saw that there was nothing in the world that contained what I had created here.

So I wanted to bring everything into one holy sacred scripture — so that seekers, no matter what tradition they belong to, no matter what they believe, can find deeper truths and deeper meaning within this holy scripture. I am not a Christian. I am not a Rastafarian. But I wanted to honour all Christians, all Rastafarians, all Ethiopians, and all seekers on every universal level while creating this.

Please forgive me if I made mistakes. If there is something that is not fully aligned, I did my best to bring out the real truth — so we can connect deeper within our hearts. So we can get a deeper understanding of what the verses are actually meaning. Because when we do, we can connect closer to Jah. Closer to the Most High. Closer to the source of all truth.

I feel very thankful to have had this inspiration — to have been given the strength and the guidance to bring this sacred scripture together. It was not always easy. But every step of the way, something kept pushing me forward. And I believe that something was Jah.

I share this with a sincere heart. Take what resonates. Leave what does not. But know that everything in these pages was placed here with love, with care, and with the honest intention of serving every human being who is looking for the real truth.

— Kritagya Das · 2026
