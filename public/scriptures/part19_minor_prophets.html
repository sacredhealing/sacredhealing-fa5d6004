The Minor Prophets

Hosea · Joel · Amos · Obadiah · Jonah · Micah · Nahum · Habakkuk · Zephaniah · Haggai · Zechariah · Malachi

Twelve voices crying in the wilderness of human history. From Hosea's broken marriage as a picture of Jah's love for unfaithful Israel, to Joel's vision of the Spirit poured out on all flesh, to Amos thundering against injustice, to Jonah swallowed by the great fish, to Micah's call to do justice and love mercy, to Malachi's final word before four hundred years of silence — these are the last voices of the Old Covenant, pointing toward the One who would come.

🕊 Jonah · Chapters I — IV The Great Fish · Nineveh Repents

1:1Now the word of Jah came unto Jonah the son of Amittai, saying,

1:2Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and cry against it; for their wickedness is come up before me.

1:3But Jonah rose up to flee unto Tarshish from the presence of Jah, and went down to Joppa; and he found a ship going to Tarshish: so he paid the fare thereof, and went down into it, to go with them unto Tarshish from the presence of Jah.

1:17Now Jah had prepared a great fish to swallow up Jonah. And Jonah was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights.

2:1Then Jonah prayed unto Jah his Jah out of the fish's belly,

2:2And said, I cried by reason of mine affliction unto Jah, and he heard me; out of the belly of hell cried I, and thou heardest my voice.

2:9But I will sacrifice unto thee with the voice of thanksgiving; I will pay that that I have vowed. Salvation is of Jah.

2:10And Jah spake unto the fish, and it vomited out Jonah upon the dry land.

3:5So the people of Nineveh believed Jah, and proclaimed a fast, and put on sackcloth, from the greatest of them even to the least of them.

3:10And Jah saw their works, that they turned from their evil way; and Jah repented of the evil, that he had said that he would do unto them; and he did it not.

4:2And he prayed unto Jah, and said, I pray thee, O Jah, was not this my saying, when I was yet in my country? Therefore I fled before unto Tarshish: for I knew that thou art a gracious Jah, and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness, and repentest thee of the evil.

🕊 Micah · Chapters I — VII Do Justice · Love Mercy · Walk Humbly With Thy Jah

4:1But in the last days it shall come to pass, that the mountain of the house of Jah shall be established in the top of the mountains, and it shall be exalted above the hills; and people shall flow unto it.

4:2And many nations shall come, and say, Come, and let us go up to the mountain of Jah, and to the house of the Jah of Jacob; and he will teach us of his ways, and we will walk in his paths: for the law shall go forth of Zion, and the word of Jah from Jerusalem.

4:3And he shall judge among many people, and rebuke strong nations afar off; and they shall beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruninghooks: nation shall not lift up a sword against nation, neither shall they learn war any more.

5:2But thou, Bethlehem Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting.

6:8He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth Jah require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy Jah?

7:7Therefore I will look unto Jah; I will wait for the Jah of my salvation: my Jah will hear me.

7:18Who is a Jah like unto thee, that pardoneth iniquity, and passeth by the transgression of the remnant of his heritage? he retaineth not his anger for ever, because he delighteth in mercy.

7:19He will turn again, he will have compassion upon us; he will subdue our iniquities; and thou wilt cast all their sins into the depths of the sea.

7:20Thou wilt perform the truth to Jacob, and the mercy to Abraham, which thou hast sworn unto our fathers from the days of old.

11:1When Israel was a child, then I loved him, and called my son out of Egypt. As they called them, so they went from them: they sacrificed unto Baalim, and burned incense to graven images. I taught Ephraim also to go, taking them by their arms; but they knew not that I healed them.

11:4I drew them with cords of a man, with bands of love: and I was to them as they that take off the yoke on their jaws, and I laid meat unto them.

11:8How shall I give thee up, Ephraim? how shall I deliver thee, Israel? how shall I make thee as Admah? how shall I set thee as Zeboim? mine heart is turned within me, my repentings are kindled together. I will not execute the fierceness of mine anger, I will not return to destroy Ephraim: for I am God, and not man; the Holy One in the midst of thee: and I will not enter into the city.
