Cast All Your Care · Jah Is Love · Holy Holy Holy · Worthy Is the Lamb

The closing books of the New Covenant. Peter calls the believers a royal priesthood, a holy nation. John declares that Jah is love — the most complete theological statement ever written in four words. Jude warns against false teachers. And Revelation — the Apocalypse of Yeshua — unveils the final victory of Jah over all the powers of darkness, the New Jerusalem descending from heaven, and the eternal reign of the Lamb upon his throne. This is where all 88 books of the Ethiopian Bible point. This is the end that is also the beginning.
