♛ Part I — The Divine Origin The Lineage · The Prophecy · The Appointment Before Time

Before Ras Tafari Makonnen was born in the hills of Harar on July 23, 1892, his coming had been written. It was written in the Book of Revelation by John the Apostle on the Island of Patmos. It was written in the Psalms of David three thousand years before his birth. It was written in the Kebra Nagast in the covenant between Solomon and Makeda. It was written in the stars — for the heavens declare the glory of Jah, and the firmament showeth his handiwork.

Revelation 5:2-5: And I saw a strong angel proclaiming with a loud voice, Who is worthy to open the book, and to loose the seals thereof? And no man in heaven, nor in earth, neither under the earth, was able to open the book, neither to look thereon. And I wept much, because no man was found worthy. And one of the elders saith unto me, Weep not: behold, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, hath prevailed to open the book, and to loose the seven seals thereof.

The Lion of the Tribe of Judah. That is his title. Not a metaphor. Not a symbol. A name — given to one man, at one time, in one place. Haile Selassie I bears this title as Emperor of Ethiopia, descended from the tribe of Judah through Solomon and Menelik I. No other king in the modern world has ever held this title. No other man has ever been crowned with these words.

Revelation 19:16: And he hath on his vesture and on his thigh a name written, KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS. These are not the titles of an ordinary emperor. These are the titles of scripture — the titles of the returning Messiah — given to Haile Selassie I at his coronation on November 2, 1930.

Psalm 68:31: Princes shall come out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall soon stretch out her hands unto Jah. Written by David three thousand years ago. Fulfilled in the twentieth century when Ethiopia — under Haile Selassie I — became the seat of the Organisation of African Unity and the spiritual center of the liberation of the African continent.

His lineage traces without interruption through two hundred and twenty-five generations: from Ras Tafari Makonnen, son of Ras Makonnen Woldemikael, son of Haile Melekot, son of Sahle Selassie — back through the royal line of Shewa — back through the Solomonic dynasty — back to Menelik I — back to Solomon the King of Israel and Makeda the Queen of Ethiopia — back to David — back to Judah — back to Jacob — back to Isaac — back to Abraham — the father of all nations.
