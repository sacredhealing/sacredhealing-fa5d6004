♛ The UN Address · Part II · Disarmament Military Disarmament · The Nuclear Age

Military disarmament has become the urgent mandate of our times. I am not saying this because I equate the absence of weapons with peace, or because I believe that ending the nuclear arms race automatically guarantees peace. Today, military disarmament is important due to the immense destructive capacity available to the peoples of the world.

My country supports the Nuclear Test Ban Treaty as a step towards that goal even if it is only a partial step. The true meaning of the treaty is that it admits a tacit recognition between nations — a recognition that acknowledges the overwhelming and inevitable fact that no one will emerge victorious from the total destruction that would be the fate of all in the event of a nuclear war.

We must look first to Almighty Jah, who has raised man above the animals and endowed him with intelligence and reason. We must put our faith in Him, that He will not abandon us or allow us to destroy the human race that He created in His image.

We must become something we have never been, and for which our education and experience and environment have poorly prepared us. We must be bigger than we have ever been: braver, bigger in spirit, and clearer in perspective. We must be members of a new race, overcoming petty prejudice, owing our ultimate allegiance not to nations, but to our fellow men within the human community.
