Ezra · Nehemiah · Esther

The Return · The Rebuilding · The Deliverance

After seventy years of Babylonian captivity, Jah moves the heart of Cyrus king of Persia to release Israel. The exiles return, the walls of Jerusalem are rebuilt, and Queen Esther — a Jewish woman in the Persian court — saves her people from destruction. These are the books of restoration after exile. Every Rastaman in Babylon knows this story.

✦ Ezra · Chapter I Cyrus Releases Israel from Babylon

1Now in the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, that the word of Jah by the mouth of Jeremiah might be fulfilled, Jah stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, that he made a proclamation throughout all his kingdom, and put it also in writing, saying,

2Thus saith Cyrus king of Persia, Jah of heaven hath given me all the kingdoms of the earth; and he hath charged me to build him an house at Jerusalem, which is in Judah.

3Who is there among you of all his people? his Jah be with him, and let him go up to Jerusalem, which is in Judah, and build the house of Jah of Israel.

✦ Nehemiah · Chapter VIII The Reading of the Law — The People Weep

8So they read in the book in the law of Jah distinctly, and gave the sense, and caused them to understand the reading.

9And Nehemiah, which is the Tirshatha, and Ezra the priest the scribe, and the Levites that taught the people, said unto all the people, This day is holy unto Jah your Jah; mourn not, nor weep. For all the people wept, when they heard the words of the law.

10Then he said unto them, Go your way, eat the fat, and drink the sweet, and send portions unto them for whom nothing is prepared: for this day is holy unto our Lord: neither be ye sorry; for the joy of Jah is your strength.

✦ Esther · Chapters IV — V For Such a Time as This

4:14For if thou altogether holdest thy peace at this time, then shall there enlargement and deliverance arise to the Jews from another place; but thou and thy father's house shall be destroyed: and who knoweth whether thou art come to the kingdom for such a time as this?

4:16Go, gather together all the Jews that are present in Shushan, and fast ye for me, and neither eat nor drink three days, night or day: I also and my maidens will fast likewise; and so will I go in unto the king, which is not according to the law: and if I perish, I perish.

8:16The Jews had light, and gladness, and joy, and honour.

8:17And in every province, and in every city, whithersoever the king's commandment and his decree came, the Jews had joy and gladness, a feast and a good day.
