Yeshua in the East

Issa · Isha · The Unknown Years · India · Tibet · Persia · Egypt

✦ ✦ ✦

The four canonical Gospels record the birth of Yeshua, the flight to Egypt, one visit to the Temple at age twelve, and then silence — complete silence — until his baptism by John at approximately age thirty. Eighteen years unaccounted for. Eighteen years hidden from the official record. This section gathers what was suppressed, what was preserved in the monasteries of Tibet and India, what the Sufi masters kept alive, what the Nath yogis of Kashmir transmitted from master to student for two thousand years. Here is the story of Issa — as he is known in the East — the prophet of Israel who walked the roads of India, sat at the feet of the Brahmin masters, studied with the Buddhist monks of the Himalayas, learned the ancient Vedic sciences, and returned to Palestine as the most complete master the world has ever seen. This is not legend. This is the hidden scripture of the East.
