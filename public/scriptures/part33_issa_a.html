Yeshua in the East · Issa Nath

The Lost Years · The Journey East · The Unknown Christ

✦ ✦ ✦

Between the twelfth year of Yeshua — when he sat among the teachers in the Temple of Jerusalem — and his thirtieth year — when he appeared at the Jordan to be baptized by John — eighteen years pass in complete silence in the canonical Gospels. Not a word. Not a deed. Not a location. This silence is the most significant silence in the history of Western religion. Where was he? What was he doing? Who was teaching him — or who was he teaching? The Eastern traditions answer these questions clearly and consistently: he was in India, Tibet, Persia, and Egypt. He was known as Issa — the Arabic and Sanskrit form of Yeshua. He studied with the Brahmin priests of Jagannath, with the Buddhist monks of the Himalayas, with the Nath yogis of Kashmir, with the mystery schools of Egypt. He returned to Palestine not as a carpenter's son from Galilee but as a fully initiated master of every great spiritual tradition of the ancient world. This is his story.
