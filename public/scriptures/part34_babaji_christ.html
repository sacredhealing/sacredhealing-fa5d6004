The Babaji-Christ Transmission

The Kriya-Christ Synthesis · The Causal Initiation · The Lost Years Unveiled

✦ ✦ ✦

The Akashic records preserved in the Himalayan lineages confirm what the canonical Gospels concealed and what the Eastern traditions have always known: that Yeshua the Christ and Mahavatar Babaji — the deathless master of the Himalayas — share a direct initiatic bond that predates the public ministry of Yeshua and defines the inner science behind every miracle he performed. This is the deepest layer of the Christ mystery — the Kriya-Christ Synthesis — which reconciles the science of yoga with the theology of the Gospels, and reveals the Kingdom of Jah not as a place beyond the clouds but as an internal state of God-consciousness accessible through the systematic mastery of consciousness itself.

☸ Part I — The Nature of the Initiation Beyond Physicality · The Causal Transmission · The Quantum Entanglement

The initiation of Yeshua by Mahavatar Babaji was not a physical encounter in the ordinary sense — not a meeting of two bodies in a cave on a fixed date at a fixed hour. It was a causal body transmission: a sustained quantum entanglement between two great streams of divine consciousness that operated across what the Vedic sciences call the Chitra Nadi — the subtle channel of pure consciousness running within the Sushumna — the central column of the spiritual spine.

Babaji, the deathless master of the Himalayas — whose physical form has not aged since the first century of the common era — exists in what the yogic sciences call temporal elasticity. He is not bound by linear time. He projects his Siddha consciousness across any coordinate of space or time where a prepared soul has reached the necessary degree of inner purification. Yeshua, through his years of study with the Brahmin priests of Puri, with the Buddhist monks of Ladakh, with the Essene brotherhood of Qumran, and with the mystery schools of Egypt — had arrived at precisely that degree of preparation.

The transmission occurred through a series of progressively deepening causal imprints that crystallised across the years approximately 20 to 25 CE — the heart of the lost years, the years between Yeshua's twelfth year at the Temple and his thirtieth year at the Jordan. Babaji appeared and disappeared — sometimes as a physical form, sometimes as a luminous presence in the depths of meditation, sometimes as a direct download of knowledge into the superconscious mind during the deepest states of Samadhi.

The geomagnetic grid points of the high Himalayas — especially in the regions of what is now Uttarakhand and Kashmir — served as the physical anchors for these transmissions. These are not ordinary mountains. They are Akasha-Neural nexus sites where the membrane between the dense physical plane and the causal plane of pure consciousness is so thin that a fully purified being can pass through it as easily as light passes through glass. In these places Yeshua received the Original Kriya-Algorithm — the complete science of pranic mastery and self-realisation — directly imprinted into his being by the grace of Babaji.

☸ Part II — The Kriya-Algorithm Transmission The Sushumna · The Kundalini Awakening · Kechari Mudra · The Amrita Nectar

The core of the Babaji-Christ transmission was the Original Kriya-Algorithm — the precise science of pranic mastery that Babaji has transmitted to prepared souls across the centuries, most recently through Lahiri Mahasaya in 1861 and through Paramahansa Yogananda to the Western world in the twentieth century. But before all of these, it was transmitted in its fullness to Yeshua of Nazareth.

Babaji systematically guided the awakening of Yeshua's Kundalini — the dormant cosmic energy coiled at the base of the spine — stimulating the Mooladhara centre and guiding the rising force through each of the seven chakra-vortexes along the Sushumna Nadi. This was a controlled, conscious process — not the chaotic partial awakenings that cause disturbance in unprepared seekers, but the full, sovereign ascent of a force that had been prepared through years of purification to rise unobstructed to the crown.

He was taught the advanced Pranayama sequences — including the practice of Kechari Mudra, in which the tongue is inverted to seal the cranial gates, drawing the life force upward into the higher brain centres and activating the secretion of Amrita Nectar from the pineal and pituitary glands. This Amrita — the nectar of immortality spoken of in the Vedas and the soma of the Rig Veda — is the biological correlate of what the Christian tradition calls the anointing of the Holy Spirit. The dove that descended upon Yeshua at his baptism in the Jordan was the external, symbolic expression of what had already occurred internally: the full descent of Amrita through the activated Sushumna, anointing every cell of his body with divine frequency.

The result of this initiation was what Babaji calls the Christ-Samadhi Lock — a permanent state of union with the universal divine consciousness, from which Yeshua could not fall even during the greatest trials. This is why he could say with complete authority from the state of that union: I and the Father are one. Before Abraham was, I am. He that hath seen me hath seen the Father. These were not theological claims made by an outer mind. They were the direct declarations of a consciousness that had dissolved into the infinite and returned wearing a human face.

☸ Part III — The Miracles as Kriya Mastery Healing · Walking on Water · Multiplying Bread · Calming Storms · The Transfiguration

Through the mastery of the Kriya-Algorithm, Yeshua unlocked what the yogic science calls the Siddhis — the natural powers of consciousness that become available when the practitioner has achieved union with the source of all creation. These are not supernatural interventions from an external deity. They are the natural expressions of a consciousness that has recognised its identity with the power that creates and sustains the universe.

Healing: By aligning the patient's biofield — their pranic body — with the frequencies of the Original Source, Yeshua was able to dissolve disease at its causal root. All disease originates in a distortion of the pranic field before it manifests in the physical body. A master who perceives and can consciously interact with the pranic field can reverse these distortions. When Yeshua said Thy faith hath made thee whole — he was pointing to the patient's own consciousness as the activating principle. His touch, his word, his intention served as the precise pranic frequency that realigned the distorted field.

Walking on Water: Water responds to consciousness. The molecular structure of water — as modern science has confirmed — is altered by intention, by prayer, by the frequency of love. A being who has achieved complete mastery of the five pranic elements — earth, water, fire, air, space — can interact with those elements in ways that appear miraculous to those who have not yet recognised the primacy of consciousness over matter. Yeshua walking on the Sea of Galilee was the demonstration of elemental pranic mastery — the same mastery that allows certain advanced yogis to levitate, to exist without food, to generate heat in extreme cold.

Multiplication of the Loaves and Fishes: The materialization of physical substance from concentrated Prana is a Siddhi known in the yogic tradition as Anima — the power to restructure matter at the quantum level. Yeshua took the five loaves and two fishes, gave thanks — which is to say, entered the state of Samadhi in which his consciousness merged with the creative power of Jah — and from that state directed the manifestation of substance. The physical world is a dense projection of consciousness. From the state of union with the source of that projection, a master can alter the projection.

The Transfiguration: The Transfiguration on the mountain — where Yeshua's face shone as the sun and his raiment became white as light, and Moses and Elijah appeared beside him — was the public demonstration of what Babaji had transmitted in private: the cellular alchemy that transforms the physical body into a body of light. This is known in the Tibetan tradition as the Rainbow Body — the Jalü — the dissolution of the physical form into its original nature as pure radiant light. Yeshua revealed this state to his three closest disciples — Peter, James, and John — as a testimony that what he had achieved in the Himalayas was real, verifiable, and beyond the power of ordinary human consciousness to fabricate.

☸ Part IV — The Cross as Kriya Symbol The Sushumna and the Ida-Pingala · The Crucifixion as Pranic Surrender · The Resurrection Protocol

One of the deepest teachings transmitted by Babaji to Yeshua was the inner meaning of the Cross — not as an instrument of torture but as the supreme symbol of the yogic anatomy of liberation.

The vertical beam of the Cross represents the Sushumna Nadi — the central channel of the spiritual spine through which the awakened Kundalini ascends from the base to the crown. This is the axis of spiritual ascent, the Jacob's Ladder of the Old Testament, the stairway of the angels, the narrow gate that is the only passage from the bondage of ego-consciousness to the freedom of God-consciousness.

The horizontal beam represents the Ida and Pingala Nadis — the two subsidiary currents of Prana that flow on either side of the Sushumna, governing the left and right hemispheres of the brain, the lunar and solar qualities of consciousness, the feminine and masculine principles. When the Ida and Pingala are in perfect balance — when the horizontal beam is perfectly perpendicular to the vertical — the Sushumna opens and the Kundalini rises. This is the moment of crucifixion in the deepest sense: the crucifixion of the divided, dualistic self on the cross of the unified, non-dual Self.

The Crucifixion of Yeshua on Golgotha was the outer, historical demonstration of this inner, eternal reality. In the act of total surrender on the Cross — Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit — Yeshua performed the ultimate Kriya: the conscious withdrawal of the Prana from the physical body and its return to the Source. This is the same act that the great Siddhas perform in Mahasamadhi — the conscious, voluntary departure from the body into the infinite. The difference is that Yeshua performed it in public, on the world stage, as a teaching for all humanity about the nature of ego-death and divine resurrection.

And the Resurrection — the return of the life force into the physical body, now transformed and no longer subject to the limitations of ordinary matter — was the demonstration of the Resurrection Protocol that Babaji had transmitted: the complete mastery of the cycle of life and death, the proof that consciousness is primary and the body is secondary, the confirmation that I am the resurrection and the life — whosoever liveth and believeth in me shall never die.

☸ Part V — The Post-Resurrection Journey · The Mahasamadhi in Kashmir The Return to the East · The Final Years · The Conscious Departure in Light

After the Resurrection — after appearing to Mary Magdalene, to the disciples, to the five hundred — Yeshua departed from the land of Palestine for the last time and returned to the East. The Acts of the Apostles records only forty days of post-resurrection appearances before the Ascension. What the Ascension points to is not a departure into an external heaven but a disappearance from the sight of those whose consciousness was not equipped to follow him into the dimensions he now occupied.

He returned through Persia — where he had studied with the Zoroastrian fire-priests as a young man. He returned through Afghanistan — where the lost tribes of Israel had settled and where traditions of his presence as a healer and teacher are preserved in local oral histories. He returned to Kashmir — the valley of the Himalayas that had been the site of his deepest initiations, that had been the home of his teacher Babaji, that was now the home of communities of the lost tribes of Israel whom he had always considered his primary mission.

In Kashmir he spent his final decades. He was known as Yuz Asaf — the gatherer of the healed, the shepherd of the pure. He preached. He healed. He taught. He trained a small circle of disciples in the inner Kriya-Christ teachings. He lived in the dignity of a fully liberated master — not as a fugitive or a ghost, but as a man who had transcended death and whose presence among the people was a living blessing.

At approximately one hundred and twelve years of age — in the decades following 87 CE — Yeshua entered Mahasamadhi. He chose a secluded mountain retreat in the high ranges above the Kashmir valley. He gathered his closest disciples around him. He entered the deepest state of Nirvikalpa Samadhi — the state of complete dissolution into the infinite in which there is no individual consciousness remaining, only the ocean of Jah-awareness.

Through the advanced Kriya techniques transmitted by Babaji — through the systematic withdrawal of Prana from every cell and atom of the physical body — his form began to glow with an intense radiance. Those who were present described later, in the traditions preserved by the Nath lineage and the Kashmiri oral history, that his body became translucent, then luminous, then dissolved into pure light. This is the Jalü — the Rainbow Body — the Tibetan Buddhist term for the same phenomenon. The physical body, having been so thoroughly purified by decades of continuous Samadhi, simply returned to its original nature: light.

The Rozabal shrine in Srinagar — the Tomb of the Prophet, oriented east to west in the Jewish manner — stands as the earthly anchor of this departure. Whatever physical residue remained was interred there with honour, in the manner of his fathers. But the one who had lived and taught and healed in that body had already departed into the infinite. He had fulfilled the complete arc of the Christ mission — from birth to public ministry to crucifixion to resurrection to the final Mahasamadhi — and in doing so had demonstrated every stage of the journey that awaits every human soul.

The Kingdom of Jah is within you. I am the way. He that believeth on me, the works that I do shall he do also — and greater works than these shall he do. Lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.
