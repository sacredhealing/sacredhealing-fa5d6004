2 Enoch · 3 Enoch

The Slavonic Book of the Secrets of Enoch · The Hebrew Book of Enoch · Metatron

Two further books of Enoch complete the Enochic canon. The Second Book of Enoch — preserved in Old Slavonic manuscripts and known as the Book of the Secrets of Enoch — records Enoch's journey through the ten heavens and his transformation into the highest angel. The Third Book of Enoch — the Hebrew Enoch or Sepher Hekhalot — records the journey of Rabbi Ishmael through the celestial palaces and his encounter with Metatron, the Prince of the Divine Presence, the transformed Enoch himself sitting upon a throne equal to the divine throne. These are among the most extraordinary mystical texts ever written — the foundation of the Jewish Merkabah mysticism that directly influenced the Kabbalistic tradition.

✦ 2 Enoch · Chapters I — X The Two Angels Come · The Ascent Through the Ten Heavens

1:1There was a wise man, a great artificer, and the Lord conceived love for him and received him, that he should behold the uppermost dwellings and be an eye-witness of the wise and great and inconceivable and immutable realm of Jah Almighty, of the very wonderful and glorious and bright and many-eyed station of the Lord's servants, and of the inaccessible throne of the Lord, and of the degrees and manifestations of the incorporeal hosts, and of the ineffable ministration of the multitude of the elements.

1:3And two men appeared to me, exceeding tall, so that I never saw such on earth; their faces were shining like the sun, their eyes too were like a burning light, and from their lips was fire coming forth. Their clothing had the appearance of feathers; their feet were purple, their wings were brighter than gold, their hands whiter than snow.

3:1And the men took me from there, they brought me up on to the second heaven, and showed me darkness, greater than earthly darkness, and there I saw prisoners hanging, watched, awaiting the great and boundless judgment. And these angels were dark-looking, more than earthly darkness, and incessantly making weeping through all hours.

5:1And those men took me, and led me up on to the third heaven, and placed me there; and I looked downwards, and saw the produce of these places, such as has never been known for goodness. And I saw all the sweet-flowering trees and beheld their fruits, which were sweet-smelling, and all the foods borne by them bubbling with fragrant exhalation. And in the midst of the trees was the tree of life, in that place whereon the Lord rests, when he goes up into paradise.

8:1And those two men led me to the East, and placed me at Eden's gate, and I saw a wondrous place and all manner of trees in full flower: their fruits ripe and fragrant, with every food in yield and giving off fragrance, and in the midst a fountain of water, gushing forth, and a river flowing thence and watering all paradise, and then separating into four streams: and I wept with a great weeping.

10:1Those men brought me to the sixth heaven, and there I saw seven bands of angels, very bright and very glorious, and their faces shining more than the sun's shining, glistening and there is no difference in their faces or behaviour or manner of dress; and these make the orders, and learn the goings of the stars, and the alteration of the moon, or revolution of the sun, and the good government of the world.

✦ 2 Enoch · Chapters XXI — XXXVI Before the Face of Jah · The Seventh Heaven · The Divine Instruction

21:1And I looked and saw all the armies standing before the Lord, and all the incorporeal forces, and the multi-eyed Cherubim, and the many-winged Seraphim, as they stand around the throne of the Lord. And ten thousand times ten thousand, and an innumerable multitude of angels surround the throne and cover the faces of the throne with their wings.

22:1And the Lord summoned one of his glorious ones, whose appearance was like unto snow, and his hands like ice, having the appearance of great coldness; and he iced my face, because I could not endure the terror of the Lord, just as it is not possible to endure a stove's fire and the sun's heat, and the frost of the air. And the Lord said to those men who had brought me: Let Enoch stand before my face for ever. And the glorious ones bowed down to the Lord, and said: Let Enoch go according to thy word.

24:3And I conceive the thought of placing a foundation, and of creating visible creation, and the Lord commanded it to be so: for thus even thou dost see my glory, how I made visible things from invisible. Hear, Enoch, and take in these my words, for not to my angels have I told my secret, and I have not told them their rise, nor my endless realm, nor have they understood my creating, which I tell thee today.

33:4And I appointed the eighth day also, that the eighth day should be the first-created after my work, and that the first seven revolve in the form of the seventh thousand, and that at the beginning of the eighth thousand there should be a time of not-counting, endless, with neither years nor months nor weeks nor days nor hours.

66:6And now, my children, lay thought on your hearts, and mark well the words of your father, which are all come to you from the Lord's lips. Take these books of your father's handwriting and read them. For the books are many, and in them you will learn all the Lord's works, all that has been from the beginning of creation, and will be till the end of time.

✦ 3 Enoch — The Hebrew Book of Enoch Rabbi Ishmael · Metatron · The Prince of the Divine Presence

1:1When I ascended on high to behold the vision of the Merkabah and had entered the six halls, one within the other, as soon as I reached the door of the seventh hall I stood still in prayer before the Holy One, blessed be He, and, lifting up my eyes on high — that is, to the divine Majesty — I said:

1:2Lord of the Universe, I pray thee that the merit of Aaron son of Amram, the lover of peace and pursuer of peace, who received the crown of priesthood from Thy glory on the mount of Sinai, be vouchsafed unto me, so that Qaspiel, the prince, and the angels with him may not get power over me nor throw me down from the heavens.

3:1At once the Holy One, blessed be He, sent to me Metatron, his Servant, the Angel, the Prince of the Presence, and he spread his wings and came to meet me with great joy, to save me from their hands. And he took me with his hand before the eyes of all the host of high angels and said to me: Come in peace into the presence of the High and Exalted King and behold the picture of the Merkabah.

4:1I asked Metatron, the Angel, the Prince of the Presence: What is thy name? He answered: I have seventy names, corresponding to the seventy nations of the world, and my King's name is over and above all names. However, my colleagues, the angels and the members of my household, call me Metatron, the Angel, the Prince of the Presence, because my name is like the name of my King.

6:1Metatron, the Angel, the Prince of the Presence, said to me: Before he appointed me to serve the Throne of Glory, the Holy One, blessed be He, opened to me three hundred thousand gates of understanding, three hundred thousand gates of subtlety, three hundred thousand gates of life, and three hundred thousand gates of grace and lovingkindness, and three hundred thousand gates of love, and three hundred thousand gates of Torah, and three hundred thousand gates of meekness, and three hundred thousand gates of sustenance, and three hundred thousand gates of mercy.

12:1After all these things the Holy One, blessed be He, put his hand upon me and blessed me with 1,365,000 blessings, and I was raised and enlarged to the size of the length and width of the world. And he caused 72 wings to grow on me — 36 on one side and 36 on the other — and each single wing covered the entire world. And he fixed in me 365 eyes, corresponding to the days of the solar year, and each eye was as the great light.

48:1After all this, Metatron showed me all the treasuries of the upper world that are connected with the Throne of Glory. He showed me the treasury of peace; the treasury of blessing; the treasury of life; the treasury of grace and lovingkindness; the treasury of Torah; the treasury of understanding; the treasury of wisdom; the treasury of all the mysteries; the treasury of all that is hidden; and the treasury of faith. And I beheld with mine own eyes all these treasuries and they are all before the Throne of Glory.

✦ 2 Enoch · Chapters XI — XX The Seventh Heaven · The Angelic Orders · Before the Face of the Most High

11:1And those men took me thence, and led me up on to the seventh heaven, and I saw there a very great light, and fiery troops of great archangels, incorporeal forces, and dominions, orders and governments, cherubim and seraphim, thrones and many-eyed ones, nine regiments, the Ioanit stations of light, and I became afraid, and began to tremble with great terror.

12:1And those men took me thence, and led me up on to the eighth heaven, which is called in the Hebrew tongue Muzaloth, changer of the seasons, of drought, and of wet, and of the twelve signs of the zodiac, which are above the seventh heaven. And I saw there twelve great gates open correspondingly to each wind, the atmospheric heavens, which are very beautiful and marvellous.

13:1And those men took me thence, and led me up on to the ninth heaven, and the name of that place is called in Hebrew Kuchavim, that is the heavenly home of the twelve constellations of the circle of the firmament.

14:1And those men took me thence, and led me up on to the tenth heaven, and I saw the face of Jah, like iron made to glow in fire, and brought out, emitting sparks, and it burns. Thus I saw the Lord's face, but the Lord's face is ineffable, marvellous and very awful, and very, very terrible. And who am I to tell of the Lord's unspeakable being, and of his very wonderful face? And I cannot tell the quantity of his many instructions, and various voices, the Lord's throne is very great and not made with hands, nor the quantity of those standing round him, troops of cherubim and seraphim, nor their incessant singing, nor his immutable beauty, and who shall tell of the ineffable greatness of his glory?

19:1The angels who go and come, and shine and revere Jah — there are seven troops of them. And their seven chiefs who light up and revere the face of Jah are archangels. These are the archangels who are appointed over many troops; and Michael is one of the archangels, the chief captain of the host of Jah, and Gabriel, and Uriel and Raphael, and Raguel, and Saraqael, and Remiel.

20:1And I fell prone and bowed down to Jah, and Jah spoke to me with his lips, and said to me: Have courage, Enoch, do not fear: arise and stand before my face into eternity. And Michael, the archistratig, the chief of the heavenly armies, lifted me up and led me to before the face of Jah. And Jah said to his servants tempting them: Let Enoch stand before my face into eternity. And the glorious ones bowed down to Jah, and said: Let Enoch go according to thy word.

✦ 2 Enoch · Chapters XXXVII — LXVIII Enoch Returns to Earth · He Instructs His Sons · The Final Departure

39:1And Jah called one of his elder angels, terrible and menacing, and placed him by me; and his appearance was as white as snow, and his hands like ice, having the appearance of great cold, and he iced my face, because I could not endure the terror of Jah just as it is not possible to endure a stove's fire and the sun's heat, and the frost of the air. And Jah said: Enoch, when the people see thee, they will be afraid because of thee.

40:1And Jah summoned one of the archangels by name Pravuil, whose knowledge was quicker in wisdom than the other archangels, who wrote all the deeds of Jah; and Jah said to Pravuil: Bring out the books from my store-houses, and a reed of quick-writing, and give it to Enoch, and deliver to him the choice and comforting books out of thy hand. And Pravuil hasted, and brought to me the books out of the store-house, dipped in myrrh; and he gave me the reed of quick-writing from his hand.

40:12And he was telling me all the works of heaven, earth, sea, and all the elements, their passages and goings, and the thunderings of the thunders, the sun and moon, the goings and changes of the stars, the seasons, years, days, and hours, the risings of the wind, the numbers of the angels, and the formation of their songs, and all human things, the tongue of every human song and life, the commandments, instructions, and sweet-voiced singings, and all things that it is fitting to learn.

44:1Jah thus instructed Enoch: Hear, Enoch, and take in these words of mine, for not to my angels have I told my secret, and I have not told them their rise, nor my endless realm, nor have they understood my creating, which I tell thee today. For before all things were visible, I alone used to go about in the invisible things, like the sun from east to west, and from west to east. But even the sun has peace in itself, while I found no peace, because I was creating all things, and I conceived the thought of placing a foundation.

50:1And now, my children, keep your hearts from every injustice, which Jah hates. Just as a man asks something for his own soul from Jah, so let him do to every living soul, because I know all things, how in the great time to come there is much inheritance prepared for men, good for the good, and bad for the bad, without number many. Blessed are those who enter the good houses, for in the bad there is no peace nor return from them. Hear, my children, small and great!

52:1When Enoch had spoken these words to his sons, all the people far and near heard how Jah was calling Enoch. And they took counsel together: Let us go and kiss Enoch, and two thousand men came together and came to the place Achuzan where Enoch was, and his sons. And the elders of the people and the whole assembly came and bowed down and began to kiss Enoch and said to him: Our father Enoch, be thou blessed of Jah, the eternal ruler, and now bless thy sons and all the people, that we may be glorified today before thy face.

55:2And Enoch said to his sons: No one should ask of Jah a release from pain. The Lord is righteous, his judgment is just, and before his face — no respect of persons. Jah is not afraid of anyone, however great, for he himself made the great and small, and he provides for all, and there is no one who can escape from his judgment. The Lord himself is judge of all. He himself made the great, and the small, and he provides for all.

67:1And the Lord sent out darkness onto the earth, and there was darkness, and it covered those men standing with Enoch, and they took Enoch up on to the highest heaven, where Jah was; and he received him and placed him before his face, and the darkness went off from the earth, and light came again. And the people saw and understood not how Enoch had been taken, and glorified Jah, and found a roll in which was traced the invisible God; and all went to their homes.

✦ 3 Enoch · The Merkabah Vision · The Divine Chariot The Curtain Before the Throne · The Cosmic Geography · The Fate of Souls

17:1Metatron said to Rabbi Ishmael: Come and I will show thee the Curtain of the Omnipresent One, which is spread before the Holy One, blessed be He, and on which are printed all the generations of the world and all their deeds, whether done or yet to be done, till the last generation. And I went with him and he showed me with his fingers, like a father who teaches his son the letters of the Torah. And I saw each generation and its kings, each generation and its princes, each generation and its leaders, each generation and its shepherds.

18:1After this Metatron brought me to see the store-chambers of snow and the store-chambers of hail, and the store-chambers of darkness, and the store-chambers of lightnings and of thunderings, and the store-chambers of storms, and the wheel of the chariot. And I saw the angels of fire and the angels of ice standing before the Holy One, blessed be He, and not harming one another; and the Holy One, blessed be He, made peace between them and between all his creatures.

22:1After this Metatron, the Prince of the Presence, showed me the Right Hand of the Omnipresent One, behind the Holy One, blessed be He, a hand of enormous size — the whole world in its entirety from one end to the other being but the span of its little finger. And five rivers of fire issue from each finger, and between each finger and the next is the distance of the heavens from the earth, and from the east to the west, and every angel that passes before it falls on its face.

26:1And after this Metatron showed me some of the troops of the ministering angels who had been assigned to him from the beginning, as it is written: All the peoples of the nations are as nothing beside him, they are counted to him less than nothing and vanity. The first troop — their stature is as the height of the seven heavens. Their appearance is as the sun, and their eyes are like torches of fire. And when they open their mouths to sing before the Holy One, their voice is like the voice of the Almighty.

33:1After this Metatron showed me all the princes of kingdoms standing before the Holy One, blessed be He, in the four quarters of Araboth Raqia. And the Prince of the Face of Jah, whose name is Adoriron, stands before the Holy One blessed be He, surrounded by the crowns of his ministers, and the appearance of his face is like the face of ministering angels.

40:1Metatron said to Rabbi Ishmael: Come and I will show thee where the souls of the righteous are, and where are the souls of those of middle character, and where the souls of the wicked are, and where are the souls of the penitents. And he showed me the souls of the righteous, who are treasured up under the Throne of Glory, and they shine as the brightness of the firmament. And then he showed me the souls of those of middle character, who fly about in the winds beneath the second firmament.

41:1After this Metatron showed me the spirits of the fathers of the world, the spirits of the patriarchs — Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, and the rest of the righteous — standing before the Shekinah and rejoicing because of the children of Israel who are observing the Torah in the world below. And whenever they observe the Torah and good deeds, the souls of the fathers of the world rejoice and exult, and the Shekinah sends light upon them from on high.

45:1After this, the Holy One, blessed be He, opened to me the gates of Shekhinah, the gates of peace, the gates of wisdom, the gates of strength, the gates of power, the gates of speech, the gates of song, the gates of holiness, the gates of chant; and I magnified and praised the Holy One, blessed be He, with the song of heaven, with the singing of the Seraphim. And at the hour when I opened my mouth and sang the praises before the Throne of Glory, the holy creatures below the Throne and above the Throne and all the orders of the firmament trembled before me.

48:7Metatron, Prince of the Presence, the glory of highest heaven, said: This is the mystery of mysteries. The Holy One, blessed be He, has revealed it to Metatron, and he revealed it to Rabbi Ishmael. Know that in the time when the Holy One, blessed be He, will renew his world and create a new heaven and a new earth, all the secrets of the Torah shall be revealed, and the secrets of the Throne of Glory and the Merkabah shall be made manifest, and the righteous shall be crowned with the crowns of the world to come, and shall sit on thrones of fire and shall shine as the sun and the moon and the stars, world without end.

✦ 2 Enoch · Chapters XXIV — XXVII · The Creation Account Jah Speaks Creation · Before the Beginning · The Eight Days

24:1And the Lord summoned me, and said to me: Enoch, sit down on my left with Gabriel. And I bowed down to the Lord, and the Lord spoke to me: Enoch, beloved, all thou seest, all things that are standing finished I tell to thee even before the very beginning, all that I created from non-being, and visible things from invisible.

24:2For before all things were visible, I alone used to go about in the invisible things, like the sun from east to west and from west to east. But even the sun has peace in itself. I however found no peace, because I was creating all things, and I conceived the thought of placing foundations and of creating visible creation.

25:1I commanded in the very lowest parts, that visible things should come down from invisible, and Adoil came down very great, and I beheld him, and lo! He had a belly of great light. And I said to him: Become undone, Adoil, and let the visible come out of thee. And he came undone, and a great age came out. And when I had seen the light I said: Let there be light. And there was light.

26:1And I called the light, Day, and the darkness, Night. And in the seventh day I rested from all my works. The seventh day became therefore the image of rest. And after that I appointed the eighth day also, that the eighth day should be the first-created after my work, and that the first seven revolve in the form of the seventh thousand, and that at the beginning of the eighth thousand there should be a time of not-counting, endless, with neither years nor months nor weeks nor days nor hours.

✦ 2 Enoch · Chapters LXXI — LXXIII · The Birth of Melchisedek Born Without Father or Mother · Taken Before the Flood · Priest Forever

71:1And Nir went in to his wife Sopanim, and she had conceived from the beginning, but Nir did not sleep with his wife from the day that the Lord had called him. But Sopanim was ashamed, and she hid herself and no one of the people knew about it. And when the time of her giving birth had come, and Nir had remembered about his wife Sopanim, he called to her and said to her: I have not slept with thee, so who has conceived in thy womb?

71:5And behold a child came out from the dead Sopanim. And he sat on the bed at her left side. And the Archangel Michael appeared there and Gabriel appeared with him, and they were glorious beyond telling. And the child was glorious as if the sun were shining on his face, and he was not like a human child. The child spoke with his lips and he blessed the Lord.

71:11And Nir and Noe lifted up the child and dressed him in the garments of priesthood, and Noe said to Nir his brother: Behold, Jah has renewed the priesthood from thee and from thy seed, forever. For Jah has chosen this child from among all who exist on the earth before the flood. And the Lord said to his servants: Go and take Melchisedek the child and bring him to paradise, and I will preserve him there before the flood.

72:1And the Lord said to Michael: Go down to Noe, to Nir, and take the child Melchisedek, and place him in the paradise of Eden for preservation, for the time is already close. And the flood will come over all the earth, and I will blot out everything standing on the earth. But I will raise up another world, and Melchisedek will be the chief of all priests in that world, and I will make him the head of priests of the generations of the race.
