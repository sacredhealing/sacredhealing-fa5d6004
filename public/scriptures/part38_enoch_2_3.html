The Enochic Trilogy

Three Books · One Tradition · The Watchers · The Seven Heavens · The Throne of Glory

The three Books of Enoch form one of the most extraordinary bodies of sacred literature ever preserved. The First Book of Enoch — the Ethiopic Enoch — is canonical scripture in the Ethiopian Orthodox Bible and was found in its entirety among the Dead Sea Scrolls. The Second Book of Enoch — the Slavonic Enoch or Book of the Secrets of Enoch — survives in Old Slavonic manuscripts and records Enoch's ascent through the ten heavens to stand before the face of Jah. The Third Book of Enoch — the Hebrew Enoch or Sepher Hekhalot — is preserved as a Sacred Addition, completing the Enochic canon: it records Rabbi Ishmael's celestial journey and his encounter with Metatron, the transformed Enoch himself, Prince of the Divine Presence. Together these three books reveal the complete cosmology of the ancient world — the nature of heaven, the orders of angels, the judgment of the Watchers, and the direct encounter with the throne of the Most High.
