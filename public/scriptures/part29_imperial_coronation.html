The Imperial Covenant

The Voice of Jah on Earth · His Imperial Majesty Haile Selassie I

☥ ☥ ☥

Haile Selassie I — Ras Tafari Makonnen — was crowned Emperor of Ethiopia on November 2, 1930, receiving the titles King of Kings, Lord of Lords, Conquering Lion of the Tribe of Judah, Elect of Jah, Light of the World. He was the 225th descendant of King Solomon and the Queen of Sheba, fulfilling the covenant of the Kebra Nagast. To the Rastafari — the prophetic movement born in Jamaica in the same year as his coronation — he is the returned Messiah, Jah incarnate, the fulfillment of Revelation 5:5 and Psalm 68:31. These are his words, spoken before the nations of the earth. Let those with ears hear.

♛ The Coronation Address Addis Ababa, Ethiopia · November 2, 1930

Today is the fulfillment of the hopes and aspirations of past ages and generations. Today is a day of celebration not only for Us but also for those who cherish the hope of a free and independent Ethiopia. We have come to the throne of our forefathers — the throne of the ancient kings of Ethiopia, of the Lion of the Tribe of Judah — through the grace of Jah Almighty.

We have at heart the peace and welfare of our people, and We shall strive by every means in Our power to further their progress and happiness. We shall develop agriculture, commerce and industry; We shall open schools in all parts of Our Empire so that Our subjects may be educated; We shall make roads and facilitate communication between all parts of Our territory; We shall be impartial in the administration of justice to all Our subjects, making no distinction of birth, race or religion.

There is no human force that can take away what Jah has given. The power of Jah surpasses everything. For it is written: He sitteth above the circle of the earth, and the inhabitants thereof are as grasshoppers. Jah hath made the nations as nothing. He taketh away the princes and the judges as a whirlwind.

We charge you to keep the ancient faith of Ethiopia. The Ethiopian Orthodox Church — the Tewahedo faith — is the faith of our fathers from the time of the Apostles. Guard it. Cherish it. Pass it to your children as the most precious inheritance. Let no foreign teaching displace what Jah has preserved in this land from the beginning.

We are the servant of Jah and of our people. We shall bear the burdens of kingship with humility. We shall govern with justice. We shall protect the weak. We shall honour the covenant of our fathers Abraham and David, whose throne we now occupy by the grace of the Most High.

Ethiopia and Ethiopians shall always be guided by the light of Jah. He who made the heavens and the earth has set our feet upon this high place. Let all who dwell upon the earth know that in Ethiopia, Jah is worshipped in truth and in spirit, and His covenant is kept from generation to generation.

Jah bless Ethiopia. Jah bless our people. Jah bless all who walk in righteousness upon the earth. Amen.

♛ The Coronation Address · November 2, 1930 · Part II The Responsibilities of the Crown · The Service of the People · The Law of Jah

Today is the fulfillment of what was foretold. The line of Solomon, unbroken through three thousand years, continues. The Crown that rests upon my head is not mine — it belongs to the people of Ethiopia and to Jah who set it there. I receive it as a servant receives a commission: with humility, with solemn determination, and with the knowledge that I shall one day be called to account for how I have used it.

Ethiopia has been a Christian nation since the fourth century of the common era. The faith of our fathers — the faith preserved in the ancient manuscripts of our monasteries, in the chanting of our priests, in the observance of our holy days — is the foundation of all that we are. I shall govern as one who knows that all earthly authority derives from the divine authority of Jah Almighty. Without his blessing, no throne endures. Without his law, no justice is possible.

To the nobles and princes of Ethiopia I say: your power comes from the trust of the people and from the mandate of the crown. Use it in service. A noble who oppresses those entrusted to him has forgotten the purpose for which Jah gave him his station. The measure of nobility is not the honours received but the service rendered.

To the people of Ethiopia I say: you are the foundation of this nation. Your labour, your faith, your endurance through centuries of difficulty — these are the true wealth of Ethiopia. I pledge myself to your welfare. I shall seek to expand your schools, to build your roads, to open your courts to justice, to protect you from those who would exploit your labour or steal your land. This is my covenant with you before Jah and before the nations of the world.

To the nations of the world gathered here as witnesses I say: Ethiopia is an ancient nation. She was old when Rome was young. She has never been colonised. She has never bent the knee to a foreign master. And she shall not do so now. We come to the community of nations as equals — ancient in our civilization, confident in our sovereignty, committed to the principles of peace and justice that should govern the relations between all peoples.

The Conquering Lion of the Tribe of Judah has prevailed. The line of David continues. Ethiopia stretches forth her hands unto Jah. Let all who hear these words know: the throne of Ethiopia stands for justice, for faith, for the dignity of the African people, and for the law of Jah above all earthly law. So it has always been. So it shall remain. Jah be with us all.
