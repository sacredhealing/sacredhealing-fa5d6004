Proverbs · Ecclesiastes · Song of Solomon

The Wisdom of Solomon · Vanity of Vanities · The Sacred Song

Three masterworks of sacred wisdom from the house of Solomon. Proverbs gives practical righteousness for daily life. Ecclesiastes cuts through all illusion — vanity of vanities, all is vanity — only Jah endures. And the Song of Solomon, the most beautiful love poem ever written, is read in the Ethiopian tradition as the sacred love between Jah and the soul, and between Solomon and the Queen of Sheba herself.

◈ Proverbs · Chapter I — III The Fear of Jah Is the Beginning of Wisdom

1:7The fear of Jah is the beginning of knowledge: but fools despise wisdom and instruction.

3:1My son, forget not my law; but let thine heart keep my commandments:

3:3Let not mercy and truth forsake thee: bind them about thy neck; write them upon the table of thine heart.

3:5Trust in Jah with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.

3:6In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.

3:7Be not wise in thine own eyes: fear Jah, and depart from evil.

3:13Happy is the man that findeth wisdom, and the man that getteth understanding.

3:17Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are peace.

3:18She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her: and happy is every one that retaineth her.

◈ Proverbs · Chapter XXXI The Virtuous Woman

10Who can find a virtuous woman? for her price is far above rubies.

17She girdeth her loins with strength, and strengtheneth her arms.

25Strength and honour are her clothing; and she shall rejoice in time to come.

26She openeth her mouth with wisdom; and in her tongue is the law of kindness.

30Favour is deceitful, and beauty is vain: but a woman that feareth Jah, she shall be praised.

◈ Ecclesiastes · Chapters I · XII Vanity of Vanities · Fear Jah and Keep His Commandments

1:1The words of the Preacher, the son of David, king in Jerusalem.

1:2Vanity of vanities, saith the Preacher, vanity of vanities; all is vanity.

1:9The thing that hath been, it is that which shall be; and that which is done is that which shall be done: and there is no new thing under the sun.

3:1To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven:

3:2A time to be born, and a time to die; a time to plant, and a time to pluck up that which is planted;

3:4A time to weep, and a time to laugh; a time to mourn, and a time to dance;

3:11He hath made every thing beautiful in his time: also he hath set the world in their heart, so that no man can find out the work that Jah maketh from the beginning to the end.

12:13Let us hear the conclusion of the whole matter: Fear Jah, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man.

12:14For Jah shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing, whether it be good, or whether it be evil.

◈ Song of Solomon · Chapters I — VIII I Am Black and Beautiful · Love Is Strong as Death

1:1The song of songs, which is Solomon's.

1:2Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth: for thy love is better than wine.

1:4Draw me, we will run after thee.

1:5I am black, but comely, O ye daughters of Jerusalem, as the tents of Kedar, as the curtains of Solomon.

1:6Look not upon me, because I am black, because the sun hath looked upon me.

2:1I am the rose of Sharon, and the lily of the valleys.

2:4He brought me to the banqueting house, and his banner over me was love.

2:10My beloved spake, and said unto me, Rise up, my love, my fair one, and come away.

2:11For, lo, the winter is past, the rain is over and gone;

2:12The flowers appear on the earth; the time of the singing of birds is come, and the voice of the turtle is heard in our land.

8:6Set me as a seal upon thine heart, as a seal upon thine arm: for love is strong as death; jealousy is cruel as the grave: the coals thereof are coals of fire, which hath a most vehement flame.

8:7Many waters cannot quench love, neither can the floods drown it.
