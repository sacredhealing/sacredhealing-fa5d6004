✦ Chapter I — Who Were the Original Israelites The People of the Book Were Not White · The Ancient Near East · Ham Shem and the African World

1:1The question of the racial identity of the ancient Israelites is not a theological question. It is an archaeological, genetic, and historical question — and the evidence has always pointed in one direction, which is why it has been so thoroughly suppressed.

1:2The land of Canaan, in which Abraham settled and in which the children of Israel were given their inheritance, sits at the crossroads of Africa and Asia. Geographically it is a bridge between Egypt and Mesopotamia. Its people were dark-skinned Semitic peoples of African and Near Eastern descent — not the pale populations of Europe.

1:3Genesis 10 — the Table of Nations — is the oldest ethnographic document in the world. It places the sons of Ham as the fathers of Egypt (Mizraim), Ethiopia (Cush), Canaan, and Phut (Libya/North Africa). Ham's descendants built the first civilisations: Egypt, Babylon, Nineveh, Akkad. The word Ham in Hebrew is Cham — cognate with Khem, the ancient Egyptian word for their own land meaning the black land, the black people.

1:4Abraham himself came from Ur of the Chaldees — modern southern Iraq — a city of dark-skinned Sumerian and Akkadian people. His wife Sarah, his nephew Lot, his son Isaac, and his grandson Jacob were all from this same stock. When Joseph was thrown into Egypt and walked among the Egyptians, no one looked at him twice. He was indistinguishable from them. It was only when he opened his mouth and spoke Hebrew that his brothers eventually recognised him as different.

1:5Moses was raised in Pharaoh's household as an Egyptian prince. Acts 7:22 tells us he was educated in all the wisdom of Egypt. When he fled to Midian after killing the Egyptian, Jethro's daughters described him to their father as an Egyptian man who delivered us — not as a Hebrew, not as a foreigner, but as an Egyptian. He looked like an Egyptian. He was Black.

1:6The prophet Jeremiah, writing to the exiles in Babylon, asked: Can the Ethiopian change his skin, or the leopard his spots? (Jeremiah 13:23). He used the Ethiopian as the archetype of a people with a fixed, recognisable skin colour — and drew no distinction between them and Israel. They were the same people. They came from the same stock.

1:7The ancient Greek historian Herodotus, who visited Egypt in the 5th century BCE, described the Egyptians and Ethiopians as having black skin and woolly hair. He also noted that the Colchians of the Black Sea — whom he believed to be descended from Egyptian soldiers — had the same characteristics. The ancient world knew what the ancient Israelites looked like. The medieval European church decided to forget.

1:8When John the Revelator in the Book of Revelation (1:14-15) described the risen Christ, he wrote: His head and his hairs were white like wool, as white as snow; and his eyes were as a flame of fire; And his feet like unto fine brass, as if they burned in a furnace. Brass burned in a furnace is dark, burnt bronze. Wool is tightly coiled. John was describing a man who looked like the Black people of the ancient world — because He was one of them.

✦ Chapter II — The Beta Israel — The Ethiopian Jews The Falasha · The House of Israel · Four Thousand Years of Unbroken Covenant · Keepers of the Ark

2:1The Beta Israel — called Falasha by outsiders, a word meaning exile or stranger in Ge'ez which they themselves reject — are the Black Jewish community of Ethiopia who have maintained the faith of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob without interruption for over three thousand years. They are among the oldest continuous Jewish communities on earth, predating the destruction of the First Temple, predating the Babylonian exile, predating the codification of the Talmud.

2:2Their own tradition holds that they descend from the retinue that accompanied Menelik I — the son of Solomon and Makeda the Queen of Sheba — when he returned to Ethiopia from Jerusalem. According to the Kebra Nagast, Menelik brought with him the firstborn sons of the elders of Israel — including Azariah the son of Zadok the High Priest, who carried the Ark of the Covenant out of Jerusalem and into Ethiopia. These were not converts. These were Israelites by blood and by covenant.

2:3A second tradition holds that the Beta Israel descend from the tribe of Dan — one of the twelve tribes — who migrated southward through Egypt into Ethiopia before the Babylonian exile of 586 BCE. This is consistent with the ancient Jewish tradition that the tribe of Dan went into exile before the other tribes and settled in various parts of Africa. The mediaeval Jewish traveller Benjamin of Tudela (1165 CE) reported large Jewish communities in Africa including in Ethiopia.

2:4A third tradition — now supported by genetic evidence — holds that the Beta Israel descend from a mixture of the original Israelite settlers who came with Menelik, subsequent waves of Jewish refugees who fled the Roman destruction of Jerusalem in 70 CE, and the indigenous Agaw people of Ethiopia who converted to Judaism at various points in Ethiopian history. The genetic research of Sergio Della Pergola, Michael Hammer and others at the Hebrew University has found Y-chromosome haplogroups in the Beta Israel consistent with Middle Eastern Semitic populations — confirming their descent from ancient Israelite men.

2:5What makes the Beta Israel unique in the history of world Judaism is what they preserved. They maintained the Torah. They maintained the Sabbath with absolute strictness — refusing to light fire, refusing to travel, refusing to work, even to the point of military disadvantage. They maintained the Levitical purity laws. They maintained animal sacrifice — a practice abandoned by Rabbinical Judaism after the destruction of the Temple in 70 CE. They had priests (Kohanim) and monks (Debtera). They built their synagogues — called mesgid — next to water for ritual purification.

2:6Crucially, the Beta Israel preserved books that Rabbinical Judaism abandoned. They preserved 1 Enoch — found in the Ethiopic Ge'ez language and only fully recovered in the West when James Bruce brought manuscripts back from Ethiopia in 1773. They preserved Jubilees. They preserved the Book of Jasher. They preserved the Kebra Nagast as their royal and sacred chronicle. These books survived for three thousand years because Black Israelites in Ethiopia kept them when no one else would.

2:7The Israeli state airlifted approximately 14,000 Beta Israel to Israel in Operation Moses (1984) and Operation Solomon (1991). Despite their documented ancient lineage and their centuries of persecution by Ethiopian Christian rulers, many were subjected to further discrimination upon arrival in Israel — forced to undergo conversion by the Chief Rabbinate, denied housing, placed in segregated absorption centres. The irony was not lost on the world: the people who had preserved Judaism in its most ancient and complete form were told they were not Jewish enough by those who had lost what the Ethiopians had kept.

2:8Among the Beta Israel today, the tradition of the Ark is kept alive. The tabot — a replica of the Ark of the Covenant — is central to Ethiopian Orthodox worship. Every church in Ethiopia contains a tabot in its inner sanctuary, the Holy of Holies, accessible only to the priests. The original Ark itself, according to Ethiopian tradition maintained for three thousand years, rests in the treasury of the Church of Our Lady Mary of Zion in Axum, guarded by a single monk whose sole vocation from birth to death is its protection. He is called the Guardian of the Ark. He never leaves. He is replaced only when he dies.

✦ Chapter III — The Twelve Tribes Across Africa The Scattering Prophesied · Deuteronomy 28 · The Lost Tribes · The Nations Within Nations

3:1Deuteronomy 28 is the most important chapter in the Torah for understanding the history of Black people. It contains the blessings for obedience and the curses for disobedience — and the curses are a precise prophetic account of what happened to the African peoples through the Atlantic slave trade and colonial subjugation. No other people in recorded history have fulfilled these curses with such exactness.

3:2Deuteronomy 28:68 says: And Jah shall bring thee into Egypt again with ships, by the way whereof I spake unto thee, Thou shalt see it no more again: and there ye shall be sold unto your enemies for bondmen and bondwomen, and no man shall buy you. Egypt in Hebrew is Mitzraim — but scholars of Biblical typology note that Mitzraim is used throughout prophetic literature as a symbol of bondage and oppression, not merely the geographic location. The African peoples carried to the Americas in ships — sold into slavery — and told that no one would ransom them — fulfilled this verse with a precision that no European or Asian or other peoples has come close to matching.

3:3Deuteronomy 28:37 says: And thou shalt become an astonishment, a proverb, and a byword, among all nations whither Jah shall lead thee. The word Negro — from the Latin for black — became the generic byword for an entire people stripped of their tribal names, their languages, their lineages, their histories. They became identified only by their colour. They became the byword.

3:4Deuteronomy 28:64 says: And Jah shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other. The African diaspora spans every continent. No other people has been scattered so thoroughly, so forcibly, and so completely as the African peoples of the Atlantic slave trade — taken from West Africa, Central Africa, and East Africa and distributed across North America, South America, the Caribbean, and Europe.

3:5The identification of specific African ethnic groups with specific Israelite tribes has been argued by scholars and communities for centuries. The Igbo of Nigeria maintain a tradition of Hebrew origin supported by linguistic parallels (the Igbo word chi for personal god mirrors the Hebrew El), circumcision on the eighth day, Sabbath observance, and prohibitions on certain foods. The historian David Asante documented that the Igbo word for marketplace — eke — appears to derive from the Hebrew ekev.

3:6The Ashanti of Ghana maintain traditions of a sacred Ark — called the Ark of the Covenant — carried before their armies. The Akan peoples have the word Nyame for the supreme God — cognate with the Hebrew Name. The Lemba people of Zimbabwe and South Africa — studied extensively by geneticist David Golding and the Oxford University genetics team — have Y-chromosome haplogroup patterns almost identical to the Jewish priestly Kohanim, particularly the Cohen Modal Haplotype, at rates comparable to Ashkenazi Jewish populations. Their oral tradition says they came from a place called Sena north of them, carried a sacred object called the ngoma lungundu — the Ark that thunders — and followed dietary and ritual laws identical to Mosaic law.

3:7The Rastafari movement — emerging from Jamaica in the 1930s under the theological framework of Marcus Garvey, Leonard Howell, Joseph Nathaniel Hibbert and others — made this identification explicit and systematic. Drawing on the Ethiopian tradition, the King James Bible, and the work of earlier Black theologians including Edward Blyden and Alexander Crummell, Rastafari theology holds that the enslaved Africans of the diaspora are the scattered children of Israel, that their return to Africa (Zion) is the prophesied restoration, and that Haile Selassie I is the fulfillment of Genesis 49:10 and Revelation 5:5 — the Lion of the Tribe of Judah who opens the seals.

3:8The Twelve Tribes of Israel organisation — founded in Jamaica in 1968 by Vernon Carrington, known as Prophet Gad — codified a system assigning each of the twelve tribes to a birth month, identifying all peoples of African descent as among the twelve sons of Jacob. The Bobo Ashanti — founded by Prince Emmanuel in 1958 — maintain the priestly and kingly order of the Levites and the royal line of Judah as living realities within their community in Jamaica. The Nyahbinghi order — the oldest Rastafari mansion — holds the spirit of resistance against Babylon (the Western colonial and capitalist system) as a sacred duty rooted in the covenant of Israel.

3:9The genetic evidence continues to accumulate. A 2020 study by the Wellcome Sanger Institute found that populations in West and Central Africa — the primary source of the Atlantic slave trade — show genetic signatures consistent with ancient Middle Eastern admixture dating to the period of the Israelite kingdoms. While genetics cannot prove tribal identity, it confirms that the African peoples are not merely spiritually connected to the ancient Israelites — they are genetically continuous with the ancient Near Eastern world from which Israel emerged.

✦ Chapter V — Why Ethiopia Was Never Colonised The Battle of Adwa 1896 · The Divine Protection · Psalm 68:31 · The Promise to Cush · Haile Selassie and the Prophecy

5:1Ethiopia is the only African nation never colonised by a European power. This is not a matter of luck. It is not a matter of geography. It is not a matter of inferior European military technology. Italy invaded with modern rifles, artillery, and trained soldiers. Ethiopia defeated them with faith, with strategy, and with what the Ethiopian people have always understood to be the direct intervention of Jah.

5:2The Battle of Adwa on 1 March 1896 was the most decisive African military victory over a European colonial power in history. Emperor Menelik II — understanding that Italy was using the Treaty of Wuchale to claim a protectorate over Ethiopia — mobilised an army of approximately 100,000 soldiers, including cavalry, infantry, and artillery. Queen Taytu Betul, his wife, commanded her own forces. The Italian army under General Oreste Baratieri had approximately 17,000 men. In a single day of fighting in the highlands of Tigray, the Ethiopian forces annihilated the Italian army — killing 6,000 soldiers, capturing 3,000, and sending the rest into rout.

5:3The Italian commander Baratieri divided his forces into four columns in difficult terrain and sent them forward at night without proper coordination. The Ethiopian forces, knowing their own land, surrounded each column separately and destroyed them in detail. It was not a superior number of Ethiopians that won. It was superior knowledge of the land, superior unity of command, superior intelligence, and — the Ethiopians believe — the intercession of the Ark of the Covenant, the tabot of Our Lady Mary of Zion, which was carried before the army as it had been carried before the armies of Israel from the time of Moses.

5:4The effect of Adwa on the African world was seismic. For the first time in the modern era, an African army had defeated a European colonial force in open battle, on African soil, on African terms. W.E.B. Du Bois, the great Pan-African scholar, wrote that Adwa proved that Black men could fight — that the African was not naturally subject to European domination. Marcus Garvey, whose theological and political framework became the foundation of Rastafari, was inspired by Adwa. Haile Selassie I was eleven years old when Adwa happened. He grew up understanding that Ethiopia had a divine mission that no European power could annul.

5:5Psalm 68:31 says: Ethiopia shall soon stretch out her hands unto Jah. This verse, written by David three thousand years ago, is the most quoted verse in Rastafari theology. It is the prophetic promise of the restoration of Black African people to their covenant relationship with Jah — not as slaves, not as converts to European religion, not as subjects of colonial administration, but as the covenant people fulfilling their original calling. Ethiopia — the land, the people, the church, the royal house — is the vessel of this prophecy.

5:6When Mussolini's Italy invaded Ethiopia in 1935 — the second Italian attempt at colonial conquest — Haile Selassie I stood before the League of Nations in Geneva and delivered an address that stunned the world. Speaking in Amharic, translated simultaneously, he said: It is us today. It will be you tomorrow. He was warning the assembled European nations that the system of collective security that was supposed to protect all nations equally was being allowed to fail because the nation being destroyed was African. The League of Nations imposed sanctions that were too weak and lifted them too quickly. Ethiopia was occupied for five years.

5:7But Ethiopia was not colonised. Occupation is not colonisation. The Italians were driven out in 1941 by Ethiopian resistance fighters — the Arbegnoch, the Patriots — in alliance with British and Commonwealth forces. Haile Selassie returned to Addis Ababa on 5 May 1941 — exactly five years after the Italian army entered the capital. When he returned he said: On this day which men of Ethiopia have long awaited, which those who have fallen in the struggle to maintain the freedom and the name of Ethiopia have hoped for even unto death, I am pleased and happy to announce to the Ethiopian people that Ethiopia has been liberated from the enemy. Those who have paid in their blood have not done so in vain. Ethiopia has arisen.

5:8The divine protection of Ethiopia is not a metaphor to those who study her history. Israel was given a promise in the covenant with Abraham that those who bless you I will bless, and those who curse you I will curse. Ethiopia blessed Israel when she preserved the Ark. Ethiopia blessed Israel when she kept the Torah for three thousand years in the Beta Israel communities. Ethiopia blessed Israel when she kept the scriptures — Enoch, Jubilees, the Kebra Nagast — that the Western world lost. And Ethiopia was blessed in return — with the one exemption in the entire continent from the colonial catastrophe that engulfed every other African nation.

5:9This is not coincidence. This is covenant. The same covenant that Jah made with Abraham. The same covenant sealed in the Kebra Nagast when Menelik I carried the Ark to Zion in Africa. The same covenant confirmed in Revelation 5:5 when the Lion of the Tribe of Judah — whose earthly representative bore the title King of Kings of Ethiopia, Conquering Lion of the Tribe of Judah — prevailed to open the seals. The covenant has not been broken. The covenant was never lost. It was kept in Ethiopia while the rest of the world forgot.

✦ Chapter VI — The Real Twelve Tribes — Where They Went The Assyrian Exile · The Babylonian Exile · The Roman Destruction · The Diaspora That Became Africa

6:1The Northern Kingdom of Israel — the ten northern tribes: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Naphtali, Manasseh, Simeon, Issachar, Zebulun, Ephraim, and Dan — was conquered by the Assyrian Empire in 722 BCE under Tiglath-Pileser III and his successor Sargon II. Approximately 27,290 Israelites were deported to Assyria and the cities of the Medes. The Bible records this in 2 Kings 17. They did not return. They scattered across the ancient Near East and into Africa.

6:2The Southern Kingdom of Judah — comprising the tribes of Judah, Benjamin, and the priestly tribe of Levi — was conquered by Babylon in 586 BCE. The Temple was destroyed. Approximately 10,000 of the elite were taken into exile in Babylon. Some fled into Egypt — as Jeremiah records in chapters 43-44. Some fled further into Africa. This is the beginning of the documented Jewish communities of Egypt, of Libya, of Sudan, and of Ethiopia.

6:3The Roman destruction of Jerusalem in 70 CE under Titus — the event commemorated annually in Judaism as Tisha B'Av — was the final catastrophic dispersion. The historian Josephus records that 1.1 million Jews died in the siege and approximately 97,000 were taken captive and sold into slavery throughout the Roman Empire. Those who escaped fled south and east — into Africa, into Arabia, into Persia. Many went to Egypt. Many went further south along the Nile into Nubia, into Sudan, into Ethiopia. Many went west along the North African coast into what is now Libya, Tunisia, Algeria, Morocco.

6:4The Jewish communities of North Africa — documented in the Talmud and in Roman records — were substantial and ancient. The Berber peoples of North Africa have genetic and cultural evidence of significant ancient Jewish admixture. The historian Ibn Khaldun recorded in the 14th century that several Berber tribes in the Maghreb were of Jewish origin. The Tuareg of the Sahara maintain practices strikingly parallel to ancient Levitical law. The Fulani of West Africa — one of the most widely distributed peoples of the continent — have traditions pointing to a Middle Eastern origin mixed with African peoples.

6:5The assignment of specific modern African or diaspora populations to specific Israelite tribes is a matter of ongoing scholarship and tradition. The following represents the framework maintained in Rastafari and Black Hebrew Israelite tradition, drawn from historical, linguistic, and cultural evidence:

6:6JUDAH — The Bantus of southern and central Africa, and those of their descendants who were taken primarily to the islands of the Caribbean — particularly Jamaica, Haiti, Barbados, and Trinidad — and to the American South. Judah was the royal tribe from which David and Solomon came, and from which the Messiah descended. The disproportionate number of leaders, musicians, and prophets of African diaspora origin from these regions has led many scholars of Rastafari theology to see the tribal identity of Judah expressed in cultural creativity and spiritual authority.

6:7LEVI — The Yoruba of Nigeria, Benin, and their diaspora in Brazil (Candomble), Cuba (Santeria), and Haiti (Vodou). The Yoruba maintained a priestly tradition — the Ifa oracular system — with a complexity and sophistication comparable to the Levitical system. Their art, their theology, their linguistic sophistication, and their role as spiritual intermediaries across West Africa and the diaspora parallels the role of the Levites in ancient Israel.

6:8SIMEON — The Ashanti and Akan peoples of Ghana and Ivory Coast, taken primarily to South Carolina and Georgia in the American South. The Akan naming system — children named for the day of the week on which they were born — has parallels in ancient Semitic naming conventions. The Akan tradition of the Nyame — the supreme God who cannot be approached directly — parallels the Israelite understanding of Jah as a God who requires intermediaries and cannot be seen by human eyes.

6:9DAN — The Samson story in Judges is a Dan story — Samson was a Danite. The tribe of Dan was associated with serpents in Jacob's blessing (Genesis 49:17: Dan shall be a serpent by the way, an adder in the path). The tradition of the Beta Israel holds that the tribe of Dan migrated into Ethiopia. The Agaw people — with whom the Beta Israel intermarried — may preserve this Danite lineage. Additionally, some traditions place Dan among the peoples of West Africa who maintained serpent-worship traditions before Islamic conversion.

6:10NAPHTALI — The Igbo of Nigeria and their diaspora in the Caribbean. The Igbo maintain the clearest and most documented parallels with ancient Israelite custom: circumcision on the eighth day, the title Eze (cognate with Hebrew Ish — man, leader) for their kings, the concept of chi as a personal divine force, dietary taboos matching Leviticus 11, the tradition of a High God who gave law through intermediaries, and oral traditions of migration from east of the great river.

6:11GAD — The Fante of Ghana. ASHER — The Fula/Fulani peoples stretching from Senegal across the Sahel. ISSACHAR — The Saharan Tuareg. ZEBULUN — The peoples of the Congo basin. EPHRAIM and MANASSEH — The two sons of Joseph, representing the dominant inheritance of the Joseph blessing, are seen in the largest and most widely scattered African diaspora populations: the Bantu-speaking majority of sub-Saharan Africa, whose expansion across the continent beginning approximately 3,000 years ago parallels the growth of Israel described in Numbers. REUBEN — The peoples of Sudan and northeastern Africa, the oldest sedentary agricultural peoples of the Nile valley.

6:12These identifications are not doctrines. They are frameworks for understanding. What is certain — from archaeology, from genetics, from the historical record, from the plain reading of the prophetic texts — is that the original Israelites were dark-skinned African and Near Eastern people; that they scattered into Africa and beyond when their kingdoms were destroyed; that their descendants in Africa preserved the covenant, the scripture, the faith, and the law with greater fidelity than any European institution; and that the restoration prophesied in Isaiah 11, Ezekiel 37, Jeremiah 30, Amos 9:14-15, and Revelation 7 is not a metaphor. It is a historical promise, addressed to real people, with real flesh and real blood and real ancestors who walked with Jah.
