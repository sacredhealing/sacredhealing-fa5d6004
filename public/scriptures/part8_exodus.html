Exodus — Shemot

The Deliverance of Israel from Egypt

△ △ △

These are the names of the children of Israel who came into Egypt. Jah heard their groaning and Jah remembered his covenant. From the burning bush to the parting of the Red Sea, from the Ten Commandments on Sinai to the building of the Ark — Exodus is the great liberation. Every oppressed people sees themselves in Israel. Every Rastaman knows: Babylon shall fall, and the people shall be free.

🔥 Chapter I — II Israel in Bondage · The Birth of Moses

1:7And the children of Israel were fruitful, and increased abundantly, and multiplied, and waxed exceeding mighty; and the land was filled with them.

1:8Now there arose up a new king over Egypt, which knew not Joseph.

1:11Therefore they did set over them taskmasters to afflict them with their burdens. And they built for Pharaoh treasure cities, Pithom and Raamses.

1:14And they made their lives bitter with hard bondage, in mortar, and in brick, and in all manner of service in the field: all their service, wherein they made them serve, was with rigour.

2:23And it came to pass in process of time, that the king of Egypt died: and the children of Israel sighed by reason of the bondage, and they cried, and their cry came up unto Jah by reason of the bondage.

2:24And Jah heard their groaning, and Jah remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob.

2:25And Jah looked upon the children of Israel, and Jah had respect unto them.

🔥 Chapter III The Burning Bush · I AM THAT I AM

1Now Moses kept the flock of Jethro his father in law, the priest of Midian: and he led the flock to the backside of the desert, and came to the mountain of Jah, even to Horeb.

2And the angel of Jah appeared unto him in a flame of fire out of the midst of a bush: and he looked, and, behold, the bush burned with fire, and the bush was not consumed.

3And Moses said, I will now turn aside, and see this great sight, why the bush is not burnt.

4And when Jah saw that he turned aside to see, Jah called unto him out of the midst of the bush, and said, Moses, Moses. And he said, Here am I.

5And he said, Draw not nigh hither: put off thy shoes from off thy feet, for the place whereon thou standest is holy ground.

6Moreover he said, I am the Jah of thy father, the Jah of Abraham, the Jah of Isaac, and the Jah of Jacob. And Moses hid his face; for he was afraid to look upon Jah.

7And Jah said, I have surely seen the affliction of my people which are in Egypt, and have heard their cry by reason of their taskmasters; for I know their sorrows.

8And I am come down to deliver them out of the hand of the Egyptians, and to bring them up out of that land unto a good land and a large, unto a land flowing with milk and honey.

10Come now therefore, and I will send thee unto Pharaoh, that thou mayest bring forth my people the children of Israel out of Egypt.

13And Moses said unto Jah, Behold, when I come unto the children of Israel, and shall say unto them, The Jah of your fathers hath sent me unto you; and they shall say to me, What is his name? what shall I say unto them?

14And Jah said unto Moses, I AM THAT I AM: and he said, Thus shalt thou say unto the children of Israel, I AM hath sent me unto you.

15And Jah said moreover unto Moses, Thus shalt thou say unto the children of Israel, Jah, the Jah of your fathers, the Jah of Abraham, the Jah of Isaac, and the Jah of Jacob, hath sent me unto you: this is my name for ever, and this is my memorial unto all generations.

🔥 Chapters VII — XII The Ten Plagues of Egypt · The Passover

7:10And Moses and Aaron went in unto Pharaoh, and they did so as Jah had commanded: and Aaron cast down his rod before Pharaoh, and before his servants, and it became a serpent.

7:20And Moses and Aaron did so, as Jah commanded; and he lifted up the rod, and smote the waters that were in the river, in the sight of Pharaoh, and in the sight of his servants; and all the waters that were in the river were turned to blood.

9:23And Moses stretched forth his rod toward heaven: and Jah sent thunder and hail, and the fire ran along upon the ground; and Jah rained hail upon the land of Egypt.

10:21And Jah said unto Moses, Stretch out thine hand toward heaven, that there may be darkness over the land of Egypt, even darkness which may be felt.

11:1And Jah said unto Moses, Yet will I bring one plague more upon Pharaoh, and upon Egypt; afterwards he will let you go hence.

12:12For I will pass through the land of Egypt this night, and will smite all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, both man and beast; and against all the gods of Egypt I will execute judgment: I am Jah.

12:13And the blood shall be to you for a token upon the houses where ye are: and when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you, when I smite the land of Egypt.

12:14And this day shall be unto you for a memorial; and ye shall keep it a feast to Jah throughout your generations; ye shall keep it a feast by an ordinance for ever.

12:31And he called for Moses and Aaron by night, and said, Rise up, and get you forth from among my people, both ye and the children of Israel; and go, serve Jah, as ye have said.

12:40Now the sojourning of the children of Israel, who dwelt in Egypt, was four hundred and thirty years.

12:41And it came to pass at the end of the four hundred and thirty years, even the selfsame day it came to pass, that all the hosts of Jah went out from the land of Egypt.

🔥 Chapter XIV The Parting of the Red Sea

13And Moses said unto the people, Fear ye not, stand still, and see the salvation of Jah, which he will shew to you to day: for the Egyptians whom ye have seen to day, ye shall see them again no more for ever.

14Jah shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace.

21And Moses stretched out his hand over the sea; and Jah caused the sea to go back by a strong east wind all that night, and made the sea dry land, and the waters were divided.

22And the children of Israel went into the midst of the sea upon the dry ground: and the waters were a wall unto them on their right hand, and on their left.

27And Moses stretched forth his hand over the sea, and the sea returned to his strength when the morning appeared; and the Egyptians fled against it; and Jah overthrew the Egyptians in the midst of the sea.

30Thus Jah saved Israel that day out of the hand of the Egyptians; and Israel saw the Egyptians dead upon the sea shore.

31And Israel saw that great work which Jah did upon the Egyptians: and the people feared Jah, and believed Jah, and his servant Moses.

🔥 Chapter XX The Ten Commandments on Mount Sinai

1And Jah spake all these words, saying,

2I am Jah thy Jah, which have brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.

3Thou shalt have no other gods before me.

4Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth.

7Thou shalt not take the name of Jah thy Jah in vain; for Jah will not hold him guiltless that taketh his name in vain.

8Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy.

9Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work:

10But the seventh day is the sabbath of Jah thy Jah: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates.

12Honour thy father and thy mother: that thy days may be long upon the land which Jah thy Jah giveth thee.

13Thou shalt not kill.

14Thou shalt not commit adultery.

15Thou shalt not steal.

16Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour.

17Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's house, thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's wife, nor his manservant, nor his maidservant, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor any thing that is thy neighbour's.

18And all the people saw the thunderings, and the lightnings, and the noise of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking: and when the people saw it, they removed, and stood afar off.

19And they said unto Moses, Speak thou with us, and we will hear: but let not Jah speak with us, lest we die.

20And Moses said unto the people, Fear not: for Jah is come to prove you, and that his fear may be before your faces, that ye sin not.
