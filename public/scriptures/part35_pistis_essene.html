Pistis Sophia

The Gnostic Christ · The Books of the Saviour · The Inner Mysteries

✦ ✦ ✦

Pistis Sophia — Faith Wisdom — is the longest and most detailed of the Gnostic Gospels. Written in Coptic from Greek originals, discovered in Egypt in 1773 and first translated into English in 1896, it records the teachings Yeshua gave to his disciples in the eleven years after the Resurrection — eleven years of post-resurrection teaching that the canonical Gospels compress into forty days. It is a vast, intricate cosmology of the divine emanations, the fall of Sophia, the structure of the inner planes, and the science of the soul's ascent back to the Light. Mary Magdalene speaks more than all the other disciples combined. She asks the questions that open each new level of teaching. Yeshua calls her the Pleroma of all Pleromas — the Fullness of all Fullness.

✦ Part I — The Structure of the Pistis Sophia The Eleven-Year Teaching · The Books of the Saviour · Mary as Principal Questioner

Pistis Sophia opens with Yeshua ascending to the highest Light and returning transformed — his garment blazing with divine radiance — to teach his disciples the mysteries of the inner worlds. He teaches for eleven years after the Resurrection. The disciples — men and women together, sitting in a circle — receive teachings of extraordinary depth and complexity about the nature of the divine worlds, the emanations of light, the fall of consciousness into matter, and the path of return.

The text is structured as a long dialogue. Yeshua speaks. A disciple asks a question. Yeshua responds. The questions are of different qualities — and this is significant. The male disciples ask questions that reveal outer, conceptual understanding. Mary Magdalene's questions — and she asks the majority of them — reveal an inner, experiential understanding. She is not learning about the mysteries from outside. She is recognising them from within, because she has already partially traversed the path Yeshua is describing.

Yeshua says of her: Mary, thou blessed one, whom I will perfect in all mysteries of those of the Height, discourse in openness, thou, whose heart is raised to the kingdom of heaven more than all thy brethren. And again: Well said, thou spiritual and pure light Mary. This is the solution of the word. Thirty-nine of the forty-six questions asked in the first three books are asked by Mary Magdalene. The text is unambiguous: she is the supreme disciple, the highest initiate, the one who understands most deeply.

✦ Part II — The Fall of Sophia and the Redemption The Thirteenth Aeon · The Descent into Matter · The Cry for Light

The central narrative of Pistis Sophia is the story of Sophia — the divine Wisdom-emanation — who, in her longing to perceive the infinite Light directly, descends from her proper place in the thirteenth Aeon into the world of matter and becomes trapped in the chaos of the lower planes, surrounded by hostile archonic forces who seek to drain her light.

Sophia cries out from the darkness with a series of repentance hymns — thirteen in total — that Yeshua connects directly to the Psalms of David. Each repentance is a Psalm. Sophia cries: O Light, I have sinned in the emanations, and I am come into these regions, since I transgressed and went forth out of my region; and now I am in these regions and my light is diminished in me — for all my power which is in me is gone from me, and I uttered my voice to the Light of the Lights, and it reached up to the Light. And now O Light, forgive my transgression and save me.

The Saviour — Yeshua — is sent by the First Mystery to descend into the chaos and retrieve Sophia, restoring her light, lifting her back to the Thirteenth Aeon. This is the Cosmic Redemption — and it is the inner meaning of every external event of the Gospel narrative. The Incarnation, the ministry, the Passion, the Resurrection — all are the outer expression of this inner cosmic act of the Logos retrieving the fallen Sophia, the masculine divine principle rescuing the feminine divine principle from exile in matter, so that together they can return to the Source.

This story is not mythology in the dismissive sense. It is the most precise description ever written of what happens in the consciousness of every human soul: the descent from the clarity of pure being into the confusion of material identification, the suffering of exile from the Light, the cry for redemption, and the response of grace. Every human heart is Sophia. Every moment of genuine prayer is Sophia's cry. And the Light that responds — the Grace that descends — is the Saviour-principle: not external to the soul but the deepest dimension of the soul itself, the divine spark that never fully went into exile, that always remained connected to the Source, that rises to meet the cry and lead the lost consciousness home.

✦ Part III — The Mysteries of Light · The Path of Return The Five Trees · The Seven Amens · The Twelve Saviours

Pistis Sophia describes a detailed inner cosmology of the divine worlds through which the soul must ascend. There are emanations called the Five Trees in the Paradise of Light — representing the five levels of divine knowing. There are Seven Amens — affirming the seven dimensions of divine truth. There are Twelve Saviours corresponding to the twelve signs of the zodiac and the twelve apostles. There are Twenty-Four Invisibles, the Three Triple Powers, and above all — the Treasure of Light, the Treasury of the First Mystery, the highest accessible dimension of the divine.

The soul's ascent through these worlds is not automatic. It requires the Mysteries — the initiatic practices transmitted by Yeshua — the Baptism of Fire, the Baptism of the Holy Spirit, the Rite of the Spiritual Chrism, the Rite of the Redemption, the Mystery of the Five Seals. These are not external religious ceremonies. They are precise inner operations — what the Vedic tradition calls Kriyas and the Tantric tradition calls Mudras and Nyasas — by which the practitioner systematically activates and purifies each level of their energy system, dissolving the karmic residues that bind the soul to lower planes and opening the channels through which the divine Light can flow upward to its source.

Yeshua says: The Mysteries of the Light are exceedingly exalted and glorious above all things in the world and above all places which are below the Light. The mysteries are the bonds of all the Aeons, and the bonds of all the worlds, and the bonds of all the powers; these are the Mystery which the Lights of the great invisible First Mystery have emanated, and their Mystery itself has drawn all the worlds and all the Aeons and all the places of all the Lights into subjection, through the bonds of the Mystery. This is the science of consciousness — the recognition that reality itself is held together by the power of divine knowing, and that the human soul, in recovering that knowing, participates in the very force that sustains the cosmos.

The Essene Gospel of Peace

Book One · The Communions · The Earthly Mother · The Heavenly Father

✦ ✦ ✦

The Essene Gospel of Peace was translated by Edmond Bordeaux Szekely from ancient Aramaic manuscripts preserved in the Secret Archives of the Vatican and in the Royal Library of the Habsburgs, first published in 1928. It records teachings of Yeshua to a group of sick and suffering people who came to him in the wilderness, teachings about the healing power of the earthly elements — sun, water, air, earth — and the angelic forces that work through them. It reveals a Yeshua deeply concerned with the body as a temple, with fasting and purification, with the living food of the earth, and with the direct encounter with the divine through the natural world.

🌿 The Essene Gospel of Peace · Book One The Sick Come to Yeshua · The Teaching of the Mother and Father Angels

And Yeshua spoke to them, saying: Happy are you, Children of Light, for the kingdom of the Earthly Mother and the kingdom of the Heavenly Father are yours. Your Heavenly Father and your Earthly Mother, and all the angels of day and of night, seek to help you. For in the beginning was Jah, and Jah was the Word, and the Word was the Law. And the Law was with Jah. And the Law was Jah. And this Law was the life of all living things, and this life was the light of all sons of man.

Know that your body is the temple of your spirit, and that your spirit is the breath of Jah. And if you defile your body with sin, your spirit will be defiled, and you will be cut off from the body of your Earthly Mother and from the spirit of your Heavenly Father. And the angels of day and of night will leave you. And Satan and his servants will dwell within your body.

And so I tell you truly: if you eat living food, the fire of life shall quicken you. But if you eat dead food, the fire of death shall destroy you. For life comes only from life, and from death comes always death. For everything which kills your foods also kills your bodies. And everything which destroys your bodies also destroys your souls.

Seek the fresh air of the forest and of the fields, and there in the midst of them shall you find the angel of air. Put off your shoes and your clothing and suffer the angel of air to embrace all your body. Then breathe long and deeply, that the angel of air may be brought within you. I tell you truly, the angel of air shall cast out of your body all uncleannesses which defiled it without and within.

And if you would that the angel of water baptise you inwardly, that you become free from sin, seek the running stream which flows from the mountains, and there shall the angel of water give you life and truth and holiness. Submerge your body in the water. For I tell you truly, the angel of water shall cast out of your body all which is unclean and evil-smelling.

Seek the angel of sunlight. Put off your shoes and your clothing and suffer the angel of sunlight to embrace all your body. Then breathe long and deeply, that the angel of sunlight may be brought within you. And the angel of sunlight shall cast out of your body all uncleannesses which defiled it without and within.

I tell you truly, holy is the angel of earth who rules over all the riches of the kingdom of our Earthly Mother. And holy is the work of our hands when we work together with the angel of earth: for no man can come before the face of Jah, whom the angel of earth does not know.

Your Earthly Mother is in you, and you in her. She bore you; she gives you life. It was she who gave to you your body, and to her shall you one day give it back again. Happy are you when you come to know her and her kingdom; if you receive your Earthly Mother's angels and if you do her laws. I tell you truly, he who does these things shall never see disease. For the power of our Earthly Mother is above all. And it destroys Satan and his kingdom, and has rule over all your bodies and all living things.

The morning communion: Our Earthly Mother who art in nature, who gatherest thy children to thyself, bless thy children and give them thy peace. Let the angel of life enfold my body. Let the angel of joy tune my soul. Let the angel of sun embrace my spirit.

The evening communion: Our Heavenly Father who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. For thine is the kingdom, the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.
