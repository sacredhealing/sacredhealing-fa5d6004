The Restored Gospels

The Words of Yeshua — Unaltered, Unfiltered, Restored

✦ ✦ ✦

Here are gathered three sacred witnesses to the life and teaching of Yeshua of Nazareth — called Issa in the Himalayan traditions, called the Christ by the Greek-speaking world. The Gospel of Yohanan (John) preserves the mystical and cosmic Christ. The Gospel of Thomas preserves 114 secret sayings found in the sands of Egypt in 1945. The Gospel of the Holy Twelve restores the compassionate teachings suppressed from the canon. All names are restored to their Hebrew and Aramaic origins. The divine name is Jah. The Spirit is Ruach Ha'Kodesh.

✝ The Gospel of Yeshua According to Yohanan · The Mystical Gospel
·I· Chapter I In the Beginning was the Word

1In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with Jah, and the Word was Jah.

2The same was in the beginning with Jah.

3All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made.

4In him was life; and the life was the light of men.

5And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.

9That was the true Light, which lighteth every man that cometh into the world.

10He was in the world, and the world was made by him, and the world knew him not.

11He came unto his own, and his own received him not.

12But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of Jah, even to them that believe on his name:

13Which were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of Jah.

14And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father, full of grace and truth.

·III· Chapter III Ye Must Be Born Again

3Yeshua answered and said unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of Jah.

5Yeshua answered, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born of water and of the Ruach Ha'Kodesh, he cannot enter into the kingdom of Jah.

6That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Ruach Ha'Kodesh is spirit.

8The wind bloweth where it listeth, and thou hearest the sound thereof, but canst not tell whence it cometh, and whither it goeth: so is every one that is born of the Ruach Ha'Kodesh.

16For Jah so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.

·XIV· Chapter XIV I Am the Way, the Truth, and the Life

1Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in Jah, believe also in me.

2In my Father's house are many mansions: if it were not so, I would have told you. I go to prepare a place for you.

6Yeshua saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.

16And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you for ever;

17Even the Ruach Ha'Kodesh of truth; whom the world cannot receive, because it seeth him not, neither knoweth him: but ye know him; for he dwelleth with you, and shall be in you.

26But the Comforter, which is the Ruach Ha'Kodesh, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.

27Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.

·XVII· Chapter XVII The High Priestly Prayer of Yeshua

1These words spake Yeshua, and lifted up his eyes to heaven, and said, Father, the hour is come; glorify thy Son, that thy Son also may glorify thee.

3And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true Jah, and Yeshua the Anointed, whom thou hast sent.

11And now I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I come to thee. Holy Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we are.

21That they all may be one; as thou, Father, art in me, and I in thee, that they also may be one in us.

22And the glory which thou gavest me I have given them; that they may be one, even as we are one.

◈ The Gospel of Thomas 114 Secret Sayings of the Living Yeshua · Nag Hammadi · Egypt · c. 340 CE

These are the secret sayings which the living Yeshua spoke and which Didymos Judas Thomas wrote down.

·1· And he said, "Whoever finds the interpretation of these sayings will not experience death."
·2· Yeshua said, "Let him who seeks continue seeking until he finds. When he finds, he will become troubled. When he becomes troubled, he will be astonished, and he will rule over the All."
·3· Yeshua said, "If those who lead you say to you, 'See, the Kingdom is in the sky,' then the birds of the sky will precede you. If they say to you, 'It is in the sea,' then the fish will precede you. Rather, the Kingdom is inside of you, and it is outside of you."
·5· Yeshua said, "Recognize what is in your sight, and that which is hidden from you will become plain to you. For there is nothing hidden which will not become manifest."
·8· "The Kingdom of the Father is like a man who had a flock of fish. After laboring for a great while, he found one large and good fish. That wise fisherman threw all the small fish back into the sea and chose the large fish without difficulty."
·13· Yeshua said to his disciples, "Compare me to someone and tell me whom I am like." Shimeon Kefa said to him, "You are like a righteous angel." Matthew said to him, "You are like a wise philosopher." Thomas said to him, "Master, my mouth is wholly incapable of saying whom you are like." Yeshua said, "I am not your master. Because you have drunk, you have become intoxicated from the bubbling spring which I have measured out."
·17· Yeshua said, "I shall give you what no eye has seen and what no ear has heard and what no hand has touched and what has never occurred to the human mind."
·22· Yeshua said to them, "When you make the two one, and when you make the inside like the outside and the outside like the inside, and the above like the below, and when you make the male and the female one and the same — then will you enter the Kingdom."
·24· His disciples said, "Show us the place where you are, since it is necessary for us to seek it." He said to them, "Whoever has ears, let him hear. There is light within a man of light, and he lights up the whole world. If he does not shine, he is darkness."
·77· Yeshua said, "I am the light that is above them all. I am the All; the All came forth from me and the All attained to me. Split a piece of wood, and I am there. Lift up the stone, and you will find me there."
·82· Yeshua said, "He who is near me is near the fire, and he who is far from me is far from the Kingdom."
·108· Yeshua said, "He who will drink from my mouth will become like me. I myself shall become he, and the things that are hidden will be revealed to him."
·113· His disciples said to him, "When will the Kingdom come?" Yeshua said, "It will not come by waiting for it. It will not be a matter of saying 'Here it is' or 'There it is.' Rather, the Kingdom of the Father is spread out upon the earth, and men do not see it."
·114· Yeshua said, "Whoever drinks from my mouth will become as I am, and I myself will become that person, and the hidden things will be revealed to that person."

✝ The Gospel of the Holy Twelve Selected Lections · The Compassionate Christ
·I· Lection I The Pre-Existence of the Christ-Soul

1In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with Jah, and the Word was Jah, the same was in the beginning with Jah.

2And the Word was manifested in flesh, and we beheld His glory, glory as of the only-begotten of the Father, full of grace and truth.

3All things were made by the Word, and without the Word was not anything made that was made. In the Word was life, and the life was the light of all. And the light shineth in the darkness, and the darkness received it not.

·VI· Lection VI Yeshua Teaches Compassion for All Creatures

1And one of the scribes came to him, and heard them reasoning together, and perceiving that he had answered them well, asked him: Which is the first commandment of all?

2And Yeshua answered him: The first of all the commandments is this: Hear, O Israel; the Lord our Jah is One. And thou shalt love Jah with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind, and with all thy strength: this is the first commandment. And the second is like, namely this: Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself — and this includes every creature — for there is none other commandment greater than these.

5And Yeshua said unto him: I have come to end the sacrifices and the feasts of blood, and if ye cease not offering and eating of flesh and blood, the wrath of Jah shall not cease from you, even as it came to your fathers in the wilderness, who lusted for flesh, and they ate until it came out at their nostrils, and became loathsome unto them, and those who ate of the flesh, the plague consumed them.

·XIII· Lection XIII The Final Blessing

1After these things Yeshua gathered together his disciples and said unto them: Go ye into all the world and preach the Gospel to every creature. Baptize them with water and the Spirit, teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you. And lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.

2And these signs shall follow them that believe: In my name shall they cast out evil; they shall speak with new tongues; they shall take up serpents; if they eat any deadly thing it shall not hurt them; they shall lay hands on the sick and they shall recover.

3And they went forth and preached everywhere, Jah working with them, and confirming the word with signs following. And the Ruach Ha'Kodesh of Truth, the Comforter, taught them all things and brought all things to their remembrance. Amen.
