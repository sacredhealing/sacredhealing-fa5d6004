✦ Chapter I — The Name Is the Teaching IS · RA · EL · Three Principles Hidden in One Word · The Key That Opens Everything

1:1The name Israel is not a name. It is a formula. It is a teaching encoded in three syllables by those who knew that the children of this covenant would one day forget who they were — and that when they were ready to remember, the Name itself would tell them everything they needed to know.

1:2IS. RA. EL. Say it slowly. Let each syllable land. IS — the Divine Mother, the throne, the feminine creative principle, the restorer of the scattered. RA — the solar light, the masculine creative principle, the eye of the Most High that sees all and illuminates all. EL — the God intelligence, the divine presence, the Almighty who holds the other two in sovereign unity. Three principles. One name. One people. One mission.

1:3Jah does not give names without meaning. When the angel of Jah appeared to Hagar in the wilderness and named her unborn son Ishmael — El hears — the Name carried the promise. When Jah renamed Abram to Abraham — father of many nations — the Name carried the covenant. When Jacob wrestled at Peniel and limped into the dawn with a new name, Israel, that Name carried the entire architecture of what his descendants would be called to do in the earth. Read the Name. The Name is the instruction.

1:4Genesis 32:28. The angel says: Thy name shall be called no more Jacob, but Israel: for as a prince hast thou power with Jah and with men, and hast prevailed. A prince hast thou power. IS-RA-EL. The prince of the divine feminine, the solar light, and the God intelligence. This is what Jacob became. This is what his children are called to become. Not a tribe. Not a race. A state of being. A level of consciousness. A covenant with the three-fold nature of the Most High.

✦ Chapter II — IS · The Divine Mother · Aset · Throne · Isis The Restorer of the Scattered · She Who Gathers the Bones · Zion Is the Mother · The Woman Clothed with the Sun

2:1Before there was a Hebrew word for God there was a Kemetic understanding. The people who built the pyramids — who looked at the night sky and mapped it in stone — who preserved the knowledge of creation in temples that still stand four thousand years after the empires that tried to destroy them have turned to dust — those people knew something. They called the Divine Mother Aset. The Greeks who came later called her Isis. The name IS in Israel is that same divine feminine principle — ancient, eternal, and African.

2:2Aset means throne. The throne is the source of all legitimate authority. No king rules without the blessing of the throne. No covenant stands without the feminine principle to hold it. When Solomon built the Temple he placed the Ark — the throne of Jah — in the Holy of Holies. When Menelik carried the Ark to Ethiopia it went to its natural home — because Ethiopia is the IS, the throne, the mother, the feminine principle that has held the covenant while the rest of the world let it fall.

2:3In the Kemetic tradition, Aset gathered the scattered body of Osiris. He had been killed by Set — the principle of chaos, division, and destruction. His body was cut into fourteen pieces and scattered across the earth. Aset searched. Aset gathered. Aset reassembled what had been broken. She breathed life back into what was dead. Read Ezekiel 37. Jah takes the prophet to a valley of dry bones — very many, very dry — and commands him to prophesy. And the bones come together. Bone to bone. Sinew upon bone. Flesh upon sinew. Breath from the four winds. The IS is doing what IS always does. Gathering the scattered. Restoring the broken. Breathing life into the dry bones of Israel.

2:4Revelation 12:1. And there appeared a great wonder in heaven — a woman clothed with the sun, and the moon under her feet, and upon her head a crown of twelve stars. This is IS. This is Aset. This is Zion. This is Ethiopia. This is the Divine Mother of all Israel — clothed with the solar light of RA, standing on the reflected light of the moon, crowned with the twelve tribes. She cries out in birth. She is persecuted by the dragon. She is given wings like an eagle and flies into the wilderness where Jah feeds her. The entire story of Israel encoded in one vision of the Divine Mother.

2:5The Queen of Sheba — Makeda — is IS made flesh. She heard the wisdom of Solomon and came from the ends of the earth to sit at his feet. Yeshua says in Matthew 12:42: The queen of the south shall rise up in the judgement with this generation, and shall condemn it: for she came from the uttermost parts of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and, behold, a greater than Solomon is here. The queen of the south shall rise up in judgement. The IS — the divine feminine throne of Ethiopia — rises in the last days to judge those who had wisdom handed to them and threw it away.

The divine feminine holds the divine light within the divine intelligence. This is the nature of Israel.

Malachi is the last word of the prophets before four hundred years of silence and then the voice of John in the wilderness crying: Prepare ye the way of the Lord.

3:3Yeshua said: I am the light of the world. He said: I am the way, the truth, and the life. He said: Before Abraham was, I am. The I AM — Ehyeh Asher Ehyeh — the Name Jah gave to Moses at the burning bush — is the same solar consciousness, the same RA principle that has been present from before creation. The elect in the light of eternal life. The RA principle — the solar consciousness — is the inheritance of the righteous. It is what Israel is called to carry in the earth.

3:5Haile Selassie I bore the title Light of the World — Birhan Alem in Amharic. Not as a metaphor. As a statement of what the throne of Ethiopia carries. The solar lineage of Solomon and Makeda — the IS and the RA united in that great union three thousand years ago — produced a royal line that carries the solar principle in its blood, confirmed in its title, expressed in its mission. When Haile Selassie I walked into Addis Ababa on 5 May 1941 after the Italians had been driven out, the people lined the streets and wept. The sun had come back. The RA had returned to Ethiopia. The Light of the World had not been extinguished.

✦ Chapter IV — ELOHIM · The Plural God · The Divine Council · The Assembly of El Psalm 82 · The Sons of God · The Watchers · The Neteru · What the Ethiopian Canon Preserved

Elohim takes the im suffix which in Hebrew makes a noun plural, like cherubim, seraphim. The first word used for God in the entire Bible is a plural word. The Western church spent centuries explaining this away. The Ethiopian tradition never needed to explain it away because the Ethiopian canon — which contains 1 Enoch, Jubilees, and the Kebra Nagast — always understood that the divine council is real.

4:2Psalm 82:1. Jah standeth in the congregation of El; he judgeth among the gods. Jah — the Most High — stands in the assembly of the divine council and judges. There are other gods. Lesser powers. Beings of great authority. And Jah judges them. Verse 6: I have said, Ye are gods; and all of you are children of the most High. But ye shall die like men, and fall like one of the princes. Yeshua himself quotes this in John 10:34 when the Pharisees accuse him of blasphemy for claiming to be the Son of Jah. He says: Is it not written in your law, I said, Ye are gods? This is the scripture. Read it. The divine council is in the scripture.

4:31 Enoch Chapter 6. And it came to pass when the children of men had multiplied that in those days were born unto them beautiful and comely daughters. And the angels, the children of the heaven, saw and lusted after them, and said to one another: Come, let us choose wives from among the children of men and beget us children. And the earth cried out against them.

Because if the congregation understands that the divine council is real — that there are powers and principalities who have authority over nations — that Babylon is not just a city but a spiritual power — that the chains Jude 1:6 describes for the fallen angels are literal — then the congregation becomes ungovernable. You cannot control people who understand the true nature of the invisible world.

Horus — the principle of the restored son, the rightful heir. Set — the principle of chaos and usurpation. Ma'at — the principle of divine order, truth, and balance. 4:6EL-O-HIM. El — the supreme intelligence. O — the circle, the zero point, the void from which all creation emerges, the Ain Soph of the Kabbalists, the Amen of the Egyptians, the Om of the Vedic tradition. Him — the breath, the ruach, the spirit that hovered over the face of the waters in Genesis 1:2. When you say Elohim you are speaking the formula of creation. The intelligence, the void, and the breath. This is what called all things into being. And Israel — IS-RA-EL — is the people assigned to carry that creative formula in the earth. To embody IS. To channel RA. To speak EL into every situation they enter.

✦ Chapter VI — Israel Is Not a Race · Israel Is a Consciousness Jacob Becomes Israel · The Wrestling at Peniel · The Wound That Changes Everything · Who Can Carry the Name

6:1Jacob was born a deceiver. His name means supplanter — one who grabs the heel, one who trips up those ahead of him. He stole the birthright from Esau. He deceived his blind father Isaac into giving him the blessing. He fled. He ran. He built a family in exile. He accumulated wealth through cunning. He was gifted. He was intelligent. He was chosen. And he was still Jacob — still the one who schemes, who manipulates, who controls. Until the night at the ford of Jabbok. Until he was left alone. Until something came in the dark and wrestled him until the breaking of day.

6:2Genesis 32:24-25. And Jacob was left alone; and there wrestled a man with him until the breaking of the day. And when he saw that he prevailed not against him, he touched the hollow of his thigh; and the hollow of Jacob's thigh was out of joint, as he wrestled with him. The hip socket dislocated. The wound of transformation. Jacob cannot walk the same way again. The scheming gait — the forward lean, the calculated stride of the man who always has a plan — is broken. He will limp. He will be slower. He will be less certain of himself. But he will have the name. He will have Israel.

6:3What did Jacob wrestle? The angel says: thou hast power with Jah and with men. The rabbis say it was an angel. The mystics say it was his own shadow — the part of himself he had never faced, the part that did not want to return to Esau, the part that was still afraid, still the supplanter. Whatever it was, Jacob refused to let go until it blessed him. That is the IS in action — the feminine principle of determination, of holding on through the darkest part of the night, of refusing to release what you have wrestled down until the blessing comes. And from that refusal, Israel is born.

6:4Israel is what you become when you have wrestled with Jah and survived. It is not given by birth. It is earned in the dark. Every person who has gone through the furnace of affliction — who has been scattered, sold, enslaved, colonised, stripped of their name, their language, their ancestors, their land — and who has held on, who has refused to release the blessing, who has said to the darkness I will not let you go until you bless me — that person is demonstrating the IS-RA-EL consciousness. The children of Africa who survived the Middle Passage and still sang to Jah — they had wrestled. They had survived. They carried the name in their spirit even when Babylon had taken their name from their tongue.

6:5Romans 9:6. For they are not all Israel, which are of Israel. Paul — himself a Hebrew of Hebrews, of the tribe of Benjamin — understands this. Being born into the physical lineage of Israel is not the same as carrying the Israel consciousness. And carrying the Israel consciousness is not limited to those born into the physical lineage. Galatians 3:29. And if ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise. The covenant of Abraham — the IS-RA-EL covenant — is available to every human being who will wrestle with Jah, survive the night, and refuse to walk away without the blessing.

6:6This is not a contradiction of the physical heritage of the Black Israelites. It is the completion of it. The physical lineage is real. The covenant with the specific people descended from Abraham, Isaac and Jacob is real. The prophecies of scattering and restoration addressed to that specific people are real and are being fulfilled now. And within that reality, the IS-RA-EL consciousness — the union of the divine feminine, the solar light, and the God intelligence — is the destination that all of that history is pointing toward. The physical restoration of Israel is the container. The IS-RA-EL consciousness is what fills it. Both are needed. Neither is sufficient without the other.
