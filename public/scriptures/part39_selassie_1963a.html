The United Nations Address

New York · October 4, 1963 · The War Speech

Haile Selassie I returned to the United Nations on October 4, 1963 — twenty-seven years after his historic 1936 League of Nations address. This speech became the foundation of Bob Marley's song War, recorded in 1976 on the Rastaman Vibration album. The central passages were taken verbatim and set to music. This is the complete speech.
