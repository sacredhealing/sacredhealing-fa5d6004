Sinodos · Book of the Covenant · Clement · Didascalia

ሲኖዶስ · The Apostolic Constitution of the Ethiopian Church

These four books form the broader canon of the Ethiopian Orthodox Bible — the Haymanot — making up the full count of 88 sacred books. The Sinodos contains the apostolic canons and church ordinances attributed to the Apostles themselves. The Book of the Covenant preserves the covenant law. The Epistle of Clement, written by the third bishop of Rome, was considered scripture by the earliest church. Together they form the constitutional foundation of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church — the oldest continuously functioning Christian institution on earth.

✦ Sinodos · Part One The Apostolic Canons · The Order of the Church

1These are the canons of the holy Apostles which they established through the Holy Spirit concerning the order of the Church, the appointment of bishops, priests, and deacons, the celebration of the holy mysteries, and the conduct of the faithful.

2A bishop must be blameless, the husband of one wife, vigilant, sober, of good behaviour, given to hospitality, apt to teach; not given to wine, not violent, not greedy of filthy lucre; but patient, not a brawler, not covetous; one that ruleth well his own house.

3Let the bishop be ordained by two or three bishops. Let the priest be ordained by one bishop. Let the deacon be ordained by the bishop. Let the reader be appointed by the bishop.

7Let no bishop, priest, or deacon undertake worldly business. Otherwise let him be deposed. For a soldier of Jah must not entangle himself with the affairs of this life that he may please him who hath chosen him to be a soldier.

11If any bishop or priest neglects the clergy or the people, and does not instruct them in the way of godliness, let him be separated. And if he continues in his negligence and idleness, let him be deposed.

15Honour the widows, the orphans, and the poor. For Jah has made them His own. The bishop who neglects the poor neglects Jah Himself. He who feeds the hungry feeds Yeshua. He who clothes the naked clothes Yeshua. For as Yeshua said — inasmuch as you do it unto the least of these my brethren, you do it unto me.

38Let the faithful pray facing the East. For as lightning comes from the East and shines to the West, so shall the coming of the Son of Man be. From the East came the light of creation. From the East shall come the light of the resurrection.

41On the first day of the week, which is the Lord's Day, let all assemble in the church with joy and gladness. Praise Jah. Read the scriptures. Break the bread. Give thanks. For this is the day on which Jah began creation, and on which Yeshua rose from the dead.

✦ Book of the Covenant · Complete The Covenant Renewed · The Law of Jah Written on the Heart

1:1This is the Book of the Covenant which Jah established with His people through His servant Moses, and which He renewed through Yeshua His Son, and which He confirms through His Spirit in the hearts of all who believe.

1:2Jah said: I am your Jah. You shall have no other gods before me. You shall not make for yourselves an idol. You shall not take my name in vain. Keep my Sabbath holy. Honour your father and mother. Do not kill. Do not commit adultery. Do not steal. Do not bear false witness. Do not covet.

1:4These ten words are the foundation of all law and all righteousness. But greater than the written law is the law of love. For he who loves Jah with all his heart and his neighbour as himself has fulfilled all the commandments.

2:1The covenant is this: I will be your Jah and you shall be my people. I will put my law in your hearts. I will write my name on your foreheads. I will be your light by day and your fire by night. I will never leave you nor forsake you.

2:3And you shall walk in my ways. You shall do justice. You shall love mercy. You shall walk humbly before me. You shall not oppress the stranger, for you were strangers. You shall not afflict the widow and the orphan. You shall not take bribes. You shall not pervert judgment.

3:1Keep this covenant and you shall be my treasure above all peoples. You shall be to me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation. All the earth is mine — but you I have chosen to be my witnesses before all the nations of the earth.

3:5This covenant is everlasting. It was made with Abraham. It was renewed with Moses. It was sealed in the blood of Yeshua. It shall stand until the last day when Jah makes all things new and the covenant is completed in the New Jerusalem. Amen.

✦ 1 Clement · Complete The Epistle to the Corinthians · Order · Humility · Love

1The Church of Jah which sojourns in Rome, to the Church of Jah sojourning in Corinth, to them that are called and sanctified by the will of Jah, through our Lord Yeshua the Anointed: Grace unto you, and peace, from Almighty Jah through Yeshua the Anointed, be multiplied.

3All glory and enlargement was given unto you, and then was fulfilled that which was written, My beloved ate, and drank, and was enlarged, and grew fat, and kicked. Hence flowed emulation and envy, strife and sedition, persecution and disorder, war and captivity.

13Let us therefore humble ourselves, brethren, laying aside all arrogance, and conceit, and foolishness, and anger, and let us do that which is written. For the Holy Spirit saith, Let not the wise man glory in his wisdom, nor the strong man in his strength, neither let the rich man glory in his riches; but let him that glorieth glory in the Lord, to seek Him, and to do judgment and justice.

49Who can declare the bond of the love of Jah? Who is able to tell the excellency of its beauty, as it ought to be told? The height to which love exalts is unutterable. Love unites us to Jah. Love covers a multitude of sins. Love beareth all things, is long-suffering in all things. There is nothing base, nothing haughty in love. Love admits of no schisms. Love gives rise to no seditions.

50Love does all things in harmony. By love have all the elect of Jah been made perfect; without love nothing is well-pleasing to Jah. In love has the Lord taken us to Himself. On account of the love he bore us, Yeshua the Anointed our Lord gave His blood for us by the will of Jah — His flesh for our flesh, and His soul for our souls.

58But if any should disobey the things which have been said by Him through us, let them know that they will involve themselves in transgression and serious danger; but we shall be innocent of this sin. And we will ask, with instant prayer and supplication, that the Creator of all preserve intact the computed number of His elect in the whole world through His beloved servant Yeshua the Anointed, through whom He called us from darkness to light, from ignorance to the full knowledge of the glory of His name.

65The grace of our Lord Yeshua the Anointed be with you, and with all everywhere that are the called of Jah through Him, by whom be glory and honour, power and greatness, and eternal dominion, unto Him, from everlasting to everlasting. Amen.
