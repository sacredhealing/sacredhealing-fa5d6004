Job

The Suffering Servant · Where Was Thou When I Laid the Foundations?

Job is the oldest book in the Bible. A righteous man stripped of everything — his wealth, his children, his health — yet he does not curse Jah. The book of Job asks the hardest question: why do the righteous suffer? And Jah answers from the whirlwind — not with explanation, but with the overwhelming majesty of creation itself. No theological argument. Just the voice of Jah speaking from the storm.

⚖ Job · Chapter I The Trial of Job Begins

1There was a man in the land of Uz, whose name was Job; and that man was perfect and upright, and one that feared Jah, and eschewed evil.

8And Jah said unto Satan, Hast thou considered my servant Job, that there is none like him in the earth, a perfect and an upright man, one that feareth Jah, and escheweth evil?

20Then Job arose, and rent his mantle, and shaved his head, and fell down upon the ground, and worshipped,

21And said, Naked came I out of my mother's womb, and naked shall I return thither: Jah gave, and Jah hath taken away; blessed be the name of Jah.

22In all this Job sinned not, nor charged Jah foolishly.

⚖ Job · Chapter XIX I Know That My Redeemer Liveth

25For I know that my redeemer liveth, and that he shall stand at the latter day upon the earth.

26And though after my skin worms destroy this body, yet in my flesh shall I see Jah.

27Whom I shall see for myself, and mine eyes shall behold, and not another; though my reins be consumed within me.

⚖ Job · Chapters XXXVIII — XXXIX Jah Speaks from the Whirlwind

38:1Then Jah answered Job out of the whirlwind, and said,

38:2Who is this that darkeneth counsel by words without knowledge?

38:3Gird up now thy loins like a man; for I will demand of thee, and answer thou me.

38:4Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth? declare, if thou hast understanding.

38:7When the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of Jah shouted for joy?

38:31Canst thou bind the sweet influences of Pleiades, or loose the bands of Orion?

38:32Canst thou bring forth Mazzaroth in his season? or canst thou guide Arcturus with his sons?

38:33Knowest thou the ordinances of heaven? canst thou set the dominion thereof in the earth?

42:10And Jah turned the captivity of Job, when he prayed for his friends: also Jah gave Job twice as much as he had before.

42:12So Jah blessed the latter end of Job more than his beginning.
