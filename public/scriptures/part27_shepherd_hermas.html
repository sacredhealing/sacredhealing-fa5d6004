The Shepherd of Hermas

The Visions · The Commandments · The Similitudes

The Shepherd of Hermas was considered scripture by the earliest Christians — Origen, Irenaeus, Clement of Alexandria all quoted it as authoritative. It was found in the Codex Sinaiticus alongside the New Testament. Ultimately excluded from the Western canon at Carthage in 397 CE, it was retained in the Ethiopian Orthodox Bible, where it has been read as holy scripture continuously for two thousand years. It records five visions, twelve commandments, and ten parables — a rich manual of early Christian life.

🕊 The Shepherd · Vision I The First Vision of Hermas · The Lady of the Church

1He who brought me up, had sold me to one Rhoda in Rome. After many years, I met her again, and began to love her as a sister. After a certain time I saw her bathing in the river Tiber; and I gave her my hand, and led her out of the river.

2So, seeing her beauty, I reasoned in my heart, saying, Happy were I, if I had such an one to wife both in beauty and in character. I merely thought this, and nothing more.

4After a little time, while I was walking, and thinking of these things, I began to honour Jah's creation, thinking how great, and admirable, and splendid it is, how it was created by His power.

6And she said to me, Hermas, what art thou thinking about? Art thou thinking of thy sins? She asked with a smile. And I said to her, Here I am, my lady. And she said to me, Why art thou cast down, thou who art patient, and good-tempered, and always smiling?

8She said, Jah is not angry with thee, but on account of thy house, which has committed iniquity against Jah, and against their parents. And, although thou lovest thy family, thou hast not warned them, but hast suffered them to become fearfully wicked.

9But Jah is merciful, and will heal all the sins of thy household if thou correct them and they repent with their whole heart. For Jah will heal all the sins of all those who have hitherto sinned, if they repent with their whole heart.

🕊 The Shepherd · Vision II The Book from the Old Woman · Read and Proclaim

1As I was going to Cumae, and was on my way there, I besought Jah to reveal to me the vision which He had shown me through the holy Church. And in my sleep the Spirit caught me up, and led me through pathless places, through which no man could pass.

3There was an old woman who came to me holding a book, and she said: Can you declare these things to the elect of Jah? I said to her: Lady, I cannot retain so many things in my memory. Give me the book that I may copy it.

4Take it, she said, and you will return it to me. I took it, and went away into a certain part of the country, and there I copied every letter, for I found no syllable.

8Hear now, O children, what these things mean. The old woman from whom you received the book, Who is she? The Church, I said. You are right, the angel said. Why does she appear as an old woman? Because she was created before all things; on her account the world was formed.

🕊 The Shepherd · The Twelve Commandments Commandments for Holy Living · The Angel of Righteousness

Cmd 1First of all, believe that Jah is One and that He created all things and organized them, and out of nothing made all things to be, and He contains all things but He alone is uncontained.

Cmd 2He said to me: Be simple and guileless, and you will be as the children who know not the wickedness that ruins the life of men. First, speak evil of no man, and hearken not to one who speaks evil of a man. Otherwise thou too wilt be a partaker of the sin of him who speaks evil.

Cmd 3Love the truth, and let nothing but truth proceed from your mouth, so that the spirit which Jah has made to dwell in this flesh may be found truthful by every man; and thus will the Lord, who dwells in you, be glorified. For the Lord is truthful in every word, and with Him there is no falsehood.

Cmd 4Keep your flesh pure, and your heart clean, that you may receive the forgiveness of your sins from Jah. He that defileth his flesh defileth the Holy Spirit; for Jah has given His Spirit to dwell in the body of man.

Cmd 5Be patient and understanding, and you will have power over all evil deeds, and you will work all righteousness. For if you are patient, the Holy Spirit that dwells in you will be pure, not being darkened by any evil spirit.

Cmd 6There are two angels with a man — one of righteousness, and the other of iniquity. The angel of righteousness is gentle and modest, meek and peaceful. When he comes to your heart he talks to you of righteousness, purity, chastity, contentment, and of every righteous deed and glorious virtue.

Cmd 7Fear Jah alone and keep His commandments, and so doing you shall be powerful against every temptation, and every evil deed. For to fear Jah is to keep His commandments. If you keep His commandments, you will be strong in every action, and every action of yours will be incomparable.

Cmd 8Restrain yourself from every evil word, and every evil desire, and purify yourself from all vanity. And do not trust yourself in your own righteousness, lest you lead your heart astray. Jah is greater than our heart and knows all things.

Cmd 12Remove from you all evil desire, and clothe yourself in good and holy desire. For if you clothe yourself with good desire, you will hate evil desire, and you will bridle it as you wish. For evil desire is wild, and is only with difficulty tamed. For it is terrible, and by its wildness it is very costly to men.

🕊 The Shepherd · Similitude IX The Tower and the Twelve Mountains · The Church of All Nations

1After I had written down the commandments and similitudes of the Shepherd, the angel of repentance, he came to me and said: I wish to show you all things that the Holy Spirit showed you when speaking with you in the form of the Church.

17These are they who from the twelve mountains believed when they heard the preaching. And some from each of the mountains believed, and from each belief came the building up of the tower. All nations under heaven have heard and believed, and were called by the name of the Son of Jah.

29The stones that were taken from the deep and placed into the building of the tower without being cut — these are those who have suffered for the name of Jah. They are exalted above all others in the building. For they have suffered greatly and kept the commandments of Jah.

33Hear me, O children: I have brought you up in much simplicity, and innocency, and purity, on account of the mercy of Jah, who has dropped His righteousness upon you, that you might be made righteous and holy from all your iniquity and depravity.

✦ Sinodos · Part Two · The Liturgical Ordinances The Order of the Assembly · The Eucharist · The Baptism · The Laying On of Hands

When the community gathers for the breaking of bread, let the bishop give thanks first over the cup, saying: We give thanks to thee, our Father, for the holy vine of David thy servant, which thou madest known to us through Yeshua thy servant; to thee be the glory for ever. And over the broken bread say: We give thanks to thee, our Father, for the life and knowledge which thou madest known to us through Yeshua thy servant; to thee be the glory for ever.

As this broken bread was scattered over the hills, and was gathered together and became one, so let thy congregation be gathered together from the ends of the earth into thy kingdom; for thine is the glory and the power through Yeshua Christ for ever.

After ye are filled, give thanks thus: We give thanks to thee, holy Father, for thy holy name which thou didst cause to tabernacle in our hearts, and for the knowledge and faith and immortality, which thou madest known to us through Yeshua thy servant; to thee be the glory for ever. Thou, Master Almighty, didst create all things for thy name's sake; thou gavest food and drink to men for enjoyment, that they might give thanks to thee; but to us thou didst freely give spiritual food and drink and life eternal through thy servant.

Before all things we give thanks that thou art mighty; to thee be the glory for ever. Remember, Lord, thy congregation, to deliver it from all evil and to make it perfect in thy love, and gather it from the four winds, sanctified for thy kingdom which thou hast prepared for it; for thine is the power and the glory for ever. Let grace come, and let this world pass away. Hosanna to the God of David. If any one is holy, let him come; if any one is not so, let him repent. Maranatha. Amen.

Concerning baptism, baptize thus: Having first said all these things, baptize into the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit in living water. But if thou have not living water, baptize into other water; and if thou canst not in cold, in warm. But if thou have not either, pour out water thrice upon the head into the name of Father and Son and Holy Spirit. But before the baptism let the baptizer fast, and the baptized, and whoever else can; but thou shalt order the baptized to fast one or two days before.

✦ The Shepherd of Hermas · The Mandates · Commandment I — V Believe in One God · Put Away Lying · Keep Purity · The Two Angels

Mand.IFirst of all, believe that God is one, who made all things and perfected them, and made all things to be out of nothing. He alone is able to contain everything, but himself cannot be contained. Believe in him therefore, and fear him; and fearing him, exercise self-control. Keep this commandment and thou shalt cast away from thyself all wickedness, putting on every virtue of righteousness, and shalt live unto God, if thou keep this commandment.

Mand.IIIAgain he saith to me: Love truth, and let nothing but truth proceed from thy mouth, that the spirit which God made to dwell in this flesh may be found true by all men; and thus shall the Lord, who dwelleth in thee, be glorified; for the Lord is true in every word, and with him there is no falsehood. They therefore that lie set the Lord at naught, and become robbers of the Lord, for they receive not the deposit which they received of him. For they received of him a spirit free from lies. If they return this as a lying spirit, they have defiled the commandment of the Lord and have become robbers.

Mand.VII commanded thee in the first commandment to keep faith and fear and self-control. Yes, sir, said I. But now I wish to explain to thee their powers also, that thou mayest understand what is the power and effect of each one of them. For their effects are two-fold. They relate then to the righteous and to the unrighteous. Trust thou then the righteous, but trust not the unrighteous. For righteousness hath a straight and level path, but unrighteousness a crooked one. But walk thou in the straight and level path, and leave the crooked one alone.

Mand.XPut away grief from thyself, for she is the sister of double-mindedness and of angry temper. How, sir, is she the sister of these? For grief and double-mindedness and angry temper are sister spirits. But put away grief and afflict not the Holy Spirit that dwelleth in thee, lest he intercede against thee. For the Spirit of God that was given to this flesh endureth neither grief nor distress. Therefore put on cheerfulness, which hath favour with Jah always, and is acceptable to him, and rejoice in it. For every cheerful man doeth good, and mindeth good things, and despiseth grief.

Mand.XII:3Thou seest therefore, said he, that faith is from above, from the Lord, and hath great power; but double-mindedness is an earthly spirit from the devil, and hath no power. Serve therefore the faith which hath power, and keep aloof from the double-mindedness which hath no power; and thou shalt live unto God; yea, and all those shall live unto God who are of this mind.
