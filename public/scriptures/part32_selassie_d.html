Haile Selassie 1 · Part 4

The Faith of the Emperor · His Words on Jah · The Bible as Foundation

Throughout his life Haile Selassie 1 spoke consistently and deeply about his personal faith — about the Bible as the foundation of his rule, about Jah as the source of all authority, about prayer as the sustaining force of his life. These are his own words, drawn from speeches, interviews, and writings across his long reign. They reveal a man whose kingship was inseparable from his devotion — a ruler who understood himself first as a servant of Jah, and only secondly as Emperor of Ethiopia.

♛ His Words on Faith · The Bible as Foundation The Source of All Authority · Prayer as the Sustaining Force

Throughout my life 1 have set myself one rule: to begin every important act with prayer, asking Jah to guide me that my deeds may be worthy of the trust placed in me by my people and by my Creator. The Bible is not simply a book among other books. For me it is the living word of the living God — the foundation upon which all true authority rests, and the mirror in which every ruler must examine himself. A king who does not govern according to the law of Jah has no authority that is worthy of the name.

1 have often said, and 1 repeat: the Bible is the source of all wisdom. In it are contained all the principles of righteous governance, all the insights of human nature, all the aspirations of the soul. No one who has read it carefully and with an open heart can doubt that it is of divine origin. 1 have read it from my childhood, and 1 read it still. It speaks to me differently in every season of life — more deeply in adversity than in prosperity, more profoundly in old age than in youth.

Prayer is not a formula. It is a living conversation with Jah. 1 do not pray because 1 am required to pray. 1 pray because without prayer 1 would be nothing — a man trying to govern by his own wisdom, which is vanity. With prayer, even the smallest act can be aligned with the divine will. Without it, even the greatest achievement is empty.

My faith has been tested many times. In 1936 when 1 stood before the League of Nations and was ignored by the world, 1 did not lose faith in Jah — only in men. And the history that followed vindicated what 1 said then. Jah's justice is slow by human reckoning, but it is certain. What is built without Jah falls. What is built with Jah endures.

The Ethiopian Orthodox Church has been the guardian of the faith of this nation for sixteen hundred years. It is older than most of the nations that presume to lecture us about civilization. The Ark of the Covenant rests in Aksum. The line of Solomon has never been broken. Ethiopia has been Christian before Europe was fully converted. Our faith is not a foreign import — it is the marrow of our bones, the heartbeat of our history.

To the young people of Ethiopia and of all Africa 1 say: do not abandon the faith of your fathers. The wisdom of the ancient scriptures is not outdated. It is more needed today than it has ever been. A man without faith is a tree without roots — he may stand in fair weather, but the first storm lays him flat. A nation without faith is an empire of sand.

♛ His Words on Unity · Peace · Human Brotherhood One Africa · The Dignity of Man · Love as the Foundation

Throughout history it has been the inaction of those who could have acted, the indifference of those who should have known better, the silence of the voice of justice when it mattered most, that has made it possible for evil to triumph. The pages of history are written only in those moments when people have acted in concert to prevent impending tragedies.

We must become something we have never been — and for which our education and experience and environment have poorly prepared us. We must be bigger than we have ever been. Braver, bigger in spirit, and clearer in perspective. We must be members of a new race, overcoming petty prejudice, owing our ultimate allegiance not to nations but to our fellow men within the human community.

Love of country is a noble sentiment. But love of humanity is nobler still. The man who loves only his own nation and despises others has understood nothing of what the great prophets taught. Jah created one human family. Every barrier we erect between one part of that family and another is a barrier we erect against Jah himself.

Africa must be united. Not because unity is convenient — but because division is a wound that has been inflicted on us from outside, and we must heal it from within. The Organization of African Unity was founded on this principle: that African peoples, whatever their language, their religion, their history, share one destiny. We are, as Jah made us, one people.

Peace is not merely the absence of war. It is the presence of justice. A peace that is maintained by force is not peace — it is occupation. A peace that is maintained by the suppression of legitimate aspirations is not peace — it is humiliation. True peace can only be built on the foundation of justice, equality, and mutual respect. This is what the Charter of the United Nations was supposed to enshrine. This is what we must hold each other accountable to uphold.

1 am an old man. 1 have seen much. 1 have seen the world at its worst — in 1935, when a modern army with poison gas fell upon my defenceless people. And 1 have seen the world at its best — when the nations of Africa gathered in Addis Ababa in 1963 and pledged themselves to each other's liberation and dignity. What 1 know with certainty is this: Jah is not mocked. Injustice does not endure. Truth rises. Love is the final authority. The rest is commentary.
