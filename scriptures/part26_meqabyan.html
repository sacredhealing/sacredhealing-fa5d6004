መቃብያን · The Ethiopian Books of the Maccabees

The three books of Meqabyan are among the most unique texts in all of world scripture — found only in the Ethiopian Orthodox Bible. They are entirely distinct from the Greek Books of Maccabees known in Catholic and Orthodox canons. These are Ethiopian compositions, written in Ge'ez, telling of righteous warriors who fought for Jah against idolatry and oppression. They speak with the voice of ancient Ethiopia — fierce, prophetic, uncompromising in their love for Jah and hatred of false worship.

♛ 1 Meqabyan · Chapters I — IV The Righteous Warriors of Jah · Against the Idolaters

1:1In the days when the kings of the nations had risen against the people of Jah and had commanded them to worship idols of wood and stone and had turned them from the covenant of their fathers, there arose men of valour from the tribe of Israel who feared Jah with all their hearts.

1:2And Meqabyan their chief said unto them, Let us not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul. Rather let us fear Jah alone who has power over body and soul alike.

1:3For what profit is it to a man if he gains the whole world but loses his portion in Jah? Better is death with righteousness than life with iniquity.

1:4And the men of valour answered him saying, We will stand with thee, O Meqabyan, and we will not bow to the idols of the nations nor will we eat of the food of the unclean nor defile our bodies which Jah has made in His image.

1:8Then the king sent soldiers against them, but Jah was with Meqabyan and his men, and fire came down from heaven and drove the soldiers back, and the righteous were not harmed.

2:1And Meqabyan prayed before Jah saying, O Jah of our fathers Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, look upon the affliction of thy people. We have not forsaken thee. We have not bowed to the idols. We have kept thy covenant in our hearts.

2:2Though they cast us into prison and though they slay our brethren before our eyes, yet will we praise thee. Though they burn our houses and scatter our children, yet will we call upon thy name.

2:3For thou art Jah and beside thee there is none other. Thou art the strength of the weak and the shield of the righteous and the hope of all who call upon thee.

2:10And Jah heard the prayer of Meqabyan and sent His angel to stand with them in the battle, and the face of the angel shone like the sun, and the enemies of Jah fled before him.

3:5And Meqabyan said to the priests of the idols, Your gods are nothing. They are the work of men's hands — they have eyes and see not, ears and hear not, mouths and speak not. But Jah who made heaven and earth — He sees and He hears and He speaks to the heart of man.

3:6Repent therefore and turn from your vanity to the living Jah, that He may have mercy on you before the day of His judgment comes upon all the earth.

4:1And in those days Meqabyan fell sick unto death, and he gathered his sons around him and said, I am going to my fathers. Fear not, for Jah will not forsake you. Keep His covenant and walk in His ways and He will be your shield as He has been mine.

4:2And remember that the righteous do not truly die — they sleep in Jah and they shall rise again in the last day when He calls all His servants by name.

4:3And Meqabyan breathed his last and his sons buried him with great honour. And the people mourned for him forty days. And after him rose up his sons who walked in his ways and Jah was with them.

♛ 2 Meqabyan · Complete The Resurrection of the Dead · The Day of Jah

1:1These are the words of the second book of Meqabyan concerning the resurrection and the judgment and the mercy of Jah upon all who have kept His covenant.

1:5And Jah spoke saying, I do not desire the death of the wicked. I desire that the wicked turn from his evil way and live. Therefore I send my prophets and my angels and my word to call all men back to me.

1:9Know therefore that there is a resurrection of the dead. The body sleeps in the earth but the soul lives before Jah. And in the last day Jah will call all souls and clothe them again with bodies and judge every man according to his works.

2:1Blessed are those who die in Jah, for they shall rest from their labours and their works shall follow them.

2:4And the righteous shall shine like the stars of heaven and like the sun in the kingdom of Jah, and they shall dwell in light without end and sorrow shall flee from them.

2:7And the wicked shall be as smoke that is driven away. They shall seek Jah and not find Him because they sought Him not when He was near.

3:1Therefore while you live, seek Jah. While you breathe, praise Him. For this life is a shadow and a mist — only Jah endures. Build your house upon His word and no storm shall overthrow it.

3:6And on the great day of Jah the mountains shall melt and the seas shall flee and every eye shall behold the glory of the Almighty, and every knee shall bow and every tongue confess that Jah alone is Jah over all the earth.

♛ 3 Meqabyan · Complete Wisdom · The Fear of Jah · The Final Teaching

1:1Hear O children of Israel and all ye nations of the earth, for these are the words of wisdom and understanding which Jah has given to his servants for the instruction of all generations.

1:3The fear of Jah is the beginning of wisdom. He who does not fear Jah does not truly know himself, for man is made in the image of Jah and his soul shall return to Jah who gave it.

1:7Do not oppress the poor, for Jah hears the cry of the poor before the cry of kings. Do not take the bread from the widow's table. Do not send the orphan away empty-handed. For these are the children of Jah's covenant and He will judge all men by how they treated the least among them.

2:1Remember that you too were strangers and that Jah was merciful to you. Therefore be merciful. Remember that you too were weak and that Jah gave you strength. Therefore give strength to the weak.

2:5Love your brother as yourself and your neighbour as your own kin. For in the last day Jah will ask of you not how much you prayed but how much you loved. Not how many offerings you brought to the altar but how many of His children you lifted from the dust.

3:1And now I charge you before Jah and before His holy angels: walk in His ways. Keep His sabbaths. Feed the hungry. Clothe the naked. Visit the imprisoned. Heal the sick. And love Jah thy Jah with all thy heart and with all thy soul and with all thy strength.

3:4For this is the whole of the law and the covenant and the testimony. This is the word that was written on the heavenly tablets before the world was made. This is the word that shall stand when all else has passed away. Amen. Praise be to Jah.

✦ 1 Meqabyan · Chapters V — XII The Faith of the Martyrs · Jah Is My Strength · The Righteous Shall Prevail

5:1And Meqabyan said unto his sons: Hear me, O my sons, and give ear to the words of my mouth. The fear of Jah is the beginning of all wisdom, and to depart from evil is understanding. Happy is the man who finds wisdom and the man who gains understanding, for the gain from her is better than gain from silver and her profit better than gold.

5:8She is more precious than jewels, and nothing you desire can compare with her. Long life is in her right hand; in her left hand are riches and honour. Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are peace. She is a tree of life to those who lay hold of her; those who hold her fast are called blessed.

6:3And Meqabyan said: I have been young, and now I am old; yet I have not seen the righteous forsaken or his children begging for bread. The righteous man is generous and lends, and his children become a blessing. Depart from evil and do good, so you shall abide forever. For Jah loves justice; he will not forsake his faithful ones. The righteous shall be preserved forever, but the children of the wicked shall be cut off.

7:1Three sons there were of Meqabyan — Meqabyan the elder, and Sareyaqos, and Pantelewon — and they were mighty men of valour and feared Jah with all their heart. And the king sent for them and commanded them to bow before the idols of gold and silver which he had set up. But they refused and said: We will bow before no god but Jah the Most High, who made the heavens and the earth.

7:6And the three brothers stood before the king and said: Do with us as you will, O king, for we are in your power in this life. But know this: Jah lives, and he shall raise up the dead. And those who have kept his commandments shall inherit eternal life, but those who have transgressed shall receive the recompense of their deeds. Therefore we will not bow to your idols, though you throw us into the fire.

8:4And they were cast into the furnace of fire, but the fire had no power over them, and the hair of their heads was not singed, and their garments were not affected, and no smell of fire came upon them. For an angel of Jah came down into the furnace and was with them, and the fire was as a cool dew upon them. And the king marvelled and said: Did we not cast three men bound into the fire? Look, I see four men walking in the fire unbound, and the appearance of the fourth is like a son of the gods.

9:1And Meqabyan said to his sons before he died: I am going the way of all flesh. But you shall not fear, for Jah is with you. Keep his commandments and walk in his ways, and he will bless you in this life and in the life to come. Do not be afraid of those who kill the body; they cannot kill the soul. But fear him who is able to destroy both soul and body. For is not the life more than food and the body more than clothing?

12:7And at the last day, Jah shall raise up the dead from their graves, and they shall stand before him — every man according to his works. Those who have done good shall go into eternal life, and those who have done evil shall go into eternal shame. But the righteous shall shine like the sun in the kingdom of their Father. He who has ears, let him hear.

✦ 2 Meqabyan · The Victory of Faith Though the Earth Be Removed · Jah Is Our Refuge · The Mountain of the Lord

2:1Blessed are the people who know Jah their strength, who walk in the light of his face. In his name they rejoice all the day, and in his righteousness they are exalted. For Jah is their glory and the lifter of their head, their shield and their strength in the day of trouble.

2:8The men of faith said among themselves: We have overcome by the word of our testimony. For we did not love our lives so much as to shrink from death. And they stood in the fire and in the sword and in the face of the king, and they said: Jah is our God. He has delivered us from the hand of our enemies in times past and he will deliver us again.

3:4And the victory of the faithful is this: that the life which Jah gives cannot be taken away by men. They may take the body — but the spirit returns to Jah who gave it. And at the last day, the body also shall be restored, for Jah is the resurrection and the life. This is the faith of Meqabyan and his sons. This is the faith of Ethiopia. This is the faith that has sustained this people through every generation of trial and suffering: Jah lives. The righteous shall prevail. The dead shall rise.
