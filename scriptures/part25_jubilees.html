The Book of Jubilees

ኩፋሌ · The Little Genesis · The Apocalypse of Moses

The Book of Jubilees is one of the most important books preserved exclusively in the Ethiopian Orthodox canon. Written in the second century BCE, it retells the history of Genesis and Exodus in the framework of Jubilee cycles — periods of 49 years. It reveals details hidden in Genesis: the names of women, the exact dating of creation, the heavenly tablets from which all history is written. Jubilees was found among the Dead Sea Scrolls, confirming its extreme antiquity. It is canonical only in Ethiopia and Eritrea.

☥ Jubilees · Chapter I Jah Speaks to Moses on Sinai · The Heavenly Tablets

1This is the history of the division of the days of the law and of the testimony, of the events of the years, of their year weeks, of their Jubilees throughout all the years of the world, as Jah spake to Moses on Mount Sinai when he went up to receive the tables of the law and of the commandment, according to the voice of Jah as he said unto him, Go up to the top of the Mount.

2And it came to pass in the first year of the exodus of the children of Israel out of Egypt, in the third month, on the sixteenth day of the month, that Jah spake to Moses, saying: Come up to Me on the Mount, and I will give thee two tables of stone of the law and of the commandment, which I have written, that thou mayst teach them.

4And Moses went up into the mount of Jah, and the glory of Jah abode on Mount Sinai, and a cloud overshadowed it six days.

5And He called to Moses on the seventh day out of the midst of the cloud, and the appearance of the glory of Jah was like a flaming fire on the top of the mount.

6And Moses was on the Mount forty days and forty nights, and Jah taught him the earlier and the later history of the division of all the days of the law and of the testimony.

26And do thou write down for thyself all these words which I declare unto thee this day, for I know their contrariness and their evil inclinations and their stiffneckedness, and they will not be obedient till they confess their own sin and the sin of their fathers.

27And after this they will turn to Me from amongst the Gentiles with all their heart and with all their soul and with all their strength, and I will gather them from amongst all the Gentiles, and they will seek me, so that I shall be found of them when they seek me with all their heart and with all their soul.

28And I will disclose to them abounding peace with righteousness, and I will remove them the plant of uprightness, with all My heart and with all My soul, and they shall be for a blessing and not for a curse, and they shall be the head and not the tail.

29And I will build My sanctuary in their midst, and I will dwell with them, and I will be their Jah and they shall be My true and righteous people.

☥ Jubilees · Chapter II The Seven Days of Creation · The Sabbath Blessed

1And the angel of the presence spake to Moses according to the word of Jah, saying: Write the complete history of the creation, how in six days Jah finished all His works and all that He created, and kept Sabbath on the seventh day and hallowed it for all ages, and appointed it as a sign for all His works.

2For on the first day He created the heavens which are above and the earth and the waters and all the spirits which serve before him — the angels of the presence, and the angels of sanctification, and the angels of the spirit of fire and the angels of the spirit of the winds, and the angels of the spirit of the clouds, and of darkness, and of snow and of hail and of hoar frost, and the angels of the voices and of the thunder and of the lightning, and the angels of the spirits of cold and of heat, and of winter and of spring and of autumn and of summer, and of all the spirits of his creatures which are in the heavens and on the earth.

17And He gave us a great sign, the Sabbath day, that we should work six days, but keep Sabbath on the seventh day from all work.

18And all the angels of the presence, and all the angels of sanctification, these two great classes — He hath bidden us to keep the Sabbath with Him in heaven and on earth.

19And He said unto us: Behold, I will separate unto Myself a people from among all the peoples, and these shall keep the Sabbath day, and I will sanctify them unto Myself as My people, and will bless them; as I have sanctified the Sabbath day and do sanctify it unto Myself, even so will I bless them, and they shall be My people and I will be their Jah.

20And I have chosen the seed of Jacob from amongst all that I have seen, and have written him down as My first-born son, and have sanctified him unto Myself for ever and ever; and I will teach them the Sabbath day, that they may keep Sabbath thereon from all work.

☥ Jubilees · Chapter III · IV · VI Eden Restored · The Covenant of Noah · The Calendar of Heaven

3:9And Adam and his wife went forth from the Garden of Eden, and they dwelt in the land of Elda, in the land of their creation.

3:27And on that day was closed the mouth of all beasts, and of cattle, and of birds, and of whatever walks, and of whatever moves, so that they could no longer speak: for they had all spoken one with another with one lip and with one tongue.

4:26And he was the first among men that are born on earth who learnt writing and knowledge and wisdom and who wrote down the signs of heaven according to the order of their months in a book, that men might know the seasons of the years according to the order of their separate months.

6:17For this reason it is ordained and written on the heavenly tablets, that they should celebrate the feast of weeks in this month once a year, to renew the covenant every year.

6:32There will be those who will assuredly make observations of the moon — now this moon disturbs the seasons and comes in from year to year ten days too soon. For this reason the years will come upon them when they will disturb the order, and make an abominable day the day of testimony, and an unclean day a feast day.

☥ Jubilees · Chapters XII · XXIII Abraham Breaks the Idols · The Promise of Eternal Life

12:2And Abram said to Terah his father, Father, what help or advantage have we from those idols which thou dost worship, and before which thou dost bow thyself? For there is no spirit in them, for they are dumb forms, and a misleading of the heart. Worship them not.

12:3Worship Jah the Jah of heaven, who causes the rain and the dew to descend on the earth and does everything upon the earth, and has created everything by His word, and all life is from before His face.

12:6And he arose in the night and burned the house of the idols, and he burned all that was in the house and no man knew it.

12:16And in the sixtieth year of the life of Abram, that is the fourth week, in the fourth year thereof, Abram arose by night, and looked at the stars from the evening to the morning, to see what would be the character of the year with regard to the rains, and he was alone as he sat and observed.

23:29And at that time the Lord will heal His servants, and they shall rise up and see great peace, and drive out their adversaries. And the righteous shall see and be thankful, and rejoice with joy for ever and ever, and shall see all their judgments and all their curses on their enemies.

23:30And their bones shall rest in the earth, and their spirits shall have much joy, and they shall know that it is Jah who executes judgment, and shows mercy to hundreds and thousands and to all that love Him.

23:31And do thou, Moses, write down these words; for thus are they written, and they record them on the heavenly tablets for a testimony for the generations for ever.

✦ Jubilees · Chapters VII — XI Noah's Covenant · The Angels of the Presence · The Seven Laws

7:20And in the twenty-eighth jubilee Noah began to enjoin upon his sons' sons the ordinances and commandments, and all the judgments that he knew, and he exhorted his sons to observe righteousness, and to cover the shame of their flesh, and to bless their Creator, and honour father and mother, and love their neighbour, and guard their souls from fornication and uncleanness and all iniquity.

7:26And thus he commanded them, saying: Behold I shall die this day. And do ye, my sons, not forsake righteousness. For ye see I, your father, am going the way of all the earth, and that righteousness alone dwelleth with Jah forever. And now be ye righteous, and love one another, my sons, as a man loves his own soul, and let each seek in what his brother lacks and supply it, and let them not introduce evil into their souls.

8:10And it came to pass in the beginning of the thirty-third jubilee that they divided the earth into three parts, for Shem and Ham and Japheth, according to the inheritance of each, in the first year in the first week, when one of us who had been sent, was with them. And he called his sons, and they drew nigh to him, they and their children, and he divided the earth into the lots which his three sons were to take in possession.

10:1And in the third week of this jubilee the unclean demons began to lead astray the children of the sons of Noah, and to make to err and destroy them. And the sons of Noah came to Noah their father, and they told him concerning the demons which were leading astray and blinding and slaying his sons' sons. And he prayed before Jah his God, and said: God of the spirits of all flesh, who hast shewn mercy unto me, and hast saved me and my sons from the waters of the flood.

10:7And Jah our God bade us to bind all. And the chief of the spirits, Mastema, came and said: Lord, Creator, let some of them remain before me, and let them hearken to my voice, and do all that I shall say unto them; for if some of them are not left to me, I shall not be able to execute the power of my will on the sons of men; for these are for corruption and leading astray before my judgment, for great is the wickedness of the sons of men.

6:17And He gave to Noah and his sons a sign that there should not again be a flood on the earth. He set His bow in the cloud for a sign of the eternal covenant that there should not again be a flood on the earth to destroy it all the days of the earth. For this reason it is ordained and written on the heavenly tablets, that they should celebrate the feast of weeks in this month once a year, to renew the covenant every year.

6:23This whole festival was celebrated in heaven from the day of creation till the days of Noah — twenty-six jubilees and five weeks of years: and Noah and his sons observed it for seven jubilees and one week of years, till the day of Noah's death, and from the day of Noah's death his sons did away with it until the days of Abraham, and they ate blood. But Abraham observed it, and Isaac and Jacob and his children observed it up to thy days, and in thy days the children of Israel forgot it until ye celebrated it anew on this mountain.

15:1And in the fifth year of the fourth week of this jubilee, in the third month, in the middle of the month, Abram celebrated the feast of the first-fruits of the grain harvest. And he offered burnt offerings to Jah, one young bullock and one ram and seven sheep, all perfect, and a meat offering and their drink offerings, and frankincense.

16:20And Abraham kept all the feasts of Jah as it is written in the heavenly tablets, from the feast of first-fruits of harvest to the feast of first-fruits of oil, and to the feast of wood-offering, and to the feast of tabernacles in the seventh month.

49:1Remember the commandment which Jah commanded thee concerning the passover, that thou shouldst celebrate it in its season on the fourteenth of the first month, that thou shouldst kill it before it is evening, and that they should eat it by night on the evening of the fifteenth from the time of the setting of the sun. For on this night — the beginning of the festival and the beginning of the joy — ye were eating the passover in Egypt, when all the powers of Mastema had been let loose to slay all the first-born in the land of Egypt.

✦ Jubilees · Chapters XXX — XXXVI The Heavenly Tablets · Written Before Jah · The Eternal Record

30:19And do thou, Moses, command the children of Israel and exhort them not to give their daughters to the Gentiles, and not to take for their sons any of the daughters of the Gentiles, for this is abominable before Jah. For this reason I have written for thee in the words of the Law all the deeds of the Shechemites, which they wrought against Dinah, and how the sons of Jacob spake, saying: We will not give our daughter to a man who is uncircumcised; for that were a reproach unto us.

31:14And the angel of the presence who went before the camp of Israel took the tables of the divisions of the years — from the time of the creation — of the law and of the testimony of the weeks of the jubilees, according to the individual years, according to all the number of the jubilees, according to the individual years, from the day of the new creation when the heavens and the earth shall be renewed and all their creation according to the powers of the heaven, and according to all the creation of the earth, until the sanctuary of Jah shall be made in Jerusalem on the Mount Zion.

36:6And Jacob said: I have not a lesser portion or inheritance than you, for I have received it all — and in addition a blessing. And Isaac said to Jacob his son: May Jah give thee seed of righteousness; and some of thy sons may He sanctify in the midst of the whole earth; may nations serve thee, and all the nations bow themselves before thy seed.
