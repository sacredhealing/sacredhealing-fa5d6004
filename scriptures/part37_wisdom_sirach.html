Wisdom of Solomon · Sirach

The Righteous Are in the Hand of Jah · Honour Thy Father · Fear Jah

The Wisdom of Solomon and the Book of Sirach (Ecclesiasticus) are among the most profound wisdom texts of the ancient world. Written in Greek by Jewish sages in Alexandria and Jerusalem approximately 200–100 BCE, they were considered sacred scripture by the Ethiopian Orthodox Church, the Catholic Church, and the Eastern Orthodox Church. The Wisdom of Solomon opens with one of the most beautiful declarations of the immortality of the soul in all of scripture. Sirach — the longest wisdom book — covers every dimension of human life: family, work, friendship, prayer, the fear of Jah, the praise of the ancestors.

✦ Wisdom of Solomon · Chapters 1 — 3 The Righteous Are in the Hand of Jah · The Soul Is Immortal

1:1Love righteousness, you rulers of the earth, think of the Lord in goodness and seek him with sincerity of heart; because he is found by those who do not put him to the test, and manifests himself to those who do not distrust him.

1:7Because the spirit of the Lord has filled the world, and that which holds all things togetherr knows what is said; therefore no one who utters unrighteous things will escape notice, and justice, when it punishes, will not pass him by.

2:23For Jah created us for incorruption, and made us in the image of his own eternity; but through the devil's envy death entered the world, and those who belong to his company experience it.

3:1But the souls of the righteous are in the hand of Jah, and no torment will ever touch them.

3:2In the eyes of the foolish they seemed to have died, and their departure was thought to be a disaster, and their going from us to be their destruction; but they are at peace.

3:4For though in the sight of others they were punished, their hope is full of immortality.

3:5Having been disciplined a little, they will receive great good, because Jah tested them and found them worthy of himself.

3:7In the time of their visitation they will shine forth, and will run like sparks through the stubble.

3:9Those who trust in him will understand truth, and the faithful will abide with him in love, because grace and mercy are upon his holy ones, and he watches over his elect.

6:13She hastens to make herself known to those who desire her.

6:16Because she goes about seeking those worthy of her, and she graciously appears to them in their paths, and meets them in every thought.

7:7Therefore 1 prayed, and understanding was given me; 1 called on Jah, and the spirit of wisdom came to me.

7:22For wisdom, the fashioner of all things, taught me. 7:26For she is a reflection of eternal light, a spotless mirror of the working of Jah, and an image of his goodness.

7:27Although she is but one, she can do all things, and while remaining in herself, she renews all things; in every generation she passes into holy souls and makes them friends of Jah, and prophets.

9:1O Jah of my ancestors and Lord of mercy, who have made all things by your word, and by your wisdom have formed humankind to have dominion over the creatures you have made — give me the wisdom that sits by your throne, and do not reject me from among your servants.

9:10Send her forth from the holy heavens, and from the throne of your glory send her, that she may labour at my side, and that 1 may learn what is pleasing to you.

✦ Wisdom of Solomon · Chapters 10 — 19 Wisdom Guided the Righteous · From Adam to Moses

10:1Wisdom protected the first-formed father of the world, when he alone had been created; she delivered him from his transgression, and gave him strength to rule all things.

10:5Wisdom also, when the nations in wicked agreement had been put to confusion, recognised the righteous man and preserved him blameless before Jah, and kept him strong in the face of his compassion for his child.

10:10When a righteous man fled from his brother's wrath, she guided him on straight paths; she showed him the kingdom of Jah, and gave him knowledge of holy things; she prospered him in his labours, and increased the fruit of his toil.

10:15A holy people and blameless race wisdom delivered from a nation of oppressors. She entered the soul of a servant of the Lord, and withstood dread kings with wonders and signs.

11:26But you spare all things, for they are yours, O Lord, you who love the living. For your immortal spirit is in all things.

✦ Sirach · Chapters 1 — 3 The Fear of Jah Is Wisdom · Honour Thy Father and Mother

1:1All wisdom is from the Lord, and with him it remains forever.

1:10He lavished her upon all his works, upon all the living according to his gift; he lavished her upon those who love him.

1:14The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom; she is created with the faithful in the womb.

1:20The root of wisdom is to fear the Lord; her branches are long life.

2:1My child, when you come to serve the Lord, prepare yourself for testing. Set your heart right and be steadfast, and do not be impetuous in time of calamity.

2:4Accept whatever befalls you, and in times of humiliation be patient. For gold is tested in the fire, and those found acceptable in the furnace of humiliation.

2:11For the Lord is compassionate and merciful; he forgives sins and saves in time of distress.

3:2For the Lord honours a father above his children, and he confirms a mother's right over her children.

3:3Those who honour their father atone for sins, and those who respect their mother are like those who lay up treasure.

3:6Whoever honours his father will be gladdened by his own children, and when he prays he will be heard.
