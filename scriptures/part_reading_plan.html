The 90-Day Reading Plan

Three Months Through the Complete Covenant · A Daily Practice · From Genesis to Zion

This plan carries you through the complete Ethiopian Bible in ninety days — one reading per day, approximately twenty to forty minutes each. It is not designed to be rushed. Each day's reading is a transmission. Sit with it before moving on. Let the Prana of the text settle into the body before the mind begins to analyse. The goal is not information. The goal is transformation.

✦ Before You Begin · The Daily Practice

Before each daily reading: sit in stillness for three breaths. Inhale through the nose — hold for a moment — exhale fully. Do this three times. Then open the text. After each reading: do not immediately reach for your phone or another task. Sit for two minutes in silence. Let what you read settle into your heart and spirit before your mind begins to analyse it. Keep a notebook beside you. Write one sentence — not a summary, not analysis — one sentence that arose from within you during or after the reading. This is your personal covenant record. Ninety days of sentences will show you where the text has been working in you.
