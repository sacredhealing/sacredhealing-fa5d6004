3 Enoch — The Hebrew Book of Enoch

Sepher Hekhalot · Rabbi Ishmael · Metatron · The Prince of the Divine Presence

The Third Book of Enoch — known as the Hebrew Enoch or Sepher Hekhalot — is not part of the Ethiopian Orthodox Biblical canon but is preserved here as a Sacred Addition, completing the Enochic tradition. It records the celestial jjourney of Rabbi Ishmael through the seven heavenly palaces and his encounter with Metatron — the transformed Enoch, Prince of the Divine Presence, seated upon a throne beside the Throne of Glory. It is the foundation of Jewish Merkabah mysticism and one of the most extraordinary mystical texts of the ancient world.

✦ 3 Enoch — The Hebrew Book of Enoch Rabbi Ishmael · Metatron · The Prince of the Divine Presence

1:1When 1 ascended on high to behold the vision of the Merkabah and had entered the six halls, one within the other, as soon as 1 reached the door of the seventh hall 1 stood still in prayer before the Holy One, blessed be He, and, lifting up my eyes on high — that is, to the divine Majesty — 1 said:

1:2Lord of the Universe, 1 pray thee that the merit of Aaron son of Amram, the lover of peace and pursuer of peace, who received the crown of priesthood from Thy glory on the mount of Sinai, be vouchsafed unto me, so that Qaspiel, the prince, and the angels with him may not get power over me nor throw me down from the heavens.

3:1At once the Holy One, blessed be He, sent to me Metatron, his Servant, the Angel, the Prince of the Presence, and he spread his wings and came to meet me with great joy, to save me from their hands. And he took me with his hand before the eyes of all the host of high angels and said to me: Come in peace into the presence of the High and Exalted King and behold the picture of the Merkabah.

4:11 asked Metatron, the Angel, the Prince of the Presence: What is thy name? He answered: 1 have seventy names, corresponding to the seventy nations of the world, and my King's name is over and above all names. However, my colleagues, the angels and the members of my household, call me Metatron, the Angel, the Prince of the Presence, because my name is like the name of my King.

6:1Metatron, the Angel, the Prince of the Presence, said to me: Before he appointed me to serve the Throne of Glory, the Holy One, blessed be He, opened to me three hundred thousand gates of understanding, three hundred thousand gates of subtlety, three hundred thousand gates of life, and three hundred thousand gates of grace and lovingkindness, and three hundred thousand gates of love, and three hundred thousand gates of Torah, and three hundred thousand gates of meekness, and three hundred thousand gates of sustenance, and three hundred thousand gates of mercy.

12:1After all these things the Holy One, blessed be He, put his hand upon me and blessed me with 1,365,000 blessings, and 1 was raised and enlarged to the size of the length and width of the world. And he caused 72 wings to grow on me — 36 on one side and 36 on the other — and each single wing covered the entire world. And he fixed in me 365 eyes, corresponding to the days of the solar year, and each eye was as the great light.

48:1After all this, Metatron showed me all the treasuries of the upper world that are connected with the Throne of Glory. He showed me the treasury of peace; the treasury of blessing; the treasury of life; the treasury of grace and lovingkindness; the treasury of Torah; the treasury of understanding; the treasury of wisdom; the treasury of all the mysteries; the treasury of all that is hidden; and the treasury of faith. And 1 beheld with mine own eyes all these treasuries and they are all before the Throne of Glory.

17:1Metatron said to Rabbi Ishmael: Come and 1 will show thee the Curtain of the Omnipresent One, which is spread before the Holy One, blessed be He, and on which are printed all the generations of the world and all their deeds, whether done or yet to be done, till the last generation. And 1 went with him and he showed me with his fingers, like a father who teaches his son the letters of the Torah. And 1 saw each generation and its kings, each generation and its princes, each generation and its leaders, each generation and its shepherds.

18:1After this Metatron brought me to see the store-chambers of snow and the store-chambers of hail, and the store-chambers of darkness, and the store-chambers of lightnings and of thunderings, and the store-chambers of storms, and the wheel of the chariot. And 1 saw the angels of fire and the angels of ice standing before the Holy One, blessed be He, and not harming one another; and the Holy One, blessed be He, made peace between them and between all his creatures.

22:1After this Metatron, the Prince of the Presence, showed me the Right Hand of the Omnipresent One, behind the Holy One, blessed be He, a hand of enormous size — the whole world in its entirety from one end to the other being but the span of its little finger. And five rivers of fire issue from each finger, and between each finger and the next is the distance of the heavens from the earth, and from the east to the west, and every angel that passes before it falls on its face.

26:1And after this Metatron showed me some of the troops of the ministering angels who had been assigned to him from the beginning, as it is written: All the peoples of the nations are as nothing beside him, they are counted to him less than nothing and vanity. The first troop — their stature is as the height of the seven heavens. Their appearance is as the sun, and their eyes are like torches of fire. And when they open their mouths to sing before the Holy One, their voice is like the voice of the Almighty.

33:1After this Metatron showed me all the princes of kingdoms standing before the Holy One, blessed be He, in the four quarters of Araboth Raqia. And the Prince of the Face of Jah, whose name is Adoriron, stands before the Holy One blessed be He, surrounded by the crowns of his ministers, and the appearance of his face is like the face of ministering angels.

40:1Metatron said to Rabbi Ishmael: Come and 1 will show thee where the souls of the righteous are, and where are the souls of those of middle character, and where the souls of the wicked are, and where are the souls of the penitents. And he showed me the souls of the righteous, who are treasured up under the Throne of Glory, and they shine as the brightness of the firmament. And then he showed me the souls of those of middle character, who fly about in the winds beneath the second firmament.

41:1After this Metatron showed me the spirits of the fathers of the world, the spirits of the patriarchs — Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, and the rest of the righteous — standing before the Shekinah and rejoicing because of the children of Israel who are observing the Torah in the world below. And whenever they observe the Torah and good deeds, the souls of the fathers of the world rejoice and exult, and the Shekinah sends light upon them from on high.

45:1After this, the Holy One, blessed be He, opened to me the gates of Shekhinah, the gates of peace, the gates of wisdom, the gates of strength, the gates of power, the gates of speech, the gates of song, the gates of holiness, the gates of chant; and 1 magnified and praised the Holy One, blessed be He, with the song of heaven, with the singing of the Seraphim. And at the hour when 1 opened my mouth and sang the praises before the Throne of Glory, the holy creatures below the Throne and above the Throne and all the orders of the firmament trembled before me.

48:7Metatron, Prince of the Presence, the glory of highest heaven, said: This is the mystery of mysteries. The Holy One, blessed be He, has revealed it to Metatron, and he revealed it to Rabbi Ishmael. Know that in the time when the Holy One, blessed be He, will renew his world and create a new heaven and a new earth, all the secrets of the Torah shall be revealed, and the secrets of the Throne of Glory and the Merkabah shall be made manifest, and the righteous shall be crowned with the crowns of the world to come, and shall sit on thrones of fire and shall shine as the sun and the moon and the stars, world without end.
