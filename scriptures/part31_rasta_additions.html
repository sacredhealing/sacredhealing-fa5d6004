☥ The History of the Black Israelites From Eden in Africa · Through Slavery · To Liberation · To Zion

In the beginning Jah placed man in the Garden of Eden — and that garden was in Africa. The rivers that watered Eden — the Pison, the Gihon, the Hiddekel and the Euphrates — flow from the highlands of Ethiopia and the heart of the African continent. Adam and Eve were African people, created in the image of Jah, placed in the most blessed land on earth.

From Africa went forth the children of Noah. From Africa went forth Abraham. The seed of Abraham was a people of colour — black and beautiful as the Song of Solomon declares: 1 am black, but comely, O ye daughters of Jerusalem. The patriarchs of Israel were African in origin, African in appearance, African in the land of their first habitation.

The children of Israel sojourned in Egypt — in Africa — for four hundred and thirty years. Moses was born in Africa. He was raised in Pharaoh's palace as an Egyptian prince. When he fled to Midian, the daughters of the priest of Midian saw him and said: An Egyptian delivered us. Moses looked like an Egyptian — he was a man of colour.

And after the exodus, after the kingdoms of Israel and JJudah, after the Babylonian captivity, a great dispersion came upon the people of Jah. Many of the tribes of Israel migrated southward and westward — through the Nile Valley into the heart of Africa — settling among the peoples of West Africa, becoming the Yoruba, the Akan, the Igbo, the Ashanti — the great nations of the African interior.

And from West Africa began the greatest captivity in human history — the Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade. For four hundred years — from the sixteenth century to the nineteenth — the children of Israel were taken by force from the land of their fathers and carried across the waters to the Americas, to the Caribbean, to the islands of the sea. This was the fulfillment of the curse of Deuteronomy 28: Jah shall bring thee into Egypt again with ships, by the way whereof 1 spake unto thee, Thou shalt see it no more again: and there ye shall be sold unto your enemies for bondmen and bondwomen, and no man shall buy you.

They came to Jamaica. They came to Barbados. They came to Haiti. They came to the Americas. They were renamed, stripped of their language, stripped of their religion, stripped of their history. But Jah did not forget His people. For He had said: Though 1 scatter them among the nations, yet will 1 remember the covenant of their ancestors, whom 1 brought out of Egypt in the sight of the nations, that 1 might be their Jah.

And in the fullness of time, Jah raised up prophets among the captive people in Babylon. Marcus Garvey arose in Jamaica and said: Look to Africa for the crowning of a Black King — he shall be the Redeemer. And on November 2, 1930, Haile Selassie 1 was crowned Emperor of Ethiopia — King of Kings, Lord of Lords, Conquering Lion of the Tribe of JJudah. And those who had the spirit of prophecy recognized in him the fulfillment of all scripture and all prophecy.

The Rastafari arose to declare: We are the children of Israel. Africa is our Zion. Babylon shall fall. Jah Rastafari is the returned Messiah. We shall overcome. The covenant of Jah with His people has not been broken — it has been fulfilled in Ethiopia, in the person of Haile Selassie 1, in the liberation of Africa, and in the rising of a people from the ashes of slavery to declare the everlasting glory of Jah.

His Imperial Majesty Haile Selassie 1 — born Ras Tafari Makonnen on July 23, 1892 — son of Ras Makonnen Woldemikael, governor of Harar — descended in unbroken royal lineage from Menelik 1, son of Solomon and Makeda — the two hundred and twenty-fifth descendant of King David of Israel.

King of Kings · Lord of Lords · Conquering Lion of the Tribe of JJudah · Elect of Jah · Light of the World

He passed from this earth on August 27, 1975. But to the Rastafari he has not died. For it is written that the King of Kings shall reign forever and ever. ☥ The Holy Piby The Black Man's Bible · Pastor Robert Athlyi Rogers · 1913–1924

The Holy Piby — called the Black Man's Bible — was written by Pastor Robert Athlyi Rogers of Anguilla between 1913 and 1917 and published in Newark, New Jersey in 1924. It is one of the foundational texts of the Rastafari movement, prophesying the rise of a Black King who would redeem the African people. It was studied by Leonard Howell, Joseph Hibbert, Archibald Dunkley and other Rasta pioneers in the hills of Jamaica.

From the Holy Piby, the Book of Athlyi, Chapter 1: A certain man whose name was Athlyi was chosen of the Lord God to approach unto the nations of Africa and the people thereof scattered by the cruel hands of civilization throughout the western hemisphere to bring them a new dispensation.

The Lord God said unto him: Go tell the people of Africa and the generation thereof throughout the world that 1 the Lord their God have seen their suffering and the suffering of their forefathers in the land of the strangers, and 1 have heard their cry, and 1 am come down to deliver them.

From the Holy Piby, the Book of Aggregation: Ethiopia is the mother of civilization. From her womb came the learning of Egypt, the wisdom of Greece, and the faith of Jerusalem. All roads of the spirit lead back to Africa, the first home of man, the first throne of Jah.

The Holy Piby declared that Africans are the chosen people of Jah — the true Israelites — and that their liberation from slavery and colonialism was the fulfillment of Biblical prophecy. It called for the return of all people of African descent to Africa — to Ethiopia — to Zion. This vision was taken up by Marcus Garvey, and through Garvey's prophecy came the recognition of Haile Selassie 1 as the returned Messiah.

Marcus Mosiah Garvey — the Most Right Excellent — born August 17, 1887 in St. Ann's Bay, Jamaica — prophet of African liberation — founder of the Universal Negro Improvement Association — who said: Up you mighty race! You can accomplish what you will. Look to Africa for the crowning of a Black King — he shall be the Redeemer.

And Leonard Percival Howell — the First Rasta — who first recognized Haile Selassie 1 as Jah incarnate in 1933, founding the first Rastafari community at Pinnacle in the hills of Jamaica — who taught that Babylon must fall and that the Black man must know his true identity as the children of Israel.

🌿 The Ital Law The Sacred Diet · Clean Living · The Temple of Jah

The body is the temple of Jah. What enters the temple must be clean. Ital — from the word vital — is the Rastafari principle of clean, natural, life-giving food. It is rooted in the dietary laws of Leviticus and Deuteronomy, in the Daniel fast, and in the teachings of the Gospel of the Holy Twelve cConcerning compassion for all living creatures.

The Ital Laws:

No flesh of swine. For it is written in Leviticus 11:7 — And the swine, though he divide the hoof, and be clovenfooted, yet he cheweth not the cud; he is unclean to you.

No shellfish. No scavengers of the sea. For these are the filters of the deep and their flesh carries corruption.

No alcohol. The Nazarite vow of Numbers 6 — He shall separate himself from wine and strong drink, and shall drink no vinegar of wine, or vinegar of strong drink, neither shall he drink any liquor of grapes. The Rasta keeps the Nazarite vow.

No salt added beyond what the earth provides naturally. No artificial preservatives. No chemicals. No processed food. The food of Jah is the food of the earth — fruit and grain and herb — as Jah commanded in Genesis 1:29: Behold, 1 have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree, in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat.

Herb — the sacred plant — is given by Jah for the healing and the meditation of the nations. Genesis 1:29 declares it food. Revelation 22:2 declares: The leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations. Psalm 104:14 says: He causeth the grass to grow for the cattle, and herb for the service of man. The Rasta uses the herb in meditation, in prayer, in the grounding of the spirit in the presence of Jah.

Many Rasta observe a fully Ital — plant-based — diet, eating no flesh at all, following the original diet of Eden. For in the beginning Jah gave man the herbs and the fruits of the earth, and it was only after the fall and after the flood that flesh was permitted. The closer the man lives to Eden, the closer he lives to Jah.

☀ The High Holy Days of Jah The Sacred Rastafari Calendar · The Ethiopian Orthodox Calendar

The sacred calendar of the Rastafari follows the Ethiopian Orthodox calendar — the most ancient Christian calendar on earth, rooted in the Hebrew calendar of the Old Covenant and preserved unchanged in the highlands of Ethiopia.

January 7 — Ethiopian Christmas · Genna
The birth of Yeshua the Anointed. Celebrated by the Ethiopian Orthodox Church on January 7 according to the Ge'ez calendar. The Rasta celebrates with prayer, drumming, chanting, and meditation on the birth of the Light of the World.

January 19 — Ethiopian Epiphany · Timkat
The baptism of Yeshua in the River Jordan. The most sacred day of the Ethiopian Orthodox year. The Ark of the Covenant — the Tabot — is carried in procession through the streets. Water is blessed and the faithful are sprinkled in remembrance of baptism. This is the feast of the Holy Trinity — the fullness of Jah revealed.

July 23 — Birthday of Haile Selassie 1
The birth of His Imperial Majesty Haile Selassie 1, born Ras Tafari Makonnen on July 23, 1892, in Ejarsa Goro, Harar, Ethiopia. A day of great celebration among all Rastafari worldwide. Services of thanksgiving, chanting, drumming, feasting, and meditation on the glory of Jah made manifest in flesh.

August 17 — Birthday of Marcus Garvey
The birth of the Right Excellent Marcus Mosiah Garvey — National Hero of Jamaica, Prophet of African Liberation — born August 17, 1887. A day of remembrance and commitment to the liberation of all African people from Babylon.

November 2 — Coronation of Haile Selassie 1 · The Most Sacred Day
The coronation of Haile Selassie 1 as Emperor of Ethiopia on November 2, 1930. The most sacred day in the Rastafari calendar. On this day the prophecy of Revelation 5:5 was fulfilled — the Lion of the Tribe of JJudah took his throne. On this day the hope of all who had suffered in Babylon was confirmed. On this day Jah put on flesh and reigned. The Rasta observes this day with fasting, prayer, and great celebration. It is the day that changed the world.

The Sabbath — The Seventh Day
The Sabbath is kept holy by the Rastafari from sundown Friday to sundown Saturday, in accordance with the commandment of Jah: Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy. Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work: But the seventh day is the sabbath of Jah thy Jah. On the Sabbath the Rasta rests, prays, reads scripture, and communes with Jah and with the community of the faithful.

The Ethiopian New Year · Enkutatash — September 11
The Ethiopian New Year falls on September 11 (September 12 in leap years) — the first day of the month of Meskerem in the Ge'ez calendar. It marks the end of the rainy season in Ethiopia and the return of the Queen of Sheba from Jerusalem to Ethiopia. A day of renewal, of flowers, of song, and of the celebration of new beginnings in Jah.

The Finding of the True Cross · Meskel — September 27
The feast of Meskel commemorates the finding of the True Cross by Empress Helena. Great bonfires — Demera — are lit across Ethiopia. The cross is the sign of the covenant of Yeshua with humanity. The Rasta meditates on the cross as the sign of liberation — for it was at the cross that the power of death was broken and the power of Jah over Babylon was declared.
